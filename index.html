What is a Yu-Gi-Oh! Archetype?

Learn about archetypes and how to use this site

In Yu-Gi-Oh!, an archetype is a group of cards that share a common naming convention and are designed to work together through their effects. These cards reference each other by name, creating powerful synergies that form the foundation of competitive deck building. Whether you're a returning player rediscovering the game or a newcomer looking to build your first deck, understanding archetypes is essential to mastering Yu-Gi-Oh! strategy.

Archetype Nexus is your comprehensive resource for exploring every archetype in the game. Each analysis page provides an introduction to the archetype's playstyle, core cards, basic combos, and strategic synergies. We cover everything from classic fan-favorites like Blue-Eyes and Dark Magician to modern competitive powerhouses such as Snake-Eyes, Tenpai Dragon, and Branded.

What sets Archetype Nexus apart is our unique "Newest Support" sorting feature. Yu-Gi-Oh! constantly releases new cards that breathe fresh life into older archetypes. By sorting by Support (Newest), you can instantly discover which archetypes have received recent support cards—helping you identify decks that are newly viable or have gained competitive upgrades.
