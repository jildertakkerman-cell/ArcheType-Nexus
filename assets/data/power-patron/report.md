# Mastering Chaos: A Beginner's Guide to the Power Patron Saga

## 1. Introduction to the Chaos Origins Universe
Welcome to the curriculum for the "Power Patron" archetype, a mechanically complex series of cards introduced in the *Chaos Origins* expansion. As a Master Judge, I have designed this guide to help you navigate the intricate interactions between four distinct sub-archetypes: **Artmage**, **DoomZ**, **Elfnote**, and the central **Shade** monsters. These groups are unified under the "Power Patron" banner, creating a deck that demands high technical proficiency and resource management.

The central objective of the strategy is to utilize the "Shade" Pendulum monsters to Special Summon high-level Extra Deck bosses (treated as their respective Summon types) from the Extra Deck. This bypasses traditional summoning requirements by exchanging deck resources for immediate board presence. Mechanically, this reflects the lore of **Medius** and the **Artmages**, where the struggle for stability against corruption leads to the transformation of individual spirits into unified, albeit unstable, entities.

## 2. The "Shade" Engine: From Pendulum to Boss
The engine is driven by three Level 10 DARK Pendulum monsters: *Jupredo*, *Nervedo*, and *Junoldo*. While on the field, these monsters can destroy themselves to Special Summon a specific "Power Patron" boss from your Extra Deck. 

### Comparison of Shade Monsters
The following table summarizes the primary gateways to your Extra Deck:

| Name | Archetype Treated As | Summoned Boss Monster | Summon Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Jupredo the Shademachine* | DoomZ | *Jupiter the Power Patron of Destruction* | Xyz Summon |
| *Nervedo the Shadebeast* | Artmage | *Nerva the Power Patron of Creation* | Fusion Summon |
| *Junoldo the Shadespirit* | Elfnote | *Junora the Power Patron of Tuning* | Synchro Summon |

### The "Resource Exchange": 3-Card Banish Cost
To activate these effects, you must **banish the top 3 cards of your deck face-down**. In technical terms, this is a mandatory cost to trigger the summoning effect. A Master Judge views this as a "Resource Exchange"—you are sacrificing deck longevity for immediate tactical superiority.

**Judge's Note on "Viraia":** If you have the Quick-Play Spell *Viraia, Desolate Realm of the Power Patrons* in your Graveyard (GY), you can banish it instead of the top 3 cards of your deck. To avoid common tournament disputes: activating *Viraia* in the GY satisfies the **entire** 3-card banishing cost for a Shade monster.

The stability of these summons is dependent on the environment established by your Field Spell.

## 3. Decoding the Field Spell: Plundered Power Patron Plane - Vidolia
*Vidolia* serves as the foundational infrastructure of the deck. Its effects are categorized as follows:

1.  **Search/Summon Power:** You can reveal 1 "Power Patron" monster in your Extra Deck and banish 1 card from your hand face-down to add to your hand or Special Summon 1 monster from your Deck that mentions the revealed monster's name.
2.  **Stat Reduction:** This is a Continuous Effect. All monsters your opponent controls lose 100 ATK for every banished card in the game (both players' banished piles). 
3.  **Graveyard Lock:** This creates a restrictive game state. While you control *Nerva*, *Jupiter*, and *Junora* simultaneously, your opponent cannot activate card effects in the GY.

### Understanding the "Once-Per-Turn Restriction Clause"
A specific line in *Vidolia* often confuses new players: it searches monsters mentioning a name *"except in its once-per-turn restriction clause."*

This is a balancing mechanic designed to prevent infinite self-searching loops. Several key monsters, such as *Jupredo the Shademachine* and *Vidolium the Unstable Power Patron*, only mention their own names within their **Hard Once Per Turn (HOPT)** restriction text. Because *Vidolia* is instructed to ignore the HOPT text when checking for mentions, it effectively "cannot see" these names. This **excludes** these cards from the search pool when you reveal a copy of the same card, preventing you from using one *Jupredo* to search another *Jupredo*.

## 4. Navigating Link and Synchro Summoning Confusion
Advanced Power Patron play involves utilizing Link and Synchro monsters that deviate from standard summoning procedures.

### Link Summoning: Purification Power Patron
*Purification Power Patron* represents the lore-significant moment when **Finmel** transforms to save **Medius**. 
*   **Material Requirement:** This Link-2 monster requires **2 non-Link monsters**. You cannot use other Link monsters as material.
*   **Search Benefit:** If Link Summoned while a "Power Patron" is in your GY, you can add 1 "Theorealize" card from your Deck to your hand.
*   **Quick-Link:** During the Main Phase, if you control another DARK Fairy, you can activate her Quick Effect to Link Summon immediately using materials you control, including herself.

### Synchro Summoning: Center Zone Strategy
While *Junora* is typically summoned via *Junoldo*, traditional Synchro plays often involve *June Bride Elfnote*.
*   **The Center Zone:** While *June Bride Elfnote* is in the center Main Monster Zone, only monsters in the center Main Monster Zones can declare an attack. 
*   **Mass Summon:** During the Main Phase, if she is in that center zone, she can return to the Extra Deck as a Quick Effect to Special Summon three "Elfnote" monsters—one each from your hand, Deck, and GY.

> ### 💡 Pro-Tips for the Extra Deck
> *   **Link Tip:** Ensure you have a DARK Fairy on the board before attempting the Quick-Link effect of *Purification*. Without it, the effect cannot be activated.
> *   **Synchro Tip:** *Junora the Power Patron of Tuning* is treated as a Synchro Summon when called by *Junoldo*. This means if it is destroyed, it can be Special Summoned from the GY later because it was "properly summoned" first.

## 5. The Ultimate Power: Vidolium and the End Times
The peak of the Power Patron curriculum is the mastery of *Vidolium the Unstable Power Patron of Unity*.

### The Boss Checklist
*Vidolium* cannot be Normal Summoned/Set. You must Special Summon it from the Extra Deck or GY by returning these three types from your field or GY to the Extra Deck:
*   [ ] **1 Fusion Monster**
*   [ ] **1 Synchro Monster**
*   [ ] **1 Xyz Monster**

### Mechanical Superiority
*   **The Global Wipe:** Upon Special Summon, *Vidolium* banishes all cards in both Graveyards face-down. This is a devastating reset that clears the opponent's resources.
*   **Stat Ceiling:** It possesses a base **5000 ATK**, currently the highest original ATK value in the game.
*   **Immunities:** It is **unaffected by other monster effects**, except for those of other "Power Patron" monsters. Note: It remains vulnerable to Spell/Trap cards and higher-priority "Power Patron" effects.

This arrival is often heralded by the Trap card *Power Patron Shadows of the End Times*, which can Special Summon any Patron from the GY or Extra Deck and banish opponent resources based on the variety of monster types (Fusion/Synchro/Xyz) you control.

## 6. Tactical Summary: The Power Patron Cheat Sheet
To ensure a clean game state during your first tournament, keep these three rulings in mind:

> 1. **Spell Speed 2 Pendulums:** *Nervedo’s* Pendulum effect is a **Spell Speed 2 (Quick-like)** effect. You can activate it in response to an opponent's monster effect to protect your "Power Patron" or "Artmage" plays.
> 2. **Deck Verification:** You must have at least 3 cards remaining in your deck to activate a Shade monster's cost. You cannot activate the effect if the cost cannot be fully paid.
> 3. **Material Legality:** Always verify monster types for *June Bride Elfnote* and the "non-Link" restriction on *Purification* before declaring a summon.

### Common Interaction Guide
*   **The Viraia Shortcut:** Banish *Viraia, Desolate Realm of the Power Patrons* from your GY to satisfy the **entire** 3-card banish cost of any Shade monster.
*   **The Medius Starter:** Use the Normal Spell *Theorealize Past Lull* while you control an Artmage, DoomZ, or Elfnote card to Special Summon *Medius the Pure* directly from the deck. This is your primary combo starter.

Mastering the Power Patron saga requires patience and a strict adherence to Problem-Solving Card Text (PSCT). By understanding the "Resource Exchange" and the specific zone requirements of your bosses, you will successfully navigate the chaos of the origins. Class dismissed, and good luck at your locals.
