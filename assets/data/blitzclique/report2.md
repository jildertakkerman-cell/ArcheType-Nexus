# Mastering the Blitzclique: A Scavenger's Guide to the Floating City

## 1. Welcome to the Sky: The Blitzclique Identity
Welcome to the curriculum, Duelist. Today we are breaking down one of the most resilient resource-loop archetypes in the *Chaos Origins* expansion: the **Blitzclique**. In the lore of the floating city, this family of Thunder scavengers survives by eking out an existence from the mechanical wreckage of the world below. 

As a Scavenger, you must adopt a specific tactical philosophy: destruction is not an end state, but a gateway to "loot." While most decks fear losing field presence, the Blitzclique views every destroyed card—whether yours or your opponent’s—as raw material for new resources. This isn't just flavor; it's a mechanical engine designed for high-end grind games.

To master this deck, you must first understand the mechanical "gears" that drive their relentless scavenging cycle.

---

## 2. The Scavenging Cycle: Understanding the Core Mechanic
The Blitzclique engine operates on a three-step Hard Once Per Turn (HOPT) loop. Precision in your timing is mandatory to avoid "fizzling" your effects or missing out on optimal Chain Links.

1.  **The Spark (Ignition/Quick Effect from Hand):** The Level 1–4 monsters feature effects that activate in the hand. You reveal the monster to target a card on the field. Note the Problem-Solving Card Text (PSCT): the destruction is a **requirement** for the Special Summon. If the target leaves the field before resolution, your monster remains in the hand and the summon is lost.
2.  **The Entry (Special Summoning):** Upon successful destruction, the Blitzclique monster (or another Thunder ally) enters the field. This step transitions your hand advantage into immediate field presence.
3.  **The Looting (Trigger Effects):** This is the heart of the engine. Blitzclique monsters already on the field possess **Trigger Effects** that activate on a new chain *after* a card is destroyed. This allows you to search for fresh "tools" (Spells, Traps, or Monsters) based on the specific scavenger currently on the board.

> [!TIP]
> **The Scavenger's Lock:** Most Blitzclique effects include a restriction: you cannot Special Summon **Effect Monsters** for the rest of the turn, except from the hand. Note that this does *not* lock you out of the Extra Deck entirely; you can still summon non-effect monsters, such as the **Mechbeast Tokens**, to maintain your board state.

To scavenge effectively, you need a constant supply of scrap—and the family has brought the right tools for the job.

## 4. The Scavenger's Workshop: Spells, Traps, and the Field
A scavenger is only as good as their workshop. The backrow provides the "scrap" and the means to recycle it.

*   **Hideout in the Sky, Coulomb (Field Spell):** The genius of this card is the **Mechbeast Token**. If your opponent’s field is empty, you *give* them a 2000 ATK target. This ensures you always have "fodder" to destroy with your hand effects, while simultaneously searching a Blitzclique monster.
*   **Blitzclique - Breakaway (Quick-Play Spell):** Highly versatile. It can place a Continuous Trap directly from the deck (bypassing *Ash Blossom*) or bounce your own Thunder monster to destroy an opponent's card.
*   **Blitzclique Alternator (Continuous Trap):** The deck's primary search-and-debuff tool. It shuffles spent Blitzcliques from the field/hand back to the deck to search *any* Thunder monster, including generic powerhouses like *Thunder King Rai-Oh*.
*   **Blitzclique - Steppleader (Continuous Spell):** The safety net. It allows a Special Summon from the hand *without* needing a destruction target, then destroys itself to trigger your scavengers' "Looting" effects.

### The Recycling Feature
Blitzclique support cards are designed for infinite utility. **Coulomb**, **Breakaway**, and **Steppleader** all share a powerful Graveyard mechanic:
*   **GY Recovery:** If a card is destroyed by a "Blitzclique" effect, you can add these cards from your GY back to your hand. This ensures that even if your backrow is dismantled, the scavengers can simply pick up the pieces.

## 5. Chain Reaction: A Step-by-Step "Pro Play" Example
The mark of a pro is the "Turn 0" play, using the opponent's first turn to establish your own board.

1.  **Opponent's Turn 1:** Your opponent activates a monster effect on the field.
2.  **Chain Link 1:** You respond with **Crackle Blitzclique** in hand. Reveal it, target their monster, and destroy it.
3.  **Resolution:** Crackle Special Summons a Thunder monster (e.g., **Surge**) to your field.
4.  **New Chain (Trigger):** Surge’s "Looting" effect triggers because a card was destroyed. You search **Emi Blitzclique**.
5.  **Chain Link 1 (New Chain):** Because Emi was added to your hand, her effect triggers. Reveal her to destroy another card and summon a third monster (e.g., **Whisker**).

> **Coach’s Tip:** Mastering **Whisker Blitzclique** is the key to high-level play. Its negation effect requires you to return a Thunder monster you control to your hand as a cost. Always return **Emi** or **Surge**. This not only negates the opponent's play but "reloads" your hand with an effect you can activate immediately or on the following turn.

## 6. Quick-Start Checklist for New Scavengers
Before entering the competitive queue, verify these technical habits:

- [ ] **Target Validation:** Always confirm your destruction target will remain on the field to ensure your summon doesn't "fizzle" in the hand.
- [ ] **Token Fodder:** If the opponent is playing "blind second" or has an empty board, use **Hideout in the Sky, Coulomb** to give them a Mechbeast Token immediately.
- [ ] **Lock Awareness:** Remember the Special Summon lock only applies to **Effect Monsters** from the hand. Plan your Extra Deck (for non-effect monsters) accordingly.
- [ ] **Breakaway Priority:** Prioritize recycling **Blitzclique - Breakaway** from the GY; it is your most flexible piece of interaction.
- [ ] **Chain Blocking:** Order your Trigger Effects so your most important search (usually Surge or Whisker) is protected by less vital triggers in the chain.

## 7. Summary of Growth
The Blitzclique family proves that one duelist's trash is another's treasure. By turning every instance of destruction into a resource-generating event, this archetype offers a level of resilience rarely seen in *Chaos Origins*. While they live on scraps, their ability to loop effects and recycle their entire backrow makes them a relentless engine of attrition. Tinker with your ratios, master your "Turn 0" timing, and you'll find that the sky is the limit for these scavengers.
