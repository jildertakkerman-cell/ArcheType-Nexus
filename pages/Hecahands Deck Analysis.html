The Paradigm Shift

The release of Phantom Revengers introduces a strategy defined by Resource Appropriation. Known in the OCG as Hecatoncheire (after the hundred-handed giants), the deck leverages the battle immunity of the Illusion type to grind games to a halt.

Rather than simply negating effects, Hecahands actively steal cards from the opponent's Hand, Deck, GY, and Extra Deck to use as Fusion Material. They operate on a principle of "Perfect Information—knowing the opponent's hand via Breus, their deck via Gygas, and their Extra Deck via Xeno.

Anatomy of the Swarm

The Enablers (Level 6)

Hecahands Ibel

Hecahands Ibel

The Pivot: Shuffles itself from hand to Deck to summon any other Hecahands. Dodges Droll/Imperm. GY Effect: Revives a Hecahands monster.

Hecahands Yadel

Hecahands Yadel

The Searcher: Shuffles back to search Spells/Traps. GY Effect: Resets a Spell/Trap from GY (Grind Game).

The Disruptors (Level 7)

Hecahands Breus

Hecahands Breus

Hand Control: Looks at opponent's hand and Special Summons 1 monster from it to YOUR field.

Hecahands Gygas

Hecahands Gygas

Deck Control: Excavates top 3 of opponent's deck; steals a monster. Essential for Fusion material.

Hecahands Goddus

Hecahands Goddus

GY Control: Quick Effect to revive a monster from opponent's GY to your field. Disables recursion.

The Fusion Titans

Hecahands Jawza

Hecahands Jawza

The Bridge: Contact Fuses using opponent's monsters. Searches Spells/Traps on summon. Battle Immunity.

Hecahands Xeno

Hecahands Xeno

Extra Deck Rip: Looks at opponent's Extra Deck and steals a monster. On death, steals ALL monsters opponent controls.

"Hecahands"

Normal Spell

The ROTA. Adds a monster, then Sends card to GY to Set a Trap. GY Effect: Forces opponent to "Draw 1, Discard 1" (Triggers Lvl 7s).

Hecahands Tartarus

Quick-Play

Fusion Spell. Lets you treat stolen monsters as "Hecahands" cards. Bounces S/T on field.

Ipt & Yad

Counter Traps

Ipt (Monster) & Yad (Spell/Trap). They negate activations and steal the card instead of destroying it.

Mandatory Synergies

Hybrid Theory

Pure Hecahands works, but hybrid builds raise the ceiling.

  • Chimera: Nightmare Apprentice bridges the decks. Chimera allows hand-ripping in End Phase.
  • Fiendsmith: Fiendsmith Engraver provides Link climbing capabilities to insulate plays from hand traps.
  • Spirit Sculptor: A tech choice for pure builds. Tributes itself to search Ibel (1600/2100) based on combined stats.

Tactical Operations

Input: Mirror Swordknight + 1 Discard
Output: Hand Rip (x2), Omni-Negate (Theft), GY Steal.

  1. 01. Normal Summon Mirror Swordknight. Tribute -> SS Big-Winged Berfomet. Add Chimera Fusion & Gazelle.
  2. 02. Fuse Berfomet + Gazelle -> Chimera King.
    Chain: Chimera (Rip), Berfomet (Revive Swordknight), Gazelle (Add Nightmare Apprentice).
  3. 03. SS Nightmare Apprentice (Discard 1). Search Hecahands Ibel.
  4. 04. Ibel Effect: Shuffle self -> Summon Hecahands Gygas. Gygas excavates opponent's deck -> Summons monster.
  5. 05. Contact Fuse Gygas + Stolen Monster -> Hecahands Jawza. Search "Hecahands" (Spell).
  6. 06. Activate Spell: Send Apprentice (Cost). Add Yadel. Set Ipt al Hecahands.
  7. 07. SS Yadel (via Ibel GY effect or hand effect if searched). Add Tartarus or Yad.

Input: Fiendsmith Tract + Hecahands Name (or Swordknight start)
Goal: High Ceiling, insulation from Droll, multiple disruption layers.

  1. 01. Standard Start: Swordknight -> Berfomet -> Search Fusion/Gazelle. Fuse for Chimera. Gazelle searches Ibel.
  2. 02. Link Berfomet (or generic body) -> Fiendsmith Requiem.
  3. 03. Requiem Effect: SS Fiendsmith's Lacrima. Lacrima sends Fiendsmith Engraver to GY.
  4. 04. Engraver Effect: SS itself. Overlay/Link into Fiendsmith Sequentia or Caesar Valius.
  5. 05. Hecahands Extension: Activate Ibel -> Summon Gygas -> Steal Monster -> Contact Fuse Jawza.
  6. 06. Jawza searches Hecahands Spell. Activate Spell (Cost: send spent Fiendsmith) -> Set Ipt al Hecahands.

Input: Spirit Sculptor
Goal: 1-Card Starter for Pure Builds.

  1. 01. Normal Summon Spirit Sculptor. Tribute itself (Cost).
  2. 02. Math: Sculptor had 1600 ATK / 2100 DEF (Total 3700). Search monster with same total stats: Hecahands Ibel (1600/2100).
  3. 03. Ibel Effect: Shuffle self -> Summon Gygas.
  4. 04. Gygas excavates -> Steals monster -> Contact Fuse Jawza.
  5. 05. Jawza searches Spell -> Spell searches Yadel -> Yadel resets Trap.

Matchup Analysis

Vs. Snake-Eye / Fire King

Advantage: Hecahands. Goddus is MVP here.

  • Stealing Promethean Princess from GY shuts down recursion.
  • Ipt stealing Snake-Eye Ash gives you a starter.

Vs. Tenpai Dragon

Advantage: Even/Slight Disadvantage.

  • Illusion battle immunity can backfire if Tenpai wants to battle.
  • Must use Xeno to rip Synchros from Extra Deck before Battle Phase.

Vs. Enneacraft (Flip)

Advantage: Disadvantage.

  • Face-down monsters cannot be targeted by Gygas/Jawza theft mechanics.
  • Rely on Tartarus to bounce sets.
