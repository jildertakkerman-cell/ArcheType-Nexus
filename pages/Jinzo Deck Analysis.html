JINZO

A Strategic Deconstruction of the Anti-Trap Archetype

Lore Archive

This iconic machine has haunted competitive play since the earliest formats, creating a one-sided lockout that renders entire strategies obsolete. His red eyes see through every hidden scheme, and his power nullifies them all."

Jinzo - Energy Shocker

New card Beyond the Brave · TCG 8 Oct 2026

Jinzo - Energy Shocker

Level 7 · DARK · Machine · 2400 / 1500

A Jinzo that stops only your opponent's Traps. When it's summoned, it destroys every Trap your opponent controls and gains 300 ATK for each one. While you have another monster that mentions Dark Time Wizard on your field or in your GY, your opponent can't activate Traps at all, from the hand, the field or the GY. It's named "Jinzo" on the field and in the GY, so parts of the classic engine still work with it.

Executive Summary

The "Jinzo" archetype is built around a collection of DARK and LIGHT Machine monsters, all designed to support the iconic boss monster, Jinzo. The strategy revolves around summoning this key figure to establish a powerful, field-wide negation of Trap cards, enabling an aggressive, uninterrupted offensive. Nearly every card in the archetype is designed to either search, summon, protect, or enhance this specific monster, making it the central pillar of the deck's strategy.

Defining the "Jinzo" Endboard

Unlike decks that build multiple omni-negates, the ideal Jinzo endboard focuses on a specific, targeted form of control. The primary goal is always to have Jinzo on the field to establish the anti-trap floodgate. The strongest board the archetype can make on its own consists of Jinzo and Jinzo - Layered, providing trap negation, non-destruction monster removal, and potential targeted destruction. In modern competitive play, however, a small Jinzo package is often used as a supplementary engine within more resilient frameworks like Horus or Fiendsmith, creating a multi-layered board with Jinzo as one of several disruption points. This strategy of creating a layered board with multiple threats, some of which function as self-contained engines, is a core part of the archetype's history and lore.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Anti-Meta Powerhouse: Devastating against Trap-reliant strategies like Labrynth or Traptrix, where Jinzo acts as a "silver bullet."
  • Proactive Control: Establishes a powerful floodgate that forces opponents off their primary defensive options, enabling aggressive pushes.
  • Potent Engine Potential: Its best cards can be integrated into stronger shells, serving as a high-impact, targeted strategy to win specific matchups.

Weaknesses

  • Narrow Application: Highly matchup-dependent. Struggles immensely against modern combo decks that rely on monster effects.
  • Vulnerable Board: Jinzo itself has no built-in protection, making it easy to remove with common monster or spell-based effects.
  • Inconsistent Pure Build: The standalone "pure" version lacks the resilience and consistency of modern archetypes.

Synergies & Engines

  • Horus Engine: Provides a stream of free high-ATK bodies, offering recursion and a powerful alternative win condition.
  • Fiendsmith Engine: A competitive framework that can incorporate a small Jinzo package as a targeted tech choice.
  • Orcust Engine: Offers a highly efficient one-card starter in Girsu to search and enable Jinzo the Machine Menace.

Beyond the Brave: Jinzo - Energy Shocker

  • TCG · Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026
  • OCG · 18 Jul 2026

Jinzo - Energy Shocker is the first Jinzo that stops only your opponent's Traps. When it's summoned it destroys every Trap they control and gains 300 ATK for each. Its lock needs a partner: another monster that mentions Dark Time Wizard, the Quick-Play Spell at the center of Beyond the Brave's Joey Wheeler theme. The anti-Trap guide, the core lineup and the Psychic Bounder combo in the Classic Analysis tab are unchanged. This tab covers the new card, which classic Jinzo cards still work with it, and the Dark Time Wizard cards that turn its lock on.

One-Sided Lock

Classic Jinzo stops Traps for both players and leaves Trap effects in the GY alone. Energy Shocker stops only your opponent, and it covers everything: Traps from the hand like Infinite Impermanence, Set Traps, and Trap effects in the GY.

Backrow Wipe on Summon

Normal or Special Summoned, it reveals your opponent's Set cards, destroys every Trap among them along with any face-up Traps, and gains 300 ATK per card. Three Traps put it at 3300.

Found by the Joey Cards

Its text mentions Dark Time Wizard. So Dark Time Wizard can add it, Swiftwind Panther Warrior can summon it from the Deck, and it can be revealed to summon Alligator's Dragon Knight.

Why Level 7 and Dark Time Wizard?

Like Jinzo the Machine Menace, Energy Shocker is Level 7, matching the manga's Jinzo. The Dark Time Wizard link is a nod to Joey Wheeler, who won Jinzo from Espa Roba during Battle City.

Card text

  1. This card's name becomes "Jinzo" while on the field or in the GY.
  2. If this card is Normal or Special Summoned: You can destroy all Traps your opponent controls (if face-down, reveal it), then this card gains 300 ATK for each card destroyed. You can only use this effect of "Jinzo - Energy Shocker" once per turn.

Role: Energy Shocker does two jobs. On its own it's a searchable Trap wipe that leaves a 2400+ ATK attacker. With a partner it becomes a one-sided Jinzo whose lock your opponent can't answer with a Trap. It has no protection of its own, so the lock lasts only while Energy Shocker stays face-up and its effects aren't negated.

Is the Lock On?

The Trap lock works only while Energy Shocker is face-up with its effects intact and another monster that mentions Dark Time Wizard is on your field or in your GY. Energy Shocker doesn't count for itself, but a second copy does. Set up a board to check it.

The Dark Time Wizard Cards That Turn the Lock On

Beyond the Brave's Joey Wheeler cards all mention Dark Time Wizard. The monsters among them are Energy Shocker's lock partners, and the rest find or protect it.

Dark Time Wizard

Dark Time Wizard

Quick-Play Spell · searcher

Adds any other card that mentions it from the Deck, Energy Shocker included, and comes back from the GY to your hand in the End Phase. Its other mode is a coin toss that destroys your opponent's monsters or yours.

Swiftwind Panther Warrior

Swiftwind Panther Warrior

Level 4 · EARTH · Beast-Warrior · lock partner

Main Phase Quick Effect: Tribute another monster from your hand or field to Special Summon Energy Shocker from your hand or Deck. It mentions Dark Time Wizard, so on the field it's also a lock partner. It can only attack on a turn a monster was Tributed.

Alligator's Dragon Knight

Alligator's Dragon Knight

Level 5 · WIND · Dragon · lock partner

Reveal Energy Shocker, or any card that mentions Dark Time Wizard, to Special Summon it from your hand. On summon it adds up to 2 differently named Dark Time Wizard Spells/Traps, then you discard 1. In the GY it keeps the lock on after you Tribute it.

Sleepy Scapegoats

Sleepy Scapegoats

Quick-Play Spell · one-card line

Up to 4 Tokens, plus Panther from the Deck if your opponent controls a monster. If a card of yours that mentions Dark Time Wizard would be destroyed, Energy Shocker included, you can destroy a Token instead. For the rest of that turn, only Fusion Monsters can come out of your Extra Deck.

Foolish Graverobber

Foolish Graverobber

Normal Trap · discard target

Banish it from your GY to Set a Spell/Trap from your opponent's GY, such as a Trap Energy Shocker just destroyed. The lock only stops your opponent, so your own Traps still work.

Red-Eyes Black Dragon Exceed

Red-Eyes Black Dragon Exceed

Level 9 · DARK · Dragon · Fusion · lock partner

When it's Special Summoned, it Special Summons a Level 8 or lower monster from your hand or GY. Pick Energy Shocker, and Exceed is its lock partner. See the Red-Eyes page for that line.

Which Classic Jinzo Cards Still Work

Energy Shocker is named "Jinzo" only on the field and in the GY. Support that fetches a card named "Jinzo" from the hand or Deck can't reach it. Support that asks for a "Jinzo" monster, or for "Jinzo" in the GY, can.

Meta-Counter Notes

What Energy Shocker does to the Traps and decks it's built to beat, and what beats it back.

Energy Shocker against common Traps, Trap decks and counters
Opponent's card or deck Result What happens
Infinite Impermanence Locked out Once the lock is on, it can't be activated from the hand or from the field. Its last chance is Panther's effect, before Energy Shocker lands.
Labrynth Strong The GY effects of Welcome Labrynth, Big Welcome Labrynth and Transaction Rollback are Trap effects, so the lock stops them along with the Normal Traps. The wipe clears whatever is Set. Lady Labrynth of the Silver Castle has 3000 ATK: in Attack Position, Energy Shocker needs 3 destroyed Traps (3300) to run over it.
Traptrix Strong Traptrix Trap Hole Nightmare would negate Energy Shocker's summon effect, and Floodgate Trap Hole would flip it face-down. With the lock already on when it lands, neither can be activated. The Traptrix monsters' own effects still work.
Set floodgates: Skill Drain, There Can Be Only One, Anti-Spell Fragrance, Royal Decree Wiped If they're still Set when Energy Shocker lands, the wipe destroys them. With the lock on, your opponent can't flip them in response.
Face-up Skill Drain Beats it It negates Energy Shocker on the field: no wipe and no lock. Destroy it first with a Spell such as Harpie's Feather Duster or Lightning Storm.
Face-up Anti-Spell Fragrance Work around it Dark Time Wizard and Sleepy Scapegoats would have to be Set and wait a turn. Monster effects still work: Panther from your hand can Tribute a monster to summon Energy Shocker, and Jector can if your opponent also has a Set Trap. The wipe then destroys Fragrance.
Effect Veiler Its answer Chained to Energy Shocker's summon effect, it cancels both the wipe and the lock until the end of the turn. It's a monster effect, so the lock can't stop it. Everlasting Alloy from your hand negates it, because Veiler targets a Machine you control.
Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring Stops the setup It hits every Deck effect on the way in: Dark Time Wizard's and the Dragon Knight's searches, Sleepy Scapegoats, Panther's Deck summon and Jector's search. Energy Shocker's wipe doesn't touch the Deck, so Ash can't stop it.
Called by the Grave and other GY banishing Breaks the lock If your only lock partner is in the GY, banishing it turns the lock off. Called by the Grave is a Spell, so the lock doesn't stop it. Keep one partner on the field and one in the GY.

Where It Goes Wrong

  • The lock isn't automatic

    In a deck with only classic Jinzo cards, Energy Shocker never locks Traps. There it's a Trap wipe on a 2400 ATK body, which is still useful, but it isn't a Jinzo.

  • A Level 7 in your opening hand

    Without Panther, Jector or two monsters to Tribute, it stays in your hand. Psychic Bounder and Law of the Cosmos can't help, because in the hand and Deck it isn't named "Jinzo". Revealing it for Alligator's Dragon Knight is the best use of a stuck copy.

  • Openings before it lands

    Every step before Energy Shocker reaches the field is open to Traps. Infinite Impermanence on Panther's effect ends Lines 1 and 2, so draw it out first if you can.

  • One wipe per turn

    The summon effect is a hard once per turn. A second Energy Shocker summoned the same turn can't wipe again, but it still keeps the first one's lock on.
