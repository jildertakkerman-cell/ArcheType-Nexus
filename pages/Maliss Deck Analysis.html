Maliss

Down the Rabbit Hole of a Digital Wonderland

Archetype Overview

The Maliss archetype (stylized M?LICE) is a sophisticated DARK Cyberse Link strategy that redefines resource management. It establishes a "banishment-centric" economy, treating the "Underground" (banish zone) as a second hand. The core mechanic mirrors computer malware: executing rapid code (Traps from Set position) and creating persistent threats that are difficult to delete. Monsters banish each other to activate effects, then Special Summon themselves from the banished zone by paying 300 LP, creating a recursive loop of "cost" becoming "advantage."

The Mainframe: Core Monster Roster

Maliss Dormouse

The premier one-card starter. Its Ignition Effect banishes any "Maliss" monster from the Deck, initiating the core combo. When banished itself, you can pay 300 LP to Special Summon it.

Maliss White Rabbit

The secondary starter. On summon, it Sets a "Maliss" Trap from the Deck, providing access to extenders or disruption. Revives from banish by paying 300 LP.

Maliss Chessy Cat

A key extender and consistency tool. It allows you to banish a "Maliss" card from hand to draw two cards, improving hand quality and triggering banished effects.

Maliss March Hare

The engine's regulator. Link Monsters that point to March Hare gain targeting protection. Extends by banishing a Maliss from hand/GY (Quick Effect), and recycles banished resources to hand.

The Source Code: Spells & Traps

Maliss in Underground

Maliss in Underground

The Environmental Engine. This Field Spell is the deck's CPU. Upon activation, it banishes a "Maliss" card from Deck, Hand, or GY. Since Maliss monsters summon themselves when banished, this effectively acts as a "Special Summon from Deck" for a cost of 300 LP.

Bonus: If you have 3+ banished Maliss Traps, Link Monsters gain 3000 ATK.

Maliss in the Mirror

Maliss in the Mirror

The Interface of Disruption. Targeted effect negation that banishes a Maliss monster for cost (triggering its effect). In the GY, it can banish a Maliss card to add a card of the same type from Deck to Hand, recycling your resources.

The "Code" Execution Protocols

These Traps can be activated the turn they are Set by banishing a face-up Maliss monster.

Maliss GWC-06

GWC-06

Recursion. Special Summons a Maliss from GY/Banishment and gains LP. Critical for recovering from board wipes.

Maliss MTP-07

MTP-07

Search & Destroy. Adds a Maliss monster from Deck to hand, then can non-target banish a card on the field.

Maliss TB-11

TB-11

The Cheat Code. Special Summons a Maliss from Deck OR cheats out a Maliss Link Monster from the Extra Deck if the opponent controls 3+ cards.

Maliss White Rabbit

Maliss Red Ransom

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • High consistency with multiple one-card starters.
  • Resilient, recursive engine that is difficult to stop permanently.
  • Explosive Link climbing potential for powerful endboards.

Weaknesses

  • Extremely vulnerable to cards that prevent banishing (Artifact Lancea).
  • The Normal Summon starter is a key choke point for hand traps.
  • Susceptible to mass board removal like Nibiru, the Primal Being.
