Dr. Faker's Scrap Heap

Garbage

Cheap DARK Fiends that pay Life Points instead of cards, and exist to build a Number.

Lore Archive

"Thrown away, cheap and easy to replace. The Garbage monsters were never meant to win on their own. They were the scrap a Number was built from."

In Yu-Gi-Oh! ZEXAL, the Garbage monsters filled Dr. Faker's Main Deck. Garbage Ogre and Garbage Lord were fodder for his Numbers: Number 53: Heart-eartH and its evolved form, Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon. Vector manipulated Faker and later possessed him in the middle of his duel. So when Beyond the Brave printed Garbage Golem, the first new Garbage monster in more than a decade, in the same set as Vector's Umbral Horror monsters, fans read it as Vector support in disguise. It works in both decks.

Executive Summary: Paying in Life Points

Garbage is one of the smallest series in the game. Before Beyond the Brave it was two Main Deck monsters. Garbage Ogre searches Garbage Lord, and Garbage Lord is a Level 5 DARK Fiend you can Special Summon from your hand by paying 2000 LP. The series dates back to Cosmo Blazer (January 2013), where Garbage Ogre debuted. The plan is to turn Life Points into Level 5 bodies, and those bodies into Rank 5 DARK Xyz Monsters, above all Dr. Faker's Number 53: Heart-eartH.

It's an expensive plan. Garbage Lord can only be Xyz Material for a DARK monster, every copy summoned from the hand costs a quarter of your starting Life Points, and neither card does anything once it's on the field. Garbage has always been a casual, anime-inspired deck rather than a competitive one, and this tab treats it that way. The Beyond the Brave tab covers Garbage Golem, which fixes much of this.

Foundational Mechanics

Pay 2000, Keep the Normal Summon

Garbage Lord's summon is a summoning procedure, not an effect. It has no once-per-turn limit, it doesn't start a chain, and it leaves your Normal Summon free. Two copies cost 4000 LP, half your starting Life Points.

DARK Xyz Only

Garbage Lord can't be Xyz Material except for a DARK Xyz Monster. The restriction covers Xyz Summons only, so it can still be a Tribute, Link Material or a non-Tuner for a Synchro Summon.

The Heart-eartH Chain

Number 53: Heart-eartH needs three Level 5 monsters. When a card effect destroys it while it has no materials, it becomes Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon from the Extra Deck, with Number 53 attached as its material.

The classic way to reach Number 53, and the reason Inferno Reckless Summon has always been in Garbage lists. It's a going-second line: Inferno Reckless Summon only works while your opponent controls a face-up monster. Opening hand: Garbage Ogre and Inferno Reckless Summon.

Other classic routes

  • Mist Archfiend: a Level 5 DARK Fiend you can Normal Summon without Tributing. With one Garbage Lord it makes a 2-material Rank 5 DARK Xyz, such as Adreus, Keeper of Armageddon, without Inferno Reckless Summon. If you Normal Summoned it without Tributing and it's still on the field in the End Phase, it destroys itself and you take 1000 damage.
  • Tannhauser Gate: Garbage Lord has 0 ATK, so it can combine Levels with another monster of the same Type you control with 1000 or less ATK.
  • Synchro: the material restriction only covers Xyz Summons. A Tuner next to Garbage Lord makes a Level 6 or higher Synchro Monster.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Free bodies: Garbage Lord's summon has no once-per-turn limit and doesn't use your Normal Summon.
  • Direct search: Garbage Ogre adds Garbage Lord straight from the Deck, and Sangan works too.
  • Sticky boss: Heart-eartH absorbs up to three destructions, then evolves into Number 92.

Weaknesses

  • Life Point drain: every Garbage Lord from the hand costs 2000 LP.
  • DARK Xyz only: Garbage Lord can't go into the generic Rank 5s most decks run.
  • No interaction: neither Ogre nor Lord touches your opponent's cards, Heart-eartH has 100 ATK, and Inferno Reckless Summon hands your opponent free monsters.

Synergies & Tech

  • Mist Archfiend: a Level 5 DARK Fiend you can Normal Summon without Tributing, for 2-material Rank 5s.
  • Inferno Reckless Summon: three Lords for 2000 LP, going second.
  • Garbage Golem: the Beyond the Brave card. See the next tab.

Beyond the Brave: What Changed

  • TCG · Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026
  • OCG · 18 Jul 2026

Beyond the Brave gives Garbage its first new card in more than a decade: Garbage Golem, a Level 4 DARK Fiend with 0 ATK and 0 DEF. The Classic Analysis tab still covers Garbage Ogre, Garbage Lord and the Heart-eartH Numbers. Golem adds three things the series never had: a free second body, removal, and a way to recycle Xyz materials.

Free Off Any 0-Stat Monster

Golem Special Summons itself while you control a monster with 0 ATK or 0 DEF. Garbage Lord has 0 ATK and Mist Archfiend has 0 DEF, and so do many Umbral Horror, Yubel and Mikanko monsters.

Removal That Takes Itself Along

Reveal three Xyz Monsters with different Ranks from your Extra Deck to destroy Golem and any other card on the field, face-up or face-down.

A Detach That Pays You Back

From the GY, Golem detaches a material from one of your monsters, then can revive a monster with 0 ATK or 0 DEF, including the one it just detached.

Xyz Links: Where Golem Plugs In

Golem touches the Extra Deck twice. Its removal needs three Xyz Monsters with different Ranks in your Extra Deck, and its GY effect needs a material to detach. These are the Xyz Monsters it works with best, and the guides that cover them.

The reveal check

  • Only the Extra Deck counts. An Xyz Monster already on the field can't be revealed. If you're counting on one, use Golem's removal before you Xyz Summon it.
  • Nothing is spent. Revealed cards stay in the Extra Deck, so you can summon them afterwards.
  • Garbage covers three Ranks naturally: Rank 4 (Number 66: Master Key Beetle), Rank 5 (Number 53: Heart-eartH or Adreus, Keeper of Armageddon) and Rank 9 (Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon).

Umbral Horror: Golem in the Barian's Deck

Golem's best home outside Garbage. Thousand Umbral Horror has 0 ATK and 0 DEF, Number 101: Silent Honor ARK - Soul Asylum can search Golem, and Golem's GY effect revives Thousand, which then brings out Shining Umbral Horror. The Barian's guide covers the rest of the engine. Opening hand: V Umbral Horror.

Locks to respect

  • Thousand Umbral Horror: after its effect you can only Special Summon Xyz Monsters from the Extra Deck until the end of your next turn. Every summon below is an Xyz Summon.

  • Barian's Seventh Untopia: each player can Special Summon non-"Number" monsters from the Extra Deck only twice per turn. Xyz Armor Fortress is the only one this line uses.

Guide: The Heart-eartH Countdown

Number 53 only becomes Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon when a card effect destroys it with no materials left, and it starts with three. Golem strips two of them in one turn. Summon Golem while a Garbage Lord is still on the field, before you Xyz Summon Number 53 (for example, during Faker's Gambit), and save this for a turn when your opponent has nothing worth Golem's removal.

  1. Garbage Golem

    Golem Targets Your Own Heart-eartH

    Reveal three Xyz Monsters with different Ranks from your Extra Deck. Number 53 is on the field, so reveal others, such as Number 66: Master Key Beetle, Adreus and Number 92. Target Number 53. When it would be destroyed, detach 1 material instead. Golem is still destroyed.

    Number 53: 2 materials. GY: Garbage Golem, Garbage Lord.

  2. Garbage Lord

    Golem Detaches Another

    Banish Golem from the GY and detach 1 material from Number 53, then Special Summon a Garbage Lord from the GY. It has 0 ATK.

    Number 53: 1 material. Field: also Garbage Lord (2400 DEF).

  3. Number 53: Heart-eartH

    The Last Shield

    The next time Number 53 would be destroyed, by battle or by a card effect, detach its last material instead.

    Number 53: no materials.

  4. Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon

    Heart-eartH Dragon

    When a card effect destroys Number 53 with no materials, Special Summon Number 92: Heart-eartH Dragon from your Extra Deck and attach Number 53 from the GY to it. This counts as an Xyz Summon.

    Field: Number 92 (1 material: Number 53).

Battle doesn't count: if Number 53 is destroyed by battle with no materials, it just goes to the GY. Only a card effect sets off the evolution.

Weak Points to Play Around

  • Dead without a 0-stat monster

    Golem can't summon itself onto an empty field. In Garbage, it needs Garbage Ogre or Garbage Lord alongside it, or Mist Archfiend.

  • The reveal cost

    Its removal needs three Xyz Monsters with different Ranks in your Extra Deck. Decks built around Synchro, Fusion or Link Monsters often can't spare the slots.

  • Your turn only

    None of its effects is a Quick Effect. Golem can't respond to anything, and it can't protect a board on your opponent's turn.

  • Destruction and targeting protection

    Its removal targets and destroys, so it does nothing to cards that can't be targeted or can't be destroyed by card effects.

  • Negation and GY disruption

    Effect Veiler or Infinite Impermanence on Golem stops its removal. Ghost Belle & Haunted Mansion can negate its GY effect, because that effect can Special Summon from the GY, and D.D. Crow can banish it from the GY first. Under Macro Cosmos, Golem is banished instead of going to the GY at all.

  • Garbage Lord still costs 2000

    Every Garbage line on this page starts at 6000 LP. Two Lords from the hand in one turn put you at 4000.

Verdict

Garbage Golem is the card Garbage has needed since 2013: a free Level 4 that turns one Garbage Lord into two bodies, adds removal, and speeds up the Heart-eartH evolution. It doesn't make Garbage competitive. The deck still pays 2000 LP for every line and has no protection. It does make Garbage a working casual deck with real first- and second-turn plans. Play 3 in Garbage. Outside it, Golem is at its best in Umbral Horror and Yubel, where 0-stat monsters are the norm.
