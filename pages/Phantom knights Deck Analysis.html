The Phantom Knights

A Strategic Deep Dive into the Ghosts of Rebellion

Lore Archive

"They are the spectral manifestations of a shattered resistance—fallen soldiers who refuse to let death extinguish their will to fight."

The tragic lore of the Phantom Knights stems from the devastating inter-dimensional war of the Yu-Gi-Oh! ARC-V era. Hailing from the peaceful Xyz Dimension, these warriors are the deck of Yuto, a desperate resistance fighter. When his home was ruthlessly invaded by the Fusion Dimension's Duel Academy, countless innocent duelists and comrades were mercilessly defeated and turned into physical cards. The Phantom Knights literally represent the souls of these fallen allies. Their ghostly, tattered armor and signature mechanic of banishing themselves from the graveyard is symbolic: they are spirits returning from the afterlife to support the living, rallying behind their ace, Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon, to avenge their devastated home.

Yuto's Phantom Knights from Yu-Gi-Oh! ARC-V are empty suits of armor, each named after a single piece of it, led by Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon. Follow a marked name to walk the trail.

  1. A cloak with no one inside. Its retrain is Decayed Cloak.

  2. Boots; with Ancient Cloak it stands in the art of The Phantom Knights' Round Blaze.

  3. Greaves, which guard the shins. Its retrain is Doomed Soleret.

  4. A solleret, the armored foot. Like Speedroid Terrortop, it calls itself to an empty field to help summon a Level 3, both tied to the dimension dragons.

  5. A vambrace, which covers the forearm.

  6. Gauntlets, guarding hands and wrists: it gains DEF for its allies. The art is from ARC-V manga chapter 2.

  7. A brigandine, body armor of rings or plates, from the same manga chapter.

  8. A bardiche, a long cleaver-bladed axe; a cover card of Legendary Hero Decks.

  9. Probably the Dullahan, the headless knight on a black horse. Raider's Knight looks like its upgraded form.

  10. A Raidraptor, but a Warrior, the only one, just as Raider's Wing is the only Winged Beast among the Phantom Knights.

  11. New in Chaos Origins: a cloaked figure like Yuto, whose scythe looks like Dark Rebellion's wings.

  12. Yuto's dragon, and Z-ARC's vengeance, carried by the resistance against Duel Academy. The only dimension dragon with a Rank, and with a single effect.

  13. A requiem, a Mass for the dead, with stained-glass wings. It was born when Yuto possessed Yuya, in ARC-V episode 110, raised by The Phantom Knights' Rank-Up-Magic Launch.

  14. Dark Rebellion and Raidraptor - Rise Falcon in one, the cover card of Phantom Rage. Its crest is on Phantom Knights' Rank-Up-Magic Force.

  15. Dark Rebellion retrained for the coming Magnificent Maestros; it counts as a Phantom Knight, since Yuto, who used both, is its master.

Archetypal Identity: The Ghosts of Rebellion

The Phantom Knights are an archetype of DARK Warrior monsters representing spectral remnants of fallen warriors. This ghostly theme is the foundation of their gameplay, which treats the Graveyard not as a discard pile, but as a primary resource zone'?a spectral armory from which the Knights launch their most potent effects. Their strategy revolves around filling the GY with their members and then banishing them to activate secondary effects, creating a relentless cycle of recursion and advantage. It's a testament to their design that even when struck down, the spectral remnants of these fallen warriors rise again, stronger than before.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Resilience: Highly resistant to single points of disruption due to redundant GY effects.
  • Versatility: Capable of building a strong control board or executing a powerful OTK.
  • Synergy: The compact engine integrates seamlessly with other powerful strategies like Burning Abyss, Raidraptors, and Adventure Token.

Weaknesses

  • Graveyard Hate: Extremely vulnerable to cards like Dimension Shifter and Bystials that banish resources from the GY.
  • Floodgates: Cards that restrict Special Summons (e.g., Summon Limit) or monster effects (e.g., Skill Drain) can completely shut down the deck.

Synergistic Engines

  • Burning Abyss ("PK Fire"): A classic pairing leveraging shared Level 3 DARK attributes for explosive combos.
  • Raidraptor: Synergizes through shared DARK Xyz support and Rank-Up-Magic spells.
  • Adventure Token: Provides an omni-negate to protect the main combo from hand traps.

Magnificent Maestros: The Dragon Joins the Knights

Yuto's dragon returns as one of the Four Heavenly Dragons, and this time it is a Phantom Knight. Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon, Four Heavenly Dragons is always treated as a "The Phantom Knights" card, so the archetype's revival Traps and Rank-Up-Magic Spells can reach it. It destroys cards when it is Special Summoned. On your opponent's turn, the moment they activate something, it turns into any Rank 4 DARK Xyz Monster in your Extra Deck. This tab covers what the card does and doesn't count for, two lines checked against the duel engine, a picker for what to turn it into, and the early claims about it that don't hold up.

You can only use each effect of "Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon, Four Heavenly Dragons" once per turn.

What the text means

  • A Phantom Knights monster. Konami's Q&A says a card treated as an archetype's card is that archetype's monster too. So Phantom Knights' Fog Blade, Sword and Wing can revive it from the GY, and The Phantom Knights' Rank-Up-Magic Requiem can bring it back or summon it.
  • An "Xyz Dragon" monster. Its name contains Xyz Dragon, and that is what Malevolent Scythe's search and Phantom Knights' Rank-Up-Magic Force look for.
  • Not Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon. Its name is different (四天の龍 ダーク・リベリオン・エクシーズ・ドラゴン in Japanese) and no clause says otherwise. Dark Requiem Xyz Dragon's effects need "Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon" as material, so they stay off on top of this card. Arc Rebellion Xyz Dragon only asks for a DARK Xyz Monster as material, so it works.
  • The first effect works on any Special Summon: an Xyz Summon, a Rank-Up or a revival. You target the same number of cards on each side, and the dragon itself can be one of yours. It needs at least one card on your opponent's field, so it does nothing on turn one.
  • The second effect doesn't negate. The dragon goes under a Rank 4 DARK Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck, materials and all, while the chain resolves. That monster's continuous effects apply before your opponent's effect resolves, but it arrives too late to respond to that chain.
  • The second effect costs a material. A dragon revived from the GY comes back with none, so it can only use the first effect. Each effect is once per turn.

What It Turns Into

The swap takes any Rank 4 DARK Xyz Monster, but the new monster arrives in the middle of your opponent's chain. What helps is a continuous effect that is already on when their effect resolves, an effect that reacts to what happens next, or a step towards your own turn. These five fit a Phantom Knights Extra Deck.

Evilswarm Ophion

While it has material, Level 5 or higher monsters can't be Special Summoned, by either player. The dragon becomes its material, so the lock is on before your opponent's effect resolves.

Evilswarm Nightmare

Evilswarm Nightmare

When your opponent Special Summons a monster, detach 1 material to turn it face-down. A face-down monster can't be Link, Synchro or Xyz Material. It has no once-per-turn limit, only the materials the dragon passed on.

Exosister Karmael

Exosister Karmael

When your opponent activates a monster effect or a Normal Spell/Trap, detach all its materials: that effect becomes "add 1 card from your opponent's GY to their hand". It is too late for the chain that summoned it, so it waits for their next one.

Raider's Knight

Raider's Knight

In your own Main Phase it stacks a Phantom Knights, Raidraptor or Xyz Dragon Xyz one Rank higher or lower on itself, such as Arc Rebellion Xyz Dragon. That monster is destroyed in your opponent's next End Phase.

Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon

Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon

The original, which Dark Requiem Xyz Dragon's effects need as material. A Set Phantom Knights' Rank-Up-Magic Force (in either player's Main Phase) or Requiem (in yours) finishes the climb.

The Four Heavenly Dragons

Each of ARC-V's four dimension dragons got a retrain for its duelist's deck. Three of them share a pattern: an effect when they are Special Summoned, then a Quick Effect on your opponent's activation that brings out another monster of their own summoning type. Each one is at its best in its own archetype rather than in a deck built around all four.

Building With It

In the Extra Deck

  • One copy. Both effects are once per turn, and Requiem and the revival Traps can bring that copy back from the GY.
  • Two or three of the Rank 4 targets above, chosen for the decks you expect.
  • Arc Rebellion Xyz Dragon for Line 2, and the original Dark Rebellion if you play Dark Requiem.

These counts are our suggestion, not tournament data.

What Stops It

  • Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring: both lines search or summon from the Deck (Scythe, Decayed Cloak, Silent Boots, Bardiche). It can't stop Soleret's Set.
  • Infinite Impermanence or Effect Veiler on Scythe mid-combo, or on the dragon before your opponent activates anything.
  • Nibiru, the Primal Being, live from the fifth summon in both lines.
  • Graveyard banishing such as D.D. Crow: Soleret and Silent Boots work from the GY.
  • Plays that activate nothing. A Link or Synchro Summon gives the dragon nothing to answer.

Verdict

The best Rank 4 the deck has had. Phantom Knights can summon it, revive it and rank it up with their own cards. Your opponent has to play around it without knowing what it will become. It doesn't negate, so it adds to the Chaos Origins board rather than replacing Fog Blade or Dark Requiem. Doomed Soleret now reaches it from a single card in hand.
