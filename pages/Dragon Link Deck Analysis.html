Lore Archive

"Dragon Link is the ultimate expression of draconic swarming—a deck that chains Dragon Special Summons into an endless cascade of Link and Synchro formations. Every Dragon summoned enables the next, creating a self-sustaining engine of draconic fury that culminates in boards so oppressive they redefine what it means to go first."

Strategic Overview

The history of the TCG is a cycle of obsolescence, yet Dragon Link stands as the eternal anomaly. Colloquially known as "D-Link," this strategy is not merely an archetype but a synergistic conglomerate of the most potent Dragon-type support printed over the last decade.

Following the prohibition of Borreload Savage Dragon and Baronne de Fleur, the deck has executed a critical pivot. No longer relying on generic omni-negates, the 2025 variant integrates the raw power of the Bystial control suite, the recursion of the Red-Eyes engine, and the oppressive disruption of the new Light and Darkness Dragonlord.

Archetypal Architecture

Rokket Core

Rokket Tracer

Function: Velocity & Extension

The tactical backbone. Rokket Tracer turns spent resources (Field Spells) into fresh bodies. Quick Launch serves as an unconditional extender.

Bystial Suite

Bystial Lubellion

Function: Control & Removal

Midrange dominance. Lubellion places continuous S/T support. Magnamhut guarantees follow-up for Turn 3.

Dragunity Bridge

Dragunity Knight - Romulus

Function: Consistency Access

The choke point. Romulus searches Dragon Ravine, unlocking the entire graveyard as a second hand.

Red-Eyes Engine

Red-Eyes Darkness Metal

Function: Recursion & Negation

Black Metal Dragon is not once-per-turn. Loops with REDMD to search Fullmetal Dragon for negation.

Operational Logic

Sequence A: The Black Metal Loop

Input: Black Metal Dragon (1-Card Starter)

  1. 01. Normal Summon Black Metal Dragon. Link into Striker Dragon.
  2. 02. Chain Link 1: Black Metal (Search REDMD). Chain Link 2: Striker (Search Boot Sector). (Chain Block)
  3. 03. Banish Striker to SS REDMD. Effect: Revive Black Metal Dragon.
  4. 04. Link Romulus using REDMD + Black Metal. Search Dragon Ravine & Fullmetal Dragon.
  5. 05. Activate Ravine -> Send Absorouter Dragon -> Search Rokket Tracer.
  6. 06. Activate Boot Sector -> SS Tracer. Tracer Pop Ravine -> SS Rokket Recharger. Proceed to Pisty/Seal lines.

Sequence B: The Chaos Bridge

Input: Starliege Seyfert + 1 Discard

  1. 01. Normal Summon Seyfert. Send self to GY -> Search Black Dragon Collapserpent.
  2. 02. Banish Seyfert -> SS Collapserpent. Link into Striker Dragon.
  3. 03. CL1 Collapserpent (Search Wyverburster), CL2 Striker.
  4. 04. Banish Collapserpent -> SS Wyverburster. Link Romulus.
  5. 05. Standard Ravine line ensues. Note: GY is now primed with LIGHT/DARK for Chaos plays.

Sequence C: The Light & Darkness Pivot

Context: Post-Banlist Adaptation

L&D Dragonlord

With Savage Dragon gone, Light and Darkness Dragonlord (LADDL) becomes the premier negation boss. It negates Spells, Traps, and Monsters by reducing its stats.

  • Summoning: Use Fallen of Albaz (dumped via Ravine/Seals) fusing with Lubellion via Branded Banishment on opponent's turn.
  • Timing: Summon LADDL after opponent commits initial resources but before they establish a board breaker like DRNM.

Competitive Analysis

Vs. Snake-Eye / Fire King

Dynamic: High-speed resource war.

The primary goal is to interrupt their recursion loop. Banishing Promethean Princess from their GY using Bystial Druiswurm or Magnamhut is often a turn-ending blow.

  • � KEY PLAY: Establish Bystial Dis Pater early to shuffle back their banished resources, permanently removing them from play.

Vs. Tenpai Dragon

Dynamic: Battle Phase survival.

Tenpai relies entirely on the Battle Phase to OTK. Your interactions must happen before they enter battle or declare an attack.

  • � KEY PLAY: Use Hieratic Seal to bounce their Normal Summon or first Synchro immediately. Do not greed for a larger board; simple interruption is safer.
