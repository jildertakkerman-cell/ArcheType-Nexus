Lore Archive

"The Lunalight dancers perform a deadly ballet under the full moon, their Fusion Summons creating increasingly powerful beast-warrior formations. Leo Dancer is immune to card effects and attacks twice, while Cat Dancer inflicts damage by attacking every opponent's monster. Their grace is lethal, and the moonlight reveals no mercy."

Executive Summary

The Lunalight archetype is a DARK Beast-Warrior, Fusion-centric strategy renowned for its aggressive, OTK (One-Turn Kill) potential. The deck operates through intricate combo lines that masterfully leverage graveyard effects and resource loops. It revolves around a core ensemble of Main Deck monsters that search, special summon, and recur resources, all building towards the crescendo of summoning their powerful Fusion boss monsters. It's a dance of shadows and moonlight, where every move is calculated to deliver a final, devastating performance. As of the 2026 Legendary ARC-V Decks update, the addition of Lunalight Scarlet Tiger has pushed the archetype beyond its purely linear OTK roots, giving it a handtrap-resilient midrange gear that can salvage a turn even after an early interruption.

The Lunalight Ensemble

Each member of the Lunalight troupe plays a vital role in the overarching choreography. Mastering their individual steps and how they interact is key to a flawless performance. You can see the full cast of Lunalight cards in the official database.

Core Dancers: Main Deck Monsters

Lunalight Kaleido Chick

The premier starter. Sends a "Lunalight" from Deck or Extra Deck to the GY as cost, setting up entire combos.

Lunalight Yellow Marten

The heart of the recursion loop. Bounces a "Lunalight" card to summon itself and searches a Spell/Trap when sent to GY by an effect.

Lunalight Tiger

A critical Pendulum extender that revives a "Lunalight" from the GY once per turn.

Lunalight Scarlet Tiger

Legendary ARC-V Decks — August 2026

Level 5 DARK Beast-Warrior, 1900/1500. The deck's handtrap insurance. Special Summons itself from hand whenever a "Lunalight" monster's effect is activated, and if that activation happened on a turn your opponent used a card or effect (i.e. they tried to interrupt you), you also add Polymerization from your Deck or GY. If sent to the GY by a card effect, it Special Summons a "Lunalight" from your GY or face-up Extra Deck with its activated effects negated — but continuous effects, like Liger Dancer's immunity and double attack, remain fully live.

Supporting Choreography: Spells

Luna Light Perfume

A powerful dual-threat: revives a "Lunalight" from GY and can be banished from GY to search for any "Lunalight" monster.

Lunalight Fusion

The archetype's fusion spell. Can use a material from the Deck or Extra Deck if your opponent controls an Extra Deck monster.

Lunalight Wolf

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Explosive OTK Potential: Capable of ending games swiftly with high-ATK, multi-attacking monsters.
  • High Consistency: Numerous starters and searchers make the core engine readily accessible.
  • Resilience to certain disruption: The "send for cost" mechanic of Kaleido Chick can play through on-field negation.
  • Handtrap Insurance (2026 Update): Lunalight Scarlet Tiger converts an opponent's negation of a starter into a free body and a Polymerization search, letting the deck continue its combo despite the interruption.

Weaknesses

  • Vulnerable to Hand Traps: Extremely weak to cards like Dimension Shifter, Artifact Lancea, and Droll & Lock Bird. (Partially mitigated since 2026 by Lunalight Scarlet Tiger's insurance effect — see Strengths — though it does nothing against hand traps that stop the Normal Summon itself.)
  • Fragile Choke Points: The Normal Summon of the starter is a clear and easily targeted point of disruption.
