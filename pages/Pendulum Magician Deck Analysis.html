Lore Archive

"The Pendulum Magicians are Yuya's evolved spellcaster corps who mastered the art of Pendulum Summoning. Harmonizing Magician, Wisdom-Eye, and Double Iris manipulate scales and searches with clockwork precision, fueling devastating combos. Their mastery of Pendulum Zones transforms every scale into a stage for magical supremacy."

Executive Summary

The Pendulum Magician archetype stands as an intricate combo-control strategy, celebrated for its unique "Destruction Economy." This potent system revolves around the deliberate destruction of its own cards to unleash a cascade of powerful effects, generating overwhelming card advantage. The core gameplay is a masterclass in resource management: setting Pendulum Scales, leveraging destruction to search for combo pieces, and culminating in an explosive Pendulum Summon to construct a formidable board of negations and disruptions.

The Conclave of Casters

Initiators & Extenders

Performapal Skullcrobat Joker

The premier Normal Summon and quintessential starter. It searches any "Magician," "Performapal," or "Odd-Eyes" monster, providing unmatched consistency to assemble your opening hand.

Wisdom-Eye Magician

The essential scale-setter and primary catalyst. It destroys itself to place any "Magician" from the Deck into the Pendulum Zone, masterfully kickstarting the Destruction Economy.

Harmonizing Magician

The most powerful main deck extender. When Pendulum Summoned from the hand, it summons another "Magician" directly from the Deck, enabling instant access to Synchro or Xyz plays.

The Destruction Engine

Double Iris Magician

When destroyed, this magician searches for a powerful "Pendulumgraph" card, a critical step that unlocks the deck's most potent advantage and control engines.

Purple Poison Magician

When destroyed, it targets and destroys one face-up card on the field, providing a searchable and repeatable form of removal that integrates seamlessly into your combos.

Black Fang Magician

When destroyed, it Special Summons a DARK Spellcaster from the Graveyard. This offers crucial recursion and combo extension, reviving key pieces for further plays.

The Grand Performance

The optimal strategy involves a calculated opening act: summoning a Link Monster *before* the main Pendulum Summon. This initial maneuver generates resources and opens monster zones, transforming the subsequent Pendulum Summon into a devastating mid-combo crescendo rather than a simple opening play.

Impact Report TCG · Magnificent Monsters · 4 Sep 2026 OCG · Limit Over Collection: The Heroes · 28 Feb 2026

Four Magicians. Two of Them Are Yours.

The same wave prints four cards with "Magician" in the name, and only two of them are built for this deck. Horoscope Sorcerer and Starfrost Magician form a self-contained opening that generates a body, a search and a second body before you touch your Normal Summon. The other two carry archetype locks that shut the Pendulum engine off the moment they resolve.

Two Astrographs, two Stargazers. These are retrains, not errata. The original Astrograph Sorcerer is still Forbidden and still a separate card; Astrograph Sorcerer, the Starfrost Magician is a brand new, fully legal Pendulum Monster. The same applies to the original Stargazer Magician and the new Horoscope Sorcerer, the Stargazer Magician. Both new cards can sit in a deck alongside their ancestors where the banlist allows.

The Two That Belong Here

Horoscope Sorcerer, the Stargazer Magician

Horoscope Sorcerer, the Stargazer Magician
Level 5 DARK Spellcaster / Pendulum Scale 1 1200 / 2400
It summons itself

Discard one other "Magician" Pendulum Monster, "Performapal" monster or "Odd-Eyes" monster from your hand and it puts itself on the field. No Normal Summon, no scale, no setup.

It tutors on arrival

Any Special Summon of this card adds a Pendulum Monster with exactly 2500 ATK from the Deck. Starfrost Magician, Chronograph Sorcerer and the Odd-Eyes line all sit in that club.

It destroys on purpose

Target a Pendulum Monster Card you control and destroy it, then add a face-up Pendulum Monster from your Extra Deck to your hand. Pointed at itself, it feeds the Destruction Economy the deck was already built around.

In the scale

A Spell negate. While you control a "Magician" Pendulum Monster or an "Odd-Eyes" monster, it negates a resolving opponent Spell and destroys itself. The archetype has never had one of these outside the Pendulumgraph Traps.

Astrograph Sorcerer, the Starfrost Magician

Astrograph Sorcerer, the Starfrost Magician
Level 7 DARK Spellcaster / Pendulum Scale 1 2500 / 2000
It answers destruction

Whenever a face-up Monster Card you control is destroyed by battle or card effect, it Special Summons itself from the hand, then revives one of the destroyed monsters from your face-up Extra Deck or Graveyard.

The Quick Effect

During the Main Phase it can Tribute itself to Fusion Summon a Dragon Fusion Monster, shuffling the materials in from your field and face-up Extra Deck. Odd-Eyes Vortex Dragon comes down without ever touching a Fusion Spell.

In the scale

Destroy it during your Main Phase to place any Scale 1 Pendulum Monster from hand or Deck into the Zone. The placed card's Pendulum Effect is switched off for the turn, so treat it as a scale fetcher, not as a way to set up a negate.

The self-loop

Destroying Starfrost in the scale is itself a face-up Monster Card destruction, so a second copy sitting in hand comes straight down off the first one.

The Two-Card Engine

Horoscope Sorcerer plus any pitchable "Magician", "Performapal" or "Odd-Eyes" card. That is the whole requirement. It resolves entirely in the hand and the Extra Deck, before the Normal Summon and before the Pendulum Summon are spent.

Hand

Discard one other "Magician" Pendulum, "Performapal" or "Odd-Eyes" card to Special Summon Horoscope Sorcerer from your hand.

Search

Its summon trigger adds a 2500 ATK Pendulum Monster from the Deck. Take Astrograph Sorcerer, the Starfrost Magician.

Destroy

Activate Horoscope's third effect targeting itself. It is destroyed and lands face-up in your Extra Deck, and you may add a face-up Pendulum Monster from there to your hand.

Trigger

A face-up Monster Card you controlled was just destroyed, so Starfrost Magician Special Summons itself out of your hand.

Recall

Starfrost then brings Horoscope Sorcerer back from the face-up Extra Deck. You end on a Level 7 and a Level 5, up a card on the exchange, with your Normal Summon and Pendulum Summon both untouched.

A Level 5 and a Level 7 is not a dead end. That pair is Link material for Beyond the Pendulum, Synchro material alongside a Tuner, or two of the three bodies for a Link-3, all before Skullcrobat Joker has been summoned. The engine's job is to arrive early enough that the rest of the deck still gets a full turn.

The Ruling That Makes It Work

Starfrost's hand trigger reads "a face-up Monster Card", not "a monster". A Pendulum Monster sitting in the Pendulum Zone is treated as a Spell Card while it is there, but it never stops being a Monster Card. Destroying a scale therefore counts.

Wisdom-Eye Magician

Its scale effect destroys itself to place a "Magician" from the Deck. That self-destruction now also drops Starfrost out of your hand.

Sky Iris

Destroying your own scale to search an "Odd-Eyes" or "Magician" card pays out twice, once for the search and once for Starfrost.

The Destruction Economy

Double Iris, Purple Poison and Black Fang were already built to be destroyed. Every one of those triggers is now also a free Level 7 body.

It survives disruption

An opponent blowing up one of your scales hands you the trigger instead of taking it away. Starfrost is a reactive extender, not just a combo piece.

The Full Turn: Engine First, Classic Combo Second

The order matters. Run the Horoscope package before anything that locks you, then hand the turn over to the deck's existing opening from the Core Analysis tab.

1

Resolve the two-card engine above. Board: Starfrost Magician and Horoscope Sorcerer, both summoned from outside the Main Deck.

2

Normal Summon Performapal Skullcrobat Joker and search Wisdom-Eye Magician or whichever scale the hand is missing.

3

Link off Joker and Horoscope into Beyond the Pendulum, then pay to search your next Magician. Horoscope has already used every effect it owns, so spending it costs nothing.

4

Set your scales. Activate Wisdom-Eye Magician in the Zone to destroy itself and place a "Magician" from the Deck, which triggers a second Starfrost if you drew one.

5

Pendulum Summon into the arrows Beyond the Pendulum opened, pulling Harmonizing Magician from hand and everything the destruction chain parked face-up in the Extra Deck.

6

Harmonizing brings another "Magician" out of the Deck. Feed Double Iris Magician to a destruction effect to search a Pendulumgraph and set it.

7

Leave Horoscope Sorcerer in a Pendulum Zone if it is still available. With any "Magician" Pendulum Monster on the field its Spell negate is live on the opponent's turn.

8

End on the classic board plus one addition: Odd-Eyes Vortex Dragon, brought down by Starfrost's Quick Effect rather than a Fusion Spell.

Nibiru Insurance

Pendulum decks reach five summons faster than almost anything else, and historically had nothing on board when the rock landed. Starfrost changes the arithmetic because its Fusion is a Quick Effect that trades summons already made for a negate.

Summon What lands Nibiru status
First Horoscope Sorcerer from the hand Safe
Second Starfrost Magician from the hand Safe
Third Horoscope recalled from the face-up Extra Deck Safe
Fourth Odd-Eyes Vortex Dragon, off Starfrost's Quick Effect, shuffling the bodies you just made back into the Deck Negate live
Fifth Whatever you continue with Nibiru window, now answerable
It is insurance, not a free ride. Cashing in Starfrost for Vortex Dragon early costs you the bodies it just made, so the follow-up has to come from the classic engine. Take the trade when the hand is strong enough to rebuild, hold it when the alternative is ending on nothing.

The Sequencing Trap: Heavenly Odd-Eyes

Odd-Eyes Pendulum Dragon, Four Heavenly Dragons arrives in the same wave and is a genuinely strong scale searcher, but its lock and the Horoscope engine want opposite turn orders. Reading its text carefully is the difference between a full combo and a stalled one.

Line What you get What it costs
Scale Heavenly Odd-Eyes first A Pendulum Monster with 1500 ATK or less straight out of the Deck, so the scales assemble themselves. You cannot activate monster effects on the field until you Pendulum Summon. Horoscope's destroy effect and Skullcrobat's search are both switched off.
Horoscope engine first Two bodies, a search and a card of profit before any lock exists. Nothing, provided Heavenly Odd-Eyes stays in the scale unactivated until after your Pendulum Summon.
Both, in the wrong order Nothing worth having. You hold a hand full of extenders you are not allowed to activate, and one Pendulum Summon to fix it with.
The rule of thumb. Every monster effect you want on the field goes before the Heavenly Odd-Eyes scale effect. If you have already activated it, your only route forward is the Pendulum Summon itself, so make sure the scales and the hand are ready to cash it in.

Where It Still Falls Over

  • Horoscope needs a discard. A hand of scales and Spells with no "Magician" Pendulum, "Performapal" or "Odd-Eyes" card to pitch leaves it stranded, so it is a two-card play, not a one-card play.
  • Ash Blossom on the 2500 ATK search stops the engine dead. Horoscope is on the field, Starfrost is still in the Deck, and the destroy effect has nothing to trigger.
  • Droll & Lock Bird is still the worst card you can face. The deck searches three or four times a turn and Droll ends all of it after the first.
  • Nibiru insurance costs real resources. Cashing Starfrost in for Vortex Dragon on summon four means the bodies it made are shuffled away, and the rest of the turn has to come from elsewhere.
  • Starfrost's scale effect turns off the Pendulum Effect of whatever it places, so it cannot be used to set up Horoscope's Spell negate on the same turn.
  • None of this restores what the banlist took. Astrograph Sorcerer and Heavymetalfoes Electrumite are still Forbidden, and the new cards add consistency rather than replacing that ceiling. Check the live banlist panel below.

The Verdict

The Horoscope Package

Core, run maximum
  • The first opening the deck has ever had that costs neither the Normal Summon nor the Pendulum Summon.
  • It plugs straight into the Destruction Economy the archetype was designed around, rather than asking for a new one.
  • A Spell negate in the scale and a Quick Effect Fusion give the deck two interactions it previously had to import from generic Extra Deck monsters.
  • It raises the floor, not the ceiling. Fewer bricked hands, not a return to the pre-banlist power level.

Gagaga Magic & Cyberse Code Magician

Not this deck
  • They share the word "Magician" and nothing else. Neither is a Pendulum Monster and neither is searchable by anything you already play.
  • Both impose a lock the moment they resolve, and both locks are aimed at summon types this deck cannot give up.
  • Running either means importing a support package that competes with your scales for Main Deck slots.
  • Both are strong cards in the decks they were written for. The Onomat and Cyberse write-ups cover them properly.
