Blue-Eyes White Dragon, the White Phantom Beast

Level 8 · LIGHT · Dragon · 3000 / 2500 · a "Phantom Beast" card

The newest "Phantom Beast" card is a Blue-Eyes. "Phantom Beast" is in its name, as it is in every Mecha Phantom Beast's, so the old Phantom Beast support counts it. It isn't a "Mecha Phantom Beast", though, and the squadron's own cards can't search it, summon it or use it. What it does share with this deck is Tokens that lose battles. When one of your monsters is destroyed by battle, it can summon itself from your hand and destroy every monster your opponent controls.

Executive Summary

The Mecha Phantom Beast archetype is a squadron of WIND Machine monsters defined by their intricate token-based gameplay. Their strategy revolves around generating "Mecha Phantom Beast Tokens" which serve a triple purpose: providing potent destruction protection, modulating monster Levels for high-Rank Xyz summons, and acting as fuel for their most powerful effects. This creates a unique, resource-intensive playstyle that can pivot between defensive control and explosive offensive plays. However, the archetype's legacy is famously split between its intended "pure" strategy and its role as a host for one of the most powerful generic combo engines in the game's history via the now-banned Mecha Phantom Beast Auroradon.

Core Mechanic: The Token Economy

The entire strategy is built on the "Mecha Phantom Beast Token," a Level 3 WIND Machine. This single resource creates a strategic trilemma, forcing the pilot to constantly decide between three critical functions.

Protection

While you control a Token, all main deck Mecha Phantom Beast monsters cannot be destroyed by battle or card effects, forming a resilient defensive line.

Level Modulation

Each monster's Level is increased by the total Levels of all Tokens you control, enabling access to high-Rank Xyz monsters like Rank 7 and 10.

Effect Fuel

The most powerful effects, from searching to special summoning, require Tributing Tokens as a cost, forcing a careful balance of resources.

Key Personnel: The Flight Crew & The Aces

Success hinges on deploying the right personnel at the right time. The entire squadron works in concert, from starters that initiate plays to the aces that finish the mission.

The Flight Crew (Main Deck)

Mecha Phantom Beast Tetherwolf

The premier starter. Generates a Token on Normal Summon, setting up your entire strategy.

Mecha Phantom Beast Megaraptor

The primary searcher. Tributes a Token to add any "Mecha Phantom Beast" monster from Deck to hand.

Mecha Phantom Beast O-Lion

A crucial Tuner and combo enabler. Generates a Token when sent to the GY from any location.

The Aces (Extra Deck)

Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack

The archetype's signature Rank 7 boss. Summons Tokens and provides repeatable destruction.

Mecha Phantom Beast Auroradon (BANNED)

The infamous Link-3 monster. Its generic summoning conditions and explosive Token generation made it a format-warping engine before it was forbidden.

Ideal "Pure" Endboard

Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack

The goal for a pure Mecha Phantom Beast deck is not an unbreakable wall of negates, but a resilient, resource-generating board designed for a long game. The centerpiece is Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack, providing repeatable destruction while being protected by its own Tokens.

  • Dracossack + 2 Tokens: Provides removal and self-protection.
  • Megaraptor: Ensures follow-up plays by searching for key monsters.
  • Set Backrow: Cards like Aerial Recharge for more Tokens or a generic disruption Trap.

Competitive Debriefing

Strengths

  • High Resilience: Token-based protection makes the board difficult to clear with standard removal.
  • Xyz Toolbox: Flexible access to powerful Rank 7, 9, and 10 Xyz monsters.
  • Grind Game: Can out-resource opponents in longer duels when its engine is established.

Weaknesses

  • Profound Inconsistency: Highly prone to drawing unplayable hands without a starter.
  • Normal Summon Reliant: The entire strategy hinges on the Normal Summon, creating an obvious choke point for disruption.
  • Outdated Pace: Designed for a slower era of the game, making its setup turns vulnerable in the modern meta.

Synergies & External Engines

  • Speedroid Engine: Cards like Speedroid Terrortop provide free Special Summons, bypassing the deck's crippling reliance on its Normal Summon.
  • Symphonic Warrior Engine: The Guitaar and Miccs combo provides a crucial additional Normal Summon.
  • Generic Machine Support: As WIND Machines, they synergize with a wide range of support cards that can extend combos or provide utility.

Magnificent Maestros: A Blue-Eyes Among the Phantom Beasts

  • TCG · Magnificent Maestros · 12 Nov 2026
  • OCG · LIMIT OVER COLLECTION -THE RIVALS- · 20 Mar 2026

The name decides the archetype. "Phantom Beast" goes back to 2006 and four Beast-Warriors: Cross-Wing, Thunder-Pegasus, Rock-Lizard and Wild-Horn. Every Mecha Phantom Beast and every Mecha Phantom Beast Token has the same words in its name, so all of them are "Phantom Beast" cards too. Blue-Eyes White Dragon, the White Phantom Beast joins them the same way. This tab covers which cards count it, what it does next to a Mecha Phantom Beast board, and which squadron cards get in its way. Its home deck is covered on the Blue-Eyes page.

"Phantom Beast", Not "Mecha"

Cross-Wing, Thunder-Pegasus and Rock-Lizard read "Phantom Beast", so they count it. The squadron's cards read "Mecha Phantom Beast", so none of them do.

Tokens as Bait

Mecha Phantom Beasts can't be destroyed by battle while you control a Token, so attacks go into the Tokens. A Token destroyed by battle lets White Phantom Beast summon itself from your hand, and the wipe follows.

Nothing Here Finds It

Megaraptor and Scramble!! Scramble!! only fetch "Mecha Phantom Beast" monsters. It reaches your hand by a draw, or through cards any deck can play, like Bystial Magnamhut and The Melody of Awakening Dragon.

The Card

Blue-Eyes White Dragon, the White Phantom Beast

"Blue-Eyes" and "Phantom Beast"

Blue-Eyes White Dragon, the White Phantom Beast

Level 8 · LIGHT · Dragon · 3000 / 2500

  1. When it's added from the Deck to your hand, or a monster you control is destroyed by battle: reveal it and Special Summon it.
  2. When it's Special Summoned from the hand or Deck: destroy every monster your opponent controls. For the rest of the turn, only "Blue-Eyes" monsters can attack directly.
  3. When a card or effect that targets it on the field is activated: discard 1 card to negate that effect. No once-per-turn limit.

The first two effects are each once per turn.

Konami's rulings

From Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

A normal draw in your Draw Phase counts as adding it from the Deck. The five cards you start the Duel with don't. The summon has to come right after the add. If the add happens at Chain Link 2 or later, or anything else happens after it, the moment has passed. After a draw from an effect like Mulcharmy Fuwalos, you can still chain the summon to your opponent's next summon effect. The negate can't be used in the Damage Step.

Does It Count?

Every card and situation is listed below with its answer; tap one to see why. The answers follow the printed card text.

"Phantom Beast" cards

  • Phantom Beast Cross-Wing (in your GY) Yes

    While Cross-Wing is in the GY, every "Phantom Beast" monster on the field gains 300 ATK. White Phantom Beast goes to 3300, and every Mecha Phantom Beast and Mecha Phantom Beast Token gains 300 too. It boosts your opponent's Phantom Beasts as well. Cross-Wing isn't a Mecha Phantom Beast, so in your GY it stops Sabre Hawk from attacking.

  • Phantom Beast Thunder-Pegasus Yes

    When your opponent's monster declares an attack, banish it from your GY to keep a "Phantom Beast" monster from being destroyed by battle. It can cover White Phantom Beast. Used on a Token, the Token survives, so White Phantom Beast's summon doesn't trigger.

  • Phantom Beast Rock-Lizard Yes

    It can be Normal Summoned with 1 Tribute instead of 2 if you Tribute a "Phantom Beast" monster. White Phantom Beast counts, and so does a Mecha Phantom Beast Token. Rock-Lizard is Level 7, the same as a Level 4 Mecha Phantom Beast next to one Token, so the two can make Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack.

"Mecha Phantom Beast" cards

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Megaraptor's search No

    It Tributes a Token to add a "Mecha Phantom Beast" monster from the Deck. White Phantom Beast isn't one.

  • Scramble!! Scramble!! No

    It Special Summons "Mecha Phantom Beast" monsters from the Deck.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Harrliard or Hamstrat No

    Harrliard Special Summons a "Mecha Phantom Beast" monster from your hand, and Hamstrat revives one from your GY.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack's destroy No

    Its cost is Tributing a "Mecha Phantom Beast" monster. A Token works and White Phantom Beast doesn't. Tributing a Token isn't destruction by battle, so it doesn't trigger White Phantom Beast either.

  • The Token Level boost No

    Only Mecha Phantom Beasts with that text add the Tokens' Levels. White Phantom Beast stays Level 8, and no Mecha Phantom Beast lands on 8: a Level 4 goes to 7, 10 or 13, a Level 3 to 6, 9 or 12. So it never shares an Xyz Summon with them.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Raiten's material lock No

    After Raiten's effect, only "Mecha Phantom Beast" monsters can be Fusion, Synchro, Xyz or Link Material that turn. White Phantom Beast can't.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Sabre Hawk Works against it

    Sabre Hawk can't attack while you have a monster in your GY that isn't a "Mecha Phantom Beast". A White Phantom Beast in your GY switches it off.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Concoruda Works against it

    While it's on the field, your Tokens can't be destroyed by battle, so none of them can trigger White Phantom Beast.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Turtletracer Works against it

    The first Mecha Phantom Beast Token that would be destroyed by battle each turn isn't, so the first lost battle doesn't trigger White Phantom Beast.

  • Mecha Phantom Beast Warbluran's Token Works against it

    After it, you can only Special Summon WIND monsters for the rest of the turn. White Phantom Beast is LIGHT.

White Phantom Beast's own effects

  • A Mecha Phantom Beast Token is destroyed by battle Yes

    That's its second summon condition: any monster you control, on either player's turn, even in the Damage Step. It came from the hand, so the wipe follows.

  • A Token is Tributed for a cost No

    Megaraptor, Dracossack, Harrliard, Aerial Recharge and Do a Barrel Roll Tribute Tokens. Tributing isn't destruction by battle.

  • A Token is destroyed by a card effect No

    Only destruction by battle counts.

  • Your Mecha Phantom Beasts attack directly after the wipe No

    For the rest of that turn only "Blue-Eyes" monsters can attack directly. White Phantom Beast can; your Mecha Phantom Beasts can't.

Next to a Mecha Phantom Beast Board

The squadron's Tokens are made to be attacked. That gives White Phantom Beast's battle condition more chances than it gets in most decks.

On your opponent's turn

Most Mecha Phantom Beasts can't be destroyed by battle while you control a Token, so the Tokens are what your opponent can actually destroy in battle. When they destroy one, White Phantom Beast can summon itself from your hand, even in the Damage Step. Its wipe then clears their monsters in the middle of their Battle Phase. A Token in Defense Position costs you no LP.

The summon is once per turn, so a second Token lost that turn does nothing.

On your turn

You can start the battle yourself. Attack a stronger monster with a Token in Attack Position. The Token is destroyed and you take the difference as damage. Then White Phantom Beast comes out, wipes, and can still attack directly for 3000.

After the wipe your Mecha Phantom Beasts can't attack directly, because they aren't "Blue-Eyes" monsters. Make any attacks you want from them before the Token's battle.

Cards That Work Against It

Mecha Phantom Beast Concoruda

While it's on the field, your Tokens can't be destroyed by battle or card effects. That makes the wall tougher, but a Token that can't lose a battle never triggers White Phantom Beast.

Mecha Phantom Beast Turtletracer

The first Mecha Phantom Beast Token that would be destroyed by battle each turn isn't. A second Token has to lose a battle before White Phantom Beast can come out.

Mecha Phantom Beast Warbluran

Used for a Machine Synchro Summon, it makes a Token and locks you into WIND monsters for the rest of the turn. White Phantom Beast is LIGHT, so it can't be Special Summoned that turn.

The Blue-Eyes routes

The Guiding Flute of Summoning Dragons needs a Spellcaster on the field. After it, you can only Special Summon Dragons and Spellcasters, and Mecha Phantom Beast Tokens are Machines. Hieratic Seal of the Heavenly Spheres needs two Dragons as Link Material. Sage with Eyes of Blue and Roar of the Blue-Eyed Dragons need a Blue-Eyes engine.

Getting It Into Your Hand

The squadron has no way to find a Dragon. These routes work in any deck.

Draw it

A normal draw in your Draw Phase lets it summon itself right away (Konami FAQ). Going second, that's a wipe before your Main Phase starts. From your opening hand it has to wait for one of your monsters to lose a battle.

Bystial Magnamhut (Limited)

Going second, it summons itself on your opponent's turn by banishing a LIGHT or DARK monster from either GY. Mecha Phantom Beasts are WIND, so you rely on their GY. In that End Phase it adds a Dragon from your Deck. White Phantom Beast then summons itself and wipes before your turn, as long as the add resolves at Chain Link 1. If White Phantom Beast is already in your GY, Magnamhut can add it from there, but then it can't summon itself.

The Melody of Awakening Dragon

Discard 1 card to add up to 2 Dragons with 3000 or more ATK and 2500 or less DEF. White Phantom Beast fits exactly. The add is the last thing Melody does, so the summon and the wipe follow on your turn.

Mulcharmy Fuwalos and Purulia

With no cards on your field, they draw when your opponent summons: Fuwalos from the Deck or Extra Deck, Purulia from the hand. A drawn White Phantom Beast summons itself only if the draw was the last thing to happen. A draw while a chain is still resolving is too late.

Should a Mecha Phantom Beast Deck Play It?

Our take: one copy at most, for going second, and only without Concoruda or Warbluran. The "Phantom Beast" name doesn't help here. The three cards that read it are 2006 Beast-Warriors that Mecha Phantom Beast decks don't play, and the squadron's own support reads "Mecha Phantom Beast". No Mecha Phantom Beast is ever Level 8: with Tokens a Level 4 goes to 7, 10 or 13, and a Level 3 to 6, 9 or 12. So it never shares an Xyz Summon with them.

What carries over is the battle condition. A Token wall gets attacked, and each lost Token is a chance to wipe your opponent's monsters. That gives the squadron a board breaker it didn't have. The rest of the time it's a 3000 ATK Dragon you can't search.
