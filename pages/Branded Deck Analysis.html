Chapter I • Anime Episode 1 Dogmatika Nation

Dogmatika Ecclesia, the Virtuous — The Sanctum of Stigmata

In the holy realm of Dogmatika, citizens revere Supreme Leader Maximus and the sacred miracles granted through their divine Stigmata. Young Ecclesia is chosen as the realm’s holy Saint, blessed with an unblemished crest of divine power. Alongside her fellow knights, she defends the cathedral gates from mysterious spatial rifts torn open by the Abyss. Unbeknownst to the devoted populace, the holy relics and divine light of Dogmatika mask a dark, calculating design orchestrated from deep within the sanctum.

Chapter II • Anime Episode 2 Dogmatika Battlefront

Dogmatika Fleurdelis, the Knighted — The Shield of Divine Wrath

When colossal abyssal behemoths breach the barrier of the outer walls, Fleurdelis, the Knighted commands the front line. Donning lightning-infused relic armor and wielding twin holy blades, Fleurdelis single-handedly slays hordes of abyssal invaders. Her unwavering resolve protects Ecclesia and the citizens, yet Fleurdelis begins to harbor quiet suspicions regarding the origin of the abyssal rifts and the cold detachment of Leader Maximus.

Chapter III • Anime Episode 3 Great Sand Sea

Tri-Brigade Shuraig the Ominous Omen — The Outcast Resistance

Across the scorched, harsh expanses of the Great Sand Sea, outcasts branded as heretics by Dogmatika unite under the banner of the Tri-Brigade. Led by the stoic winged commander Shuraig the Ominous Omen, alongside Ferrijit and Rugal, the resistance utilizes mechanical weapons powered by iron and gunpowder to clash against Dogmatika’s oppressive zealots and survive in the wilderness.

Chapter IV • Anime Episode 4 The Rift Incursion

Fallen of Albaz — The Abyss Child & The Branded Exile

During a violent storm, an abyssal hole opens over Dogmatika, plummeting a mysterious young boy with zero memories—the Fallen of Albaz—to the battlefield. Ordered to execute him as an impure demon, Ecclesia chooses mercy instead, shielding him with her body. Threatened by Dogmatika knights, Albaz’s dormant dragon power detonates, fusing him into Titaniklad the Ash Dragon. Recognizing their shared plight, Ecclesia renounces her holy title, joining Albaz as exiled fugitives hunted by the church.

Chapter V • Anime Episode 5 Golgonda Desert

Tri-Brigade Kitt — Mechanical Alchemy & Springans Alliance

Wandering through the barren Golgonda Desert, Albaz and Ecclesia encounter the energetic Tri-Brigade engineer Kitt and the rambunctious mercenary gang known as the Springans (led by Captain Sargas). Kitt equips Albaz and Ecclesia with custom mechanical gear and artillery to combat both giant sand-serpents and incoming Dogmatika pursuers, forging a deep bond of friendship between the exiles and the desert scavengers.

Chapter VI • Anime Episode 6 Despia Transformation

Albion the Branded Dragon — The Despia Betrayal & Dark Theater

Supreme Leader Maximus discards his holy façade, corrupting the Dogmatika church into the demonic theater realm of Despia. Transforming into Dramaturge of Despia, he unleashes Aluber the Jester of Despia to steal Albaz’s dragon core. Forced into a violent struggle of identities, Albaz taps into the raw power of the Branded covenant, transforming into Albion the Branded Dragon to shield Ecclesia from Aluber’s malevolent flames.

Chapter VII • Extended Lore Swordsoul & Icejade Realm

Icejade Cenote & Swordsoul Sanctuary — The Iceblade Blade

Seeking sanctuary from Despia’s onslaught, Albaz and Ecclesia journey to the sacred waters of the Icejade and the snow-capped peaks of Mount Swordsoul. Guided by Supreme Sovereign Chengying and Mo Ye, Albaz harmonizes his inner dragon power with the frozen spirit essence, evolving into Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon. However, betrayal strikes from within as Swordsoul Strategist Longyuan steals the sacred flame, corrupting himself into a fiery warlord.

Chapter VIII • Saga Conclusion Abyss War Climax

The Bystial War & Albion the Sanctifire Dragon — The Light of Salvation

Aluber absorbs ancient dragon remains, giving birth to the terrifying Bystial dragons (Bystial Lubellion and Dis Pater), threatening to plunge the entire Abyss into darkness. In the final battle, supported by Fleurdelis (now Iris Swordsoul), Kitt, and the combined remnants of all allied factions, Albaz channel the purified hope of his bonds. Fusing with Ecclesia’s light, he ascends into Albion the Sanctifire Dragon, purging Despia’s malice, rescuing Ecclesia, and opening a bright new era across the land.

Executive Summary

The "Branded" archetype, interwoven with the lore of "Fallen of Albaz," represents a pinnacle of modern Yu-Gi-Oh! card design. It operates as a highly synergistic, fusion-centric strategy renowned for its consistency, recursive power, and high skill ceiling. The deck's core philosophy revolves around a non-linear approach to combo execution, enabling it to adapt to various game states and play through significant disruption. At its heart, the engine is a complex network of searchers, extenders, and powerful fusion monsters that treat the Graveyard (GY) and banished zone not as discard piles, but as active resource pools.

Anatomy of the Branded Engine

Primary Starters & Consistency Tools

The deck's success is built on consistently accessing its primary playmaker, Branded Fusion. These cards are the pathways to that goal.

Aluber the Jester of Despia

The premier one-card starter. On Normal or Special Summon, it searches any "Branded" Spell/Trap (typically Branded Fusion).

Branded Opening

A Quick-Play Spell that discards 1 to summon a "Despia" (like Aluber) from the deck. Also provides protection from the GY.

Guiding Quem, the Virtuous

A key starter and extender. On summon, sends Fallen of Albaz (or a card mentioning it) from Deck to GY. Revives monsters when a card leaves the Extra Deck.

Springans Kitt

An alternative searcher for "Branded" S/T. Can Special Summon itself from the hand, making it a great extender that doesn't use your Normal Summon.

The Fusion Powerhouses: Key Spells

Branded Fusion

The single most powerful card in the deck. Fuses using monsters from your Deck as material, setting up your GY and board simultaneously.

Branded in Red

A Quick-Play Spell for fusing on the opponent's turn. Recycles a "Despia" or Albaz from GY and enables devastating Guardian Chimera plays.

The Extra Deck Toolbox: The Albaz Pantheon

The Extra Deck is a toolbox where monsters are not just end goals, but often intermediary combo pieces. The pantheon is led by its chief boss monster, supported by crucial enablers.

Boss Monster

Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon

The quintessential boss monster. It provides a non-targeting banish as a Quick Effect (by sending an "Albaz" fusion from Extra Deck to GY as cost) and nukes the opponent's board with a powerful lingering effect if it leaves the field by an opponent's card.

Core Enablers & Support

Combo Enabler

Lubellion the Searing Dragon

The key combo piece summoned by Branded Fusion. It recycles materials from the GY/banished zone to fuse into Mirrorjade.

Extender / Follow-up

Albion the Branded Dragon

A vital extender. Fuses by banishing materials. Its End Phase GY effect sets a "Branded" S/T (like Branded in Red) for follow-up.

Utility / Setup

Granguignol the Dusk Dragon

A utility bridge that sends a Lvl 6+ LIGHT/DARK from Deck/Extra Deck to GY on summon, setting up other resources like Bystial Saronir.

The Grind Game: Recursion Engine

The deck's long-term power comes from its ability to out-resource the opponent with a web of recursive effects.

Blazing Cartesia, the Virtuous

A key recursive piece. Special Summons itself and provides a Quick Effect Fusion. Adds itself back to hand in the End Phase.

The Bystial Lubellion

Searches other Bystials and places a "Branded" Continuous S/T (like Branded Regained) from Deck to field, starting the draw engine.

Analysis of New Support

New Support

The Fallen & The Virtuous

A versatile Quick-Play Spell that serves as searchable removal. It sends an "Albaz" Fusion from the Extra Deck to the GY (triggering its effect) to destroy a face-up card. It can also revive a monster if Ecclesia is on field/GY, making it an incredible utility card for both offense and defense.

New Support

The Dragon that Devours the Dogma

A new apex boss monster. On summon, it shuffles GY/banished cards back into the Deck, providing key disruption against resource-looping decks. While Ecclesia is on field/GY, it gains 500 ATK and becomes *unaffected* by other cards' effects, creating a formidable "towers" that many decks cannot out.

Core Combo Lines

Foundational Combo: The "Branded Fusion" Resolution

This is the deck's "bread and butter" play, turning one card into a full board presence and follow-up.

  1. Activate Branded Fusion. Send Fallen of Albaz + Despian Tragedy to GY to summon Lubellion the Searing Dragon.
  2. Activate effects. Chain Link 1: Lubellion. Chain Link 2: Tragedy (to "chain block" Ash Blossom).
  3. Chain resolves: Tragedy searches Ad Libitum of Despia. Lubellion discards 1 card to fuse itself and Albaz from GY back into the Deck.
  4. Summon Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon.

Result: Mirrorjade on field (a banish) and Ad Libitum in hand for a follow-up Branded in Red play.

Bystial-Enhanced Combo: Resilient Board

This line incorporates the Bystial engine to build a more resource-rich board with multiple interruptions.

  1. Activate Branded Fusion. Send Fallen of Albaz + The Bystial Lubellion to GY to summon Albion the Branded Dragon.
  2. Activate Albion's effect. Banish Albaz (GY) and Aluber (Hand/Field) to summon Lubellion the Searing Dragon.
  3. Activate Lubellion's effect. Discard 1, shuffle banished Albaz and on-field Lubellion to summon Mirrorjade.
  4. Tribute Albion to Special Summon The Bystial Lubellion from GY.
  5. Activate The Bystial Lubellion's on-field effect to place Branded Regained from Deck to field.
  6. Go to End Phase. Albion's GY effect triggers. Set Branded in Red from Deck.

Result: Mirrorjade, The Bystial Lubellion, Branded Regained, and a Set Branded in Red. This provides a banish, a pop/draw, a continuous draw engine, and potential GY disruption.

The Endboard Blueprint

Standard Endboard

  • Mirrorjade: 1 non-targeting banish (Quick Effect).
  • Set Branded in Red: Summons Guardian Chimera on opponent's turn.
  • Guardian Chimera: Destroys 1-2 cards and draws 1-2 cards.
  • Revived Mirrorjade: Ad Libitum (used for Chimera) revives Mirrorjade, allowing a 2nd banish.

Bystial-Enhanced Board

  • All Standard Endboard interruptions.
  • Branded Regained: Provides card draw whenever a LIGHT/DARK is banished (e.g., by your own Bystials).
  • Branded Beast (Alternative): Provides targeted destruction by Tributing a Dragon.
  • Bystials in Hand: Magnamhut / Druiswurm provide GY hate and on-field removal.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • High Consistency: Numerous 1-card starters and extenders.
  • Powerful Grind Game: Unmatched recursion; can out-resource almost any deck.
  • Non-Linear: Can pivot to different combo lines when interrupted.
  • Good Going Second: Engine cards like Super Poly, Albaz, and Fallen & Virtuous are excellent board breakers.

Weaknesses

  • Choke Points: Branded Fusion is a critical chokepoint. A well-timed Ash Blossom can be devastating without an extender.
  • GY Hate: Relies heavily on the GY. Cards like Dimension Shifter or opposing Bystials can be crippling.
  • Floodgates: Can struggle against Macro Cosmos, Anti-Spell Fragrance, or cards that lock out Fusions.

Hybrids & Tech Cards

  • Branded Chimera: A potent hybrid where Branded Fusion sends Gazelle to start the Chimera engine.
  • Dogmatika: Nadir Servant provides an alternative, powerful normal summon and engine.
  • Bystials: Considered almost core. Magnamhut, Saronir, and Druiswurm provide free bodies and disruption.
  • Super Polymerization: A powerful, unchainable board breaker the deck can easily run.
