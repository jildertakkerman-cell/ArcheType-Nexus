Call of the Haunted

Archetype Analysis • 2026 Format

Zombie Pivot Legacy Retrain Trap Engine

Lore Archive

"The graveyard stirs. Call of the Haunted is among the most iconic resurrection spells ever inscribed, a Continuous Trap that reaches into the realm of the dead and drags a fallen warrior back to the battlefield. Its legacy spans decades of competitive play, a testament to the eternal truth that in this game, death is never permanent."

Executive Summary

The 2026 Maze of Muertos expansion has successfully retrofitted the iconic trap card, Call of the Haunted, into the engine of a sophisticated Zombie strategy. Drawing heavy inspiration from the "Bonz" character of the Duelist Kingdom era, this archetype modernizes the "set-and-pass" obsolescence of 2002 into a high-octane 2026 engine.

By turning the trap card into a pseudo-Quick-Play spell via Pumpking the King of Grave Ghosts, the deck bridges the gap between GY recursion and board breaking. For a full list of the restless dead involved in this strategy, you should consult the Tomes of the Departed in the official database.

The "One-Card" Pumpking Sequence

Minimal Committment

  1. 01. Reveal Pumpking in hand. Set Call of the Haunted from Deck.
  2. 02. Activate the Set Trap immediately. Revive Pumpking from GY (discarded as cost).
  3. 03. Pumpking Effect: Special Summon Changshi the Spiridao from Deck.
  4. 04. Changshi dumps Glow-Up Bloom. Bloom banishes to add Stare of the Snake Hair.
  5. 05. Overlay Pumpking + Changshi -> Pumpking the Great Ghost King.
  6. 06. Ghost King Effect: Add Call of the Forgotten. Activate Field Spell -> Set 3 Traps.
  7. RESULT: Rank-Up into Undying Legion. 3 Set Traps. Field Spell active.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • ▪ Interaction Bypass: Activating traps the turn they are set bypasses "Turn 0" hand traps that target monster effects.
  • ▪ Material Locking: Stare of the Snake Hair creates "dead zones" on the opponent's board, effectively countering Link strategies.
  • ▪ Resource Loop: Army of the Haunted creates an infinite grind game by constantly resetting traps.

Weaknesses

  • ▪ Backrow Hate: Harpie's Feather Duster is devastating if the Ghost King is not established.
  • ▪ Bystials: Because the strategy relies on reviving DARK/Zombies from GY, Bystials can banish key targets in response to "Call of the Haunted".
  • ▪ Time Rules: The grind nature of the deck can lead to losses in time due to LP deficits if not careful.

Synergies

  • ▪ Eldlich: Natural synergy. Eldlich sends the Continuous Traps to GY for cost; Army resets them.
  • ▪ Vampires: Perfect Level 6 synergy facilitates Rank 6 plays without consuming the Normal Summon.
  • ▪ Ryzeal Matchup: Favorable. Trap-based removal bypasses Ryzeal's monster-heavy protection.
