Lore Archive

"The Dododo warriors are a tribe of ancient brutes who compensate for their simplicity with overwhelming physical power. Dododo Warrior, Buster, and Witch form a straightforward but effective core that enables quick Rank 6 Xyz Summons. Their philosophy is refreshingly direct: hit first, hit hardest, ask questions never."

Executive Summary

The Dododo archetype, in its modern competitive form, is not a standalone strategy. Instead, it functions as a highly efficient, one-card engine for generating Rank 4 Xyz monsters. Its primary role is to act as the foundational resource that pivots the deck into a sophisticated control strategy, enabled by its seamless integration into the broader Onomat family?now perfected by Onomatokage?and the powerful new Ryzeal Xyz engine.

The Dododo Core & Onomat Bridge

The deck's strength comes from how Dododo starters efficiently build a board, which is then extended by the Onomat support cards to enable the final Ryzeal control pieces.

The Dododo Foundation

Dodododo Warrior

The flagship starter. A one-card combo (thanks to Onomatokage) that results in two Level 4 monsters on the field and sets up the Graveyard for further plays.

Dododo Witch

A potent extender that can Special Summon another "Dododo" monster from the hand, searchable by Reinforcement of the Army.

Dodododwarf Gogogoglove

A previous extender that has been almost completely superseded by Onomatokage, which performs the same role from the hand or GY with more flexibility and searchability.

The Onomat Bridge

Onomatokage

Onomatokage (NEW)

The transformative new lynchpin. It's treated as all 4 Onomat archetypes, making it universally searchable. It Special Summons itself from the hand or GY, making it the deck's best extender and the core of the new 1-card combos. It also recycles Xyz monsters from the GY.

Zubababancho Gagagacoat

A critical extender that Special Summons itself and revives a Dododo or Gogogo monster, enabling high-material Xyz Summons.

The Xyz Lock

A key strategic challenge. Cards like Gagagacoat lock you into only summoning Xyz monsters from the Extra Deck. The main combo is built to work around this, using a generic Link monster before the lock is applied.

  • Persistent Disruption: A Quick Effect to detach a material and destroy one card on the field. With optimal material stacking, this can provide up to 4 instances of destruction.
  • Board Protection: Can detach a material to protect any of your Xyz monsters from being destroyed.

Proactive Hand Reduction

2 Cards Removed

  • Guaranteed Hand Rips: The combo sequence guarantees two mandatory hand rips via Evilswarm Ouroboros, crippling the opponent's resources before they can play.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • High Consistency: Powerful 1-card starters (Warrior->Kage) and multiple search spells.
  • Nibiru Resilience: The combo can be structured to play around Nibiru by setting up a negate or key piece before the 5th summon.
  • High Disruption Density: Can end on 7+ interactions, including hand rips.
  • Great Grind Game: Onomatokage's 2nd effect recycles Xyz monsters, fixing the deck's old weakness of running out of resources.

Weaknesses

  • Droll & Lock Bird: The deck's reliance on multiple searches makes it very vulnerable to Droll.
  • Choke Points: The initial search spells and the effect of Ryzeal Duo Drive are key choke points that can be negated.
  • Extra Deck Intensive: The combo requires many specific Extra Deck monsters, leaving little room for generic utility.

Key Tech & Support

  • Onomatopaira: A powerful search spell. Its 'cost' of discarding one card is turned into a combo-enabler by discarding Onomatokage, which can then Special Summon itself from the GY.
  • Onomatopickup: A field spell that directly searches for a core combo piece, including the new Onomatokage.
  • Utopia Package: While not the main focus, cards like Utopia Double can provide a fast OTK option.

Gagaga Magician
— Gagaga Magic

Yuma's signature Spellcaster returns as a retrain that never touches the field the slow way. It summons itself and a partner out of the hand or graveyard, matches their Levels for you, and puts an Xyz Monster on the board before your opponent has a chance to answer the Normal Summon that used to be the whole plan.

The Dododo half of the Onomat family always had the bodies and never the tempo. Level 6 monsters sat in hand waiting for tributes, and every line ran through a single Normal Summon. Gagaga Magic removes both problems from one card, and its partner from the same set finally gives the deck something to do on the opponent's turn.

Gagaga Magician - Gagaga Magic

Gagaga Magician - Gagaga Magic
Level 4 Dark Spellcaster 1500 / 1000 MAMO-EN011
It starts in the hand

No Normal Summon, no field presence required. Your Normal Summon stays free for Gogogo Goblindbergh or a second line entirely.

The partner can come from the graveyard

Anything the deck already threw away is live again. A discarded Dododo Buster, a used Onomatokage, or a monster your opponent just negated all count.

Level matching is built in

Treat one Level as the other's, in either direction. Pair it with a Level 6 and you get Rank 6. Pair it with a Level 3 or 4 and you get Rank 4. Dododo Driver and Onomatopickup are no longer needed to bridge the gap.

The catch

Hard once per turn, and the Xyz Summon is compulsory and restricted to nine name families. Extra copies past the first are dead weight.

Trigger

Any Extra Deck Special Summon by your opponent, which in practice means their first real play of the turn.

No name restriction on the Xyz

Unlike Gagaga Magic, this one can climb into any Xyz Monster. A leftover Level 4 becomes Number 41: Bagooska, Abyss Dweller or Tornado Dragon mid-combo.

Detach payoff

Whenever it is detached to pay for an Xyz effect, drop an opponent monster to 0 ATK. It is a free rider on cards you were detaching anyway.

The catch

It does nothing against decks that do not touch the Extra Deck, and it needs a surviving face-up monster to pair with. Dead in the opening hand going first.

What Actually Changes

Normal Summon freed

The old lines all bottlenecked on one summon that Ash Blossom or Infinite Impermanence could end. Gagaga Magic runs entirely from the hand.

Bricks become starters

Level 6 Dododo monsters no longer need tribute fodder. Discard one, revive it, and you are on a Rank 6 in two effects.

Negation insurance

If a starter on the field eats a negate, Gagaga Magic revives it out of the graveyard later and turns the loss into an Xyz Summon.

Xyz only, all turn

The enablers around it lock the Extra Deck to Xyz Monsters, so no Link or Synchro escape hatch once the line starts.

How the Build Shifts

Gagaga Magic takes the Normal Summon off the critical path, so the Level modulators and the spare Normal Summon bodies lose their jobs: Dododo Driver, Dododo Witch, Gagagatag and the original Gagaga Magician all come out. Dodododraw goes to three, because the discard is now a summon. This is the 40 that replaces them.

Gagaga Magician - Gagaga Magic

Main Deck staple
  • Run three. It is searchable off both Onomat spells and never asks for the Normal Summon.
  • The graveyard clause means it stays live in the mid-game, long after the opening hand is gone.
  • Hard once per turn on the name, so the second copy in hand is only a discard for Onomatopaira.

Gagaga Girl - Cell Phone Subtraction

Situational tech
  • One copy at most. It is the deck's first genuine opponent-turn interaction, but only against Extra Deck decks.
  • Its Xyz Summon has no name restriction, so it reaches generic Rank 4 answers that Gagaga Magic legally cannot.

Where It Still Falls Over

  • Graveyard hate ends it. Dimension Shifter or a resolved Macro Cosmos removes the revival half of the card, and an opposing Abyss Dweller shuts the same window.
  • Droll & Lock Bird is worse than ever. The long line chains five searches. Gagaga Magic does not search, but everything feeding it does.
  • It needs a partner. Opening it with no other Onomat monster in hand or graveyard leaves a vanilla Level 4 Spellcaster.
  • No Link outs. Dodododo Warrior, Utopic Onomatopoeia and Zubababancho Gagagacoat all lock the Extra Deck to Xyz Monsters, so S:P Little Knight and friends stay in the Extra Deck.
  • The board is bodies, not negates. Even the long line ends on one interruption. Against a deck that plays through Draco Future, there is no second layer.
