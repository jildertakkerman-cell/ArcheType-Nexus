Small, adorable, and absolutely essential—the ultimate guardian angel fits in the palm of your hand."

Executive Summary (Classic)

The Kuriboh archetype is a fascinating case study in evolution, originating from a single, iconic monster into a sprawling family. Unlike traditional archetypes, Kuriboh functions as a versatile, Level 1 swarm engine. Its modern strategy isn't to win with its own members, but to flood the field with materials for powerful, generic Extra Deck monsters. While rooted in nostalgia, its contemporary use is a casual-to-rogue combo deck capable of producing formidable boards, though it can be hampered by inconsistency.

The Kuriboh Roster: Key Players

The Core Swarm Engine

The Kuriboh Brothers

Kuribah, Kuribee, Kuriboo, and Kuribeh, alongside the original, form a five-part engine that is the heart of the deck's ability to generate a critical mass of monsters.

Kuribabylon

The nominal boss of the Brothers engine. It can be Special Summoned from the hand and has a powerful effect to return itself to the hand to Special Summon all five original Brothers from the hand and/or Graveyard.

Primary Enablers: Spells

Five Star Twilight

Arguably the most important combo starter. It lets you Tribute a Level 5 monster to Special Summon all five Kuriboh Brothers from your hand, Deck, and/or Graveyard.

Multiply

A classic Spell that Tributes one Kuriboh to Special Summon five "Kuriboh Tokens," providing five materials for Link Summoning.

The Flute of Summoning Kuriboh

A versatile Quick-Play Spell that can add or Special Summon a Kuriboh, making it a flexible combo piece or defensive tool.

New Card Spotlight: Kuriboh - Multiply! & Sabatiel LV10

Limit Over Collection: The Heroes handed the fluffballs their first genuinely proactive cards. Kuriboh - Multiply! compresses the old Kuriboh-plus-Multiply pairing into a single Level 1 body that summons itself out of the hand and tutors from the archetype's own stat line. Winged Kuriboh Sabatiel LV10 turns a famously unplayable Level 10 into a hand-revealed Fusion tutor and an opponent-turn burn finisher. Both are 300 ATK / 200 DEF, and that detail is what ties them together.

One card, two names. The TCG prints it as Kuriboh - Multiply!, while the Japanese OCG original and its early fan translations call it Multiplying Kuriboh! They are the same card.

What Actually Changes

Turn zero with an empty board

The old Multiply needed a face-up Kuriboh to Tribute, so it was dead in the opening hand. This summons itself for free.

300/200 became a toolbox

The archetype's signature stat line is now a search key. Twenty-plus Kuriboh monsters read off one clause, from the Deck or the Graveyard.

The two cards find each other

Sabatiel LV10 is itself 300/200, so Kuriboh - Multiply! can pull it straight out of the Deck. That is the whole engine in two cards.

Winged Kuriboh LV10 is playable

No Transcendent Wings, no three-card investment. Sabatiel reaches the board off a Life Point total instead.

The HERO lock fights the deck

Sabatiel's search bans every non-HERO Extra Deck summon for the turn, which is exactly the Link climbing and Rank 1 Xyz the deck lives on.

Half your Life Points, twice

Getting under 1000 LP for the burn takes two payments. A stall deck with no negates spends that turn one bad read from losing.

The 300/200 Club

Everything Kuriboh - Multiply! can add to hand or Special Summon, from Deck or Graveyard. The clause reads on the exact stat line, not on the "Kuriboh" name, so a few non-Kuriboh bodies sneak in and a few Kuribohs are locked out.

Strategic Combo Lines

The core interaction needs nothing but Kuriboh - Multiply! in hand and an opponent who wants to do something. Everything below happens on their turn, from an empty board.

Starting position Kuriboh - Multiply! in hand  ·  empty field  ·  Normal Summon untouched
Effect one Opponent declares an attack or activates a monster effect, so Special Summon Kuriboh - Multiply! from the hand
Effect two · their next on-field monster effect Special Summon Winged Kuriboh Sabatiel LV10 straight out of the Deck, because it is 300 ATK / 200 DEF
Result Two monsters on an empty board, zero cards spent past the one you dropped, and that opponent monster sitting at 0 ATK
What you did not pay Summoning Sabatiel this way skips both of its own drawbacks: no half-LP cost, and no HERO lock, because the lock lives on Sabatiel's first effect and you never activated it

The Level 1 half is also Linkuriboh fodder, so the same two bodies can become a Link plus a Level 10 instead.

Sabatiel's second effect turns an opponent's best board against them, but only under 1000 Life Points. Getting there is the actual puzzle, and the two cards solve it together.

1

Reveal Sabatiel, pay half your LP, tutor a Fusion Spell. You drop to 4000 and Sabatiel shuffles itself back into the Deck.

2

Repeat it a turn later with a second copy or a fresh draw, and you are at 2000. Battle damage or the End Phase hit from Where Arf Thou? covers the last stretch.

3

Use Kuriboh - Multiply!'s second effect to add Sabatiel back from the Deck to your hand. This is the clause that makes the loop work.

4

On the opponent's turn, at 1000 LP or less, Special Summon Sabatiel and burn for their highest ATK monster. Against a 3000 ATK boss that is 3000 damage for free.

Read Where Arf Thou? carefully. Its 2000 damage lands in the End Phase, and only if you never Normal Summoned the monster you searched. Kuriboh - Multiply! wants to be Special Summoned from the hand on their turn, so you rarely dodge it. That is a clean way down to 1000 Life Points, and a loss if you were already at 2000.

Kuriboh - Multiply!

Play three
  • Never a dead draw. Worst case it is a Level 1 DARK Fiend for Linkuriboh, Allure of Darkness or a Rank 1 overlay.
  • Easy to find. Where Arf Thou? takes any Level 1, Generation Next takes any Kuriboh under the Life Point gap, and One for One puts it straight on the field.
  • Hold it for their second monster effect where you can. Effect one baits, effect two is where the card advantage lives.
  • It names Dark Magician outright, which is the cheapest bridge this archetype has ever had into a real boss monster.

Winged Kuriboh Sabatiel LV10

Play one
  • One copy is enough because it shuffles itself back into the Deck, and Kuriboh - Multiply! can fetch it again.
  • In a pure build, treat its first effect as emergency-only. The HERO lock switches off your Extra Deck for the turn.
  • Its real job in Kuriboh is effect two. Summoning it off Kuriboh - Multiply! gets the body with none of the strings.
  • If you are running the HERO hybrid below, the lock costs nothing and it becomes a three-of instead.

Where It Still Falls Over

The HERO lock is a cage. Pure Kuriboh wins through generic Link climbing and Rank 1 Xyz. Sabatiel's search turns all of that off for the turn.
Fusion Spells brick. Tutoring a Polymerization means main-decking Fusion targets, in an archetype that already opens badly.
Low LP with no negates. Halving twice leaves you at 2000 behind a board that interrupts nothing. Any removal that ignores battle just wins.
Attack replays. Summoning a monster mid-Battle Phase forces a replay, so the opponent can redirect the attack or simply stop, dodging the ATK-to-0.
Effect two is picky. It wants a monster effect activated on the field. A hand trap or a Graveyard effect does not switch it on.

Earlier Analysis · The Darkuriboh Paradigm

Strategic Evolution: The Darkuriboh Paradigm

The release of the promotional "Darkuriboh" and the Limit Over Collection support has fundamentally redesigned the archetype. It has transformed from a passive defensive suite into a highly proactive, resource-generating Fusion engine.

The new strategy bridges multiple distinct competitive archetypes, introducing sophisticated layers of targeting protection, un-negatable effect resolution, and consistent access to "Polymerization". This is no longer just a stall deck—it is a resilient control and combo-enabler strategy.

The New Trinity: Core Engines

Darkuriboh

Darkuriboh

The ultimate protector. Discard to prevent targeting and ensure effects resolve.

  • Protects Fiends/Fusions from targeting.
  • Prevents effect negation.
  • Searches "Polymerization" if sent to GY by card effect (e.g. Fusion Material).
Kuriboh - Multiply!

Kuriboh - Multiply!

Dynamic swarm and toolbox. Special summons itself on opponent's action.

  • SS from hand when opponent attacks/activates effect.
  • Searches/SS "Dark Magician" or any 300/200 monster.
  • Changes opponent's monster ATK to 0.
Winged Kuriboh Sabatiel LV10

Sabatiel LV10

High-risk Fusion economy and burn damage.

  • Pay half LP to search "Polymerization" or "Fusion" spell.
  • SS itself if LP < 1000 to inflict massive burn damage equal to highest ATK on field.

The Burn Finisher
