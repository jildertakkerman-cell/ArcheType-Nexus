Executive Summary

With the release of "Burst Protocol" (2026), the "Radiant Typhoon" archetype has evolved from a niche stun strategy into a robust mid-range contender. By leveraging the new Link Monster "Radiant Typhoon Varuroon, the Marine Eidolon" and the Quick-Play extender "Radiant Typhoon Ascendance", the deck now boasts consistent resource recursion and non-linear interaction. It continues to weaponize "Mystical Space Typhoon", transforming it from simple removal into a sophisticated engine starter.

The Core Components (2026 Update)

The Engine

Swen: The primary Normal Summon; searches Ascendance to begin the combo.

Ascendance: New Quick-Play from BPRO. Revives a monster or searches MST; loops itself when destroyed.

MST: The fuel. Triggers Mandate and destroys your own Ascendance for "free" advantage.

The Extra Deck Boss

Varuroon, the Marine Eidolon: The new Link-2 centerpiece. It searches MST on summon, converts opponent's monsters into Continuous Spells (non-destruction removal), and places Mandate directly from Deck.

The Main Deck Power

Varuroon, the Vibrant Vortex: Negates monster effects when Quick-Plays are used. Can destroy cards if you have 2+ MST in GY.

Radiant Typhoon Mandate: Continuous Trap. Negates opponent's cards whenever an MST is activated.

Core Combo: The 1.5 Card Opening

The standard play for the "Burst Protocol" era relies on opening Swen + any discard (or just Swen if going second).

Competitive Analysis (Post-BPRO)

Strengths

  • Non-Destruction Removal: Varuroon (Link) turns opponent's monsters into Continuous Spells, bypassing floating effects.
  • Infinite Resources: Ascendance loops itself, ensuring you never run out of grind power.
  • Consistency: Redundant searchers mean access to MST is nearly guaranteed.

Weaknesses

  • Banishment: If Ascendance is banished instead of destroyed, the resource loop breaks.
  • Key Loss: The banning of Harpie's Feather Storm removes the deck's easiest "auto-win" button.
  • Anti-Spell Fragrance: Shuts down the entire Quick-Play engine.

Hybrid Builds

  • Runick: The synergy remains strong; Runick spells trigger Varuroon's Mandate placement.
  • Fiendsmith: Provides free bodies to Link Climb into Varuroon before using your Normal Summon.
  • Dracotail: A grind-focused variant that benefits from Typhoon's backrow removal.
