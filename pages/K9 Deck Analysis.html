Executive Summary: The Paradigm Shift

The introduction of K9-X "Ripper/M" in the Burst Protocol expansion represents a paradigm shift for the K9 archetype. Historically a reactive engine reliant on the fragile "Quinquery Lock" (Plan A), the deck has evolved into a high-velocity, interactive powerhouse centered on "Plan B": Reactive Annihilation.

With "Ripper/M" acting as a 0-Point boss monster in the Genesys Format, the deck can now deploy a non-targeting board wipe that negates monster effects, pierces through defense, and shuffles resources—all while leaving room for maximum "Power Staples."

Protocol: "Plan B" Execution

The "Double Interruption" Sequence

This interaction condenses destruction, GY hate, and an omni-negate board wipe into a single Spell activation window.

K9-17 "Ripper"
1

The Setup

End your turn with a Rank 5 (e.g., K9-17 "Ripper") with K9-ØØ Lupis attached as material. Set K9-X Forced Release.

K9-X Forced Release
2

Activate & Destroy

On opponent's turn, activate Forced Release. Summon K9-X "Ripper/M" using the Rank 5. Destroy 1 card immediately upon resolution.

K9-X "Ripper/M"
3

Shuffle & Nuke

1. Ripper/M triggers: Shuffle 2 opponent's GY/Banished cards into deck.
2. Quick Effect: When opponent activates a monster effect, detach 2 materials to negate it and destroy ALL cards on the field.

Hybrids & Synergies

Vanquish Soul K9

The "Simultaneous Destruction" Loop.

  • The Interaction: Use Vanquish Soul, Start! (or similar protection) when Ripper/M nukes the field.
  • The Result: The board wipe resolves, destroying all opponent's cards, but your VS monsters survive due to the protection applying simultaneously.
  • Status: YCS Bologna Winner (Pierre Burgals).
