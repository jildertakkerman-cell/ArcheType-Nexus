The Predatory Philosophy

The Predaplant archetype thrives on a dual strategy of board control and explosive Fusion Summoning. Thematically designed around carnivorous plants, the deck weakens and marks opposing monsters with its signature Predator Counters before "consuming" them as materials for powerful DARK Fusion Monsters. This interplay of control and aggression defines the archetype's core gameplay loop and strategic identity. You can almost feel the vines coiling around your opponent's monsters.

Core Mechanics

Predator Counters

Placing a Predator Counter on a monster reduces its Level to 1, disrupting Synchro and Xyz plays. More importantly, it marks them as targets to be tributed or used as Fusion Material by your Predaplant effects.

Fusion Summoning Engine

The ultimate goal is to summon powerful DARK Fusion monsters. Cards like Predaplant Chlamydosundew can even use your opponent's monsters with Predator Counters as material, turning their board into your advantage.

The Blazing Dominion Paradigm Shift

Historically, the "Predaplant" archetype operated as a fragile, strictly linear combo deck notoriously reliant on the Normal Summon of Ophrys Scorpio. Following the widespread prohibition of Verte Anaconda, the strategy struggled to maintain a cohesive identity.

The release of the Blazing Dominion (BLZD) core set in early 2026 fundamentally re-engineered the archetype. By introducing new Tuners, Extenders, and Pendulum-Fusion hybrids, the strategy transitioned from a brittle Fusion-centric build into a highly resilient, mid-range Synchro-Fusion hybrid.

Battles of Legend: Thorns of Violet Poison

The Pure OTK & Burn Paradigm Shift

The release of the continuous-impact Normal Spell "Thorns of Violet Poison" (initially translated as "Thorn Fangs of Violet Poison") in Battles of Legend: Glorious Gallery completely redefines the Predaplant strategy!

Instead of playing a slow game of resource attrition and wearing your opponent down over time, this card transforms Predaplant into a lightning-fast One-Turn Kill (OTK) powerhouse. By summoning Starving Venom Fusion Dragon and letting it grow past one of their Special Summoned monsters, you can wipe every weaker monster from their board and deal burn damage based on their original Attack values—often winning the duel in a single move!

Card Breakdown & Simple Rules

Understanding "Thorns of Violet Poison" in Simple Terms

Thorns of Violet Poison
Normal Spell Card
TCG Set: Battles of Legend: Glorious Gallery
(OCG: Premium Pack 2026)

Official Rules Text (Simplified):

"Target 1 'Starving Venom Fusion Dragon' you control; destroy as many monsters your opponent controls with lower ATK as possible, then you can discard your entire hand, and if you do, inflict damage to your opponent equal to the destroyed monsters' total original ATK, also the targeted monster cannot attack this turn."

Three Key Rules Explained for Beginners:

1

Super Easy to Search (Rule Clause): The card has a rule stating: "(This card is always treated as a 'Predap' card.)" This means it behaves exactly like a Predaplant card. You can search for it directly from your deck using monsters like Ambulomelides or Longinephila, so you always have it ready!

2

The "Then" Board Wipe (Resolution): The spell destroys all weaker enemy monsters first. If (and only if) at least one opposing monster is successfully destroyed, you can then discard your hand to deal damage. If your opponent protects their monsters so they aren't destroyed, you don't discard any cards!

3

Full Damage Payoffs (Original ATK): The burn damage deals points equal to the destroyed monsters' original Attack printed on their cards. So even if their current Attack was reduced to 0 by your card effects, you still inflict their full, maximum original damage directly to your opponent's Life Points!

Thorns of Violet Poison targets a monster named exactly "Starving Venom Fusion Dragon" in your Monster Zone. The newer Starving Venom dragons have names of their own, so in the TCG only one card is a legal target. Here's where each dragon fits:

Resource Management & Safety Shields

Graveyard Synergy & Going-First Tactics

Turning the Hand Discard into an Advantage:

Discarding your entire hand sounds risky, but the Predaplant deck turns this into a massive advantage! You want to discard cards that have effects in the Graveyard. For example, discarding Predaplant Byblisp instantly triggers it in the Graveyard to search for another monster, immediately replenishing your hand! Other discarded cards like Longinephila or Predaplanning can be banished from the GY to place counters or disrupt your opponent on their turn.

Board Insulation (Protecting Your Plays):

Predaplant Daredevil

Predaplant Daredevil (Level 6): Summoned easily using Longinephila. In a Main Phase of the turn it's Special Summoned, it destroys Spells and Traps, up to the number of monsters with a Predator Counter. Use Daredevil to sweep backrow clean before activating your win condition! Full breakdown in the Daredevil tab

Predaplant Bal Teah

Predaplant Bal Teah (Level 4 Synchro): Actively protects your plays by Special Summoning your dragons back from the Graveyard if they are negated or destroyed. It also lets you Fusion Summon instantly on your opponent's turn to dodge target removals!

Going-First Side Deck Tactics:

In competitive tournament play, if you win Game 1, your opponent will typically force you to go first in Game 2. Going first renders your OTK Spell card useless since the opponent will not control any monsters to destroy.

In this scenario, pivot your deck into a defensive, oppressive control board! Side out a few OTK combo extenders and focus on establishing a defensive wall centered around Predaplant Dragostapelia and Starving Venom Wing Dragon to distribute counters, negate opposing monster effects, and seal the game!

New card Beyond the Brave

Predaplant Daredevil: The Plant That Eats Protected Bosses

Predaplants have always turned opposing monsters into Level 1 targets. Daredevil is the first Predaplant that cashes those counters in for Spell and Trap removal. You can Fusion Summon it like any Fusion Monster, or summon it straight from the Extra Deck by Tributing a monster with a Predator Counter, yours or your opponent's, together with a "Predaplant" you control. It's a going-second and opponent's-turn card. Going first, with no counters and no enemy backrow, it's a 2200 ATK body and Fusion material.

In plain words

  • Two ways in. Fuse a "Predaplant" with any Level 1 monster, or skip the Spell and Tribute a monster with a Predator Counter (either field) together with a "Predaplant" you control.
  • The Tribute isn't an effect. It doesn't target and can't be chained to, so "cannot be targeted" and effect negation don't stop it. Cards that negate a Summon still do.
  • One Spell/Trap per counter. In a Main Phase of the turn it was Special Summoned, yours or your opponent's, it destroys up to one Spell/Trap for each monster with a Predator Counter, yours included.

Super Polymerization Synergy

Super Polymerization uses monsters from both fields, and nobody can respond to it. Daredevil's second material only has to be a Level 1 monster, so once a Predator Counter is on it, almost any opposing monster qualifies. It's a Quick-Play Spell, which also makes it the easiest way to get Daredevil out on your opponent's turn, when its effect hits their backrow.

  1. Make it Level 1A Predator Counter turns a Level 2 or higher monster into Level 1 for as long as the counter stays. Monsters that are Level 1 already need nothing.
  2. Fuse itDiscard 1 and fuse it with your "Predaplant". Super Polymerization doesn't target, and no one can chain to it.
  3. Count the restDaredevil was Special Summoned this turn, so in that Main Phase it destroys a Spell/Trap for every other monster with a counter.

Daredevil in Branded Predaplant

Branded Predaplant keeps its Branded Fusion, Mirrorjade and Branded Banishment package. Daredevil fits around it, not inside Branded Banishment.

Works

  • Lubellion and Albion can summon it when they're Fusion Summoned, since both reach Level 8 or lower.
  • It's the Fusion Monster that Mirrorjade needs next to Fallen of Albaz, and both halves of Dragostapelia's "1 Fusion + 1 DARK".
  • Branded Fusion's turn only allows Fusion Monsters from the Extra Deck, and Daredevil is one.
  • Longinephila can be Branded Fusion's DARK material, then place the counter from the GY (line 5).

Doesn't

  • Branded Banishment and Branded in Red only summon Level 8 or higher, so neither can make Daredevil.
  • Fallen of Albaz's own effect has to use Albaz, which is neither a "Predaplant" nor Level 1.
  • Branded Lost only protects and adds on a Fusion Summon, not on the Tribute summon.

Rulings to Know

Card text

The Tribute summon starts no chain

It's the card's own summoning procedure, not an effect. It can't be chained to, doesn't target, and gets around "cannot be targeted" and effect negation. Cards that negate a Summon still work.

Game rule

Tribute summon: your Main Phase only

Summons by a card's own procedure need your Main Phase. On your opponent's turn, use a Fusion route: Super Polymerization, Bal Teah or Four Heavenly Dragons.

Game rule

Unaffected monsters stay out of reach

A Predator Counter is placed by an effect, so a monster unaffected by your card effects can't get one, and Daredevil can't Tribute it. A monster that only can't be targeted can still get a counter from Triantis, Longinephila's GY effect or Predaplanning.

Card text

Xyz and Link Monsters

They can get a Predator Counter but have no Level, so they can't be the Level 1 material. They can still be Tributed for the summon.

Card text

What the effect counts

Every monster with a Predator Counter on either field when you activate it, yours included; Daredevil itself has none. "Up to" lets you target fewer. It targets, so your opponent can chain the Set cards it targets, and Spells/Traps that can't be targeted are safe.

Card text

When the effect works

In any Main Phase of the turn it was Special Summoned, yours or your opponent's. If it was summoned in the Battle Phase, it can still use the effect in Main Phase 2. It's once per turn by name, so a second Daredevil can't use it that turn.

Card text

The Tribute summon isn't a Fusion Summon

Predaplanning's GY effect and Branded Lost only trigger on a Fusion Summon. A Byblisp Tributed this way is banished, and Sarraceniant doesn't add a card: a Tribute for a summon isn't a card effect.

Community reading

Are the Tributed monsters Fusion Material?

A Reddit rulings thread says yes, as with other Fusion Monsters that have a Tribute procedure. That would trigger Triantis and Bufolicula. There's no official TCG ruling yet, so none of the lines here rely on it.

When to Play It

Shines

  • Going second into a boss that can't be targeted: the Tribute ignores that protection.
  • Against decks that Set several Spells and Traps or rely on Continuous and Field Spells.
  • On your opponent's turn after Predaplanning, or with Starving Venom Wing Dragon in the Pendulum Zone, which puts a counter on everything they Special Summon.
  • Against Level 1 monsters: Super Polymerization takes them without any setup.

Dead

  • Going first: no counters and no enemy backrow. It's still a DARK Fusion Monster for Dragostapelia or Mirrorjade.
  • Monsters unaffected by card effects: no counter, no Tribute.
  • Decks with no backrow: the effect has nothing to hit, so it's a Tribute removal and a 2200 ATK attacker.
  • Nibiru: the Tribute summon still counts toward your opponent's five summons.
