Lore Archive

In Yu-Gi-Oh! ZEXAL, Barian World is ruled by seven Emperors: Nash, Dumon, Merag, Vector, Alito, Girag and Mizael. Their personal Numbers are 101 to 107, and Rank-Up-Magic Barian's Force turns each one into its Chaos form.

Beyond the Brave tells Vector's side of the story. The Umbral Horror monsters and Number 104: Masquerade V are his, and CXyz Hope Chaos Barian Dragon imagines a timeline where Vector, not Nash, defeated the other Emperors and absorbed their power. It is a dark twin of Nash's CXyz Barian Hope built from Vector's Number C5: Chaos Chimera Dragon, and it wears the crest of Don Thousand, the god of Barian World.

Barian's Hope closes the arc. Its artwork restages episode 138, where Yuma reaches for Vector as Don Thousand pulls him away, drawn as Number 39: Utopia catching Number 104: Masquerade by the arm.

Gameplay Evolution Summary

Beyond the Brave (OCG 18 July 2026 · TCG 8 October 2026): four Barian's cards turn the Number 101–107 family from Rank-Up-Magic high rolls into a control deck. V Umbral Horror ranks up without a Spell, CXyz Hope Chaos Barian Dragon stacks on any Chaos Number and stops searches, and Barian's Seventh Untopia limits every player to two non-Number Extra Deck summons a turn.

Archetype Overview

A going-first control deck, and a finisher any Chaos Number deck can borrow.

"Barian's" is the name family of the Seven Emperors' own cards: their Rank-Up-Magic, their Spells and Traps, and now a boss monster. For most of their history these were high-roll Rank-Up plays that lost a card to every interruption. Beyond the Brave fixes that with a Main Deck engine. The Umbral Horror monsters make , ranks it into Number C104 from the Graveyard, and stacks on top.

The result is a going-first lock. The Dragon stops your opponent adding cards from their Deck to their hand except by drawing. lets each player Special Summon only two non-Number monsters from the Extra Deck per turn, and negates the first card that tries to break the board. Because the Dragon can sit on any Number C101 to C107, the same finisher also fits Shark, Galaxy-Eyes and plain Rank 4 decks.

Three ideas behind the deck

  1. Rank up without Rank-Up-Magic

    V Umbral Horror banishes itself from the GY to put a Number one Rank higher on top of a Masquerade Xyz. It counts as an Xyz Summon and keeps every material. Rank-Up-Magic Limited Barian's Force is the backup.

  2. Both locks point outward

    Numbers don't count toward Untopia's limit and the Dragon's lock only affects your opponent. Your own engine spends one of its two non-Number summons, on the Dragon itself.

  3. Materials are the resource

    Each material is 1000 ATK on the Dragon. Its own effect, Barian's Hope and Barian's Seal all add more, and the Seal takes them from your opponent.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Very hard to break going first, and much weaker going second.

Strengths

  • Two floodgates at once. A Thunder Dragon Colossus-style search lock plus an Extra Deck limit that most of this deck's own summons don't count toward.
  • Chaos evolution without a Spell. V Umbral Horror's Rank-Up comes from the GY, so Spell negation doesn't stop it.
  • A boss that grows. The Dragon nets +1 material and +1000 ATK a turn while taking 2 cards off the top of your opponent's Deck.
  • Built-in recovery. Seal revives 2 Umbral Horror monsters from the GY, Hope revives a Masquerade, and Thousand can place a new Untopia on either player's turn.
  • Many one-card starts. Gorgonic Umbral Horror, V Umbral Horror, Seventh Tachyon and Untopia's search all lead into the same line.

Weaknesses

  • Search choke points. Gorgonic, V Umbral Horror, Untopia and Masquerade V all search from the Deck, and Ash Blossom and Droll & Lock Bird punish each one.
  • Nibiru arrives early. The main line reaches its fifth summon before the Dragon exists.
  • Removal that doesn't activate. Kaiju and Lava Golem summon by Tributing the Dragon, which the Seal can't negate.
  • Relies on the Graveyard. V Umbral Horror is a DARK monster in the GY, which D.D. Crow, Bystials and Dimension Shifter all stop.
  • Weak going second. The deck has no board breakers, and the Untopia burn hits you as well.

Competitive Verdict

The support takes the Seven Emperors from casual Rank-Up decks to a real control strategy.

In the Advanced Format, Barian's is at its best as a going-first control deck or as a compact Rank 4 engine. The research grades it a Tier 2 anti-meta deck. It is vulnerable to hand traps at a few single choke points, but a search lock backed by an Extra Deck limit is one of the hardest going-first boards in modern Xyz. Going second it struggles: the deck has no board breakers and can't play through several negations.

Card text

3+ Level 7 monsters

You can also Xyz Summon this card by using 1 monster you control with "Number C101" through "Number C107" in its name (transfer its materials). Gains 1000 ATK for each material attached to it. While this card has a "Number C" monster as material, cards cannot be added from the Main Deck to your opponent's hand except by drawing them. Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; attach the top 2 cards of your opponent's Deck to this card.

Effect by effect

  1. ①Stacks on any Chaos Number from 101 to 107. The Chaos Number and all its materials go underneath, so it arrives with at least three. Three Level 7s is the backup route, and decks rarely use it.
  2. ②1000 ATK per material. 3000 straight off the C104 line, 5000 by the end of it.
  3. ③The search lock. While a "Number C" is attached, your opponent can't add cards from their Main Deck to their hand except by drawing. Adding from the GY, banishment or the Extra Deck still works.
  4. ④Takes their Deck as material. Detach 1 and attach their top 2 cards, for a net +1 material and +1000 ATK a turn. The cards are attached, not sent to the GY, so they give GY decks nothing.

Why it mattersThe first boss the whole 101–107 family can reach with a single overlay. The Umbral Horror line gets there from one card, Shark gets there through C101 and Galaxy-Eyes through C107. It has no protection of its own, which is Barian's Seal's job.

Card text

Each player can only Special Summon non-"Number" monsters from the Extra Deck twice per turn while this card is face-up on the field. You can only use each of the following effects of "Barian's Seventh Untopia" once per turn. During your Main Phase: You can add 1 "Umbral Horror" monster from your Deck to your hand, then discard 1 card. During the End Phase: Each player takes 400 damage for each Xyz Monster on the field.

Effect by effect

  1. ①Two non-Number Extra Deck summons per turn, for both players. Decks built on Link chains, Synchro climbs or Fusion loops stall at two. Masquerade V and C104 are Numbers, so they don't count; the Dragon does.
  2. ②Searches any Umbral Horror, then discard 1. Get the Shining you need this turn or a Gorgonic for the next. The discard can stock Barian's Seal or Barian's Hope, since both have GY effects.
  3. ③400 damage per Xyz on the field, to each player. A clock that counts both sides of the field, in every End Phase, including your own.

Why it mattersA lopsided floodgate: it limits almost every modern Extra Deck while barely touching this one. Thousand Umbral Horror places it straight from the Deck, so you can often play it as a 2-of and still have it every game.

Rulings & Timing

The interactions that decide whether the lock holds.

  • Keep a Chaos Number attached

    The Dragon's lock needs a "Number C" as material. Detach anything else with its effect, and use Barian's Hope to attach a second one as a backup.

  • The Dragon uses one of Untopia's two summons

    It is a "CXyz", not a "Number". Masquerade V and C104 are Numbers, so the whole Umbral Horror line uses only one of your two.

  • Gorgonic's lock lasts only while it's on the field

    While Gorgonic is face-up, only Number Xyz can come from your Extra Deck. Use it as material first, and remember that reviving it brings the lock back.

  • Masquerade V's effect goes one step up

    The monster summoned on top of Masquerade V (usually C104) gets the banish-10 effect. The Dragon, summoned on C104, does not.

  • Thousand's Quick Effect works on either turn

    Its once-per-turn resets on your opponent's turn. Thousand on the field can place a new Untopia from the Deck if they remove the first one.

  • One Barian's Seal effect per turn

    The negate and the GY revive share one use per turn across every copy. It only negates activations, so Kaiju and Lava Golem get past it.
