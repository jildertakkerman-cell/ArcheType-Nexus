Doom-Z

Evolution of the Machine Paradigm

Lore Archive

"Forged in the darkest apocalyptic timelines, the Doom-Z machines are relentless, self-replicating engines of destruction. Driven by a grim, overriding directive to adapt and conquer, they assimilate the shattered remnants of fallen allies to fortify their own armor. What appears as a metallic graveyard is merely a dormant assembly line; when activated, they rise from the scrap in an unending wave, ensuring that the march of the Machine Paradigm never halts."

  1. At the heart of the Artmages' artistic magic stands the Acropolis academy.
  2. There, students like Graflare, Litera, Finmel, and Medius train under the silent blessing of their Power Patron, Nerva.
  3. At the succession ceremony, Finmel was about to be chosen as the next successor.
  4. In a Theorealize vision, Medius foresaw that the ceremony would be attacked.
  5. The DoomZ raided the ceremony, and the academy's peace was violently shattered.
  6. Medius threw himself between Finmel and the raiders and was gravely wounded. At that moment the Power Patron spoke to him.
  7. Made a pseudo-successor and granted Nerva's power, Medius rose as Artmage Diactorus to protect his friends.
  8. The raiders were the DoomZ, the assault squadron of the Thunderclap Empire D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G., led by Drastea: successor of the Imprisoned God Jupiter, and Graflare's own elder brother.
  9. Graflare, once a DoomZ himself, arrived with a stolen mass-produced DoomZ system, but Medius foresaw him going berserk with it.
  10. Medius took the system into himself instead and became DoomZ Break - Diactorus. Together, he and Graflare brought down Drastea's final form, Drastrius.
  11. As Drastrius began to self-destruct, Litera's transportation magic carried everyone to her homeland, Oratorio Garden. There a corrupted ritual turned the forest's Grand Spirit into a rampaging monster.
  12. To protect the unconscious Medius, Finmel made a temporary contract with Nerva's Power Patron Beast, becoming Artmage Non-Finito.
  13. In the chaos, Junora's Power Patron Spirit opened the three sealed portals hidden in Medius's mind. Nerva, Jupiter and Junora poured into him at once, and the unstable merged god Vidolium the Unstable Power Patron of Unity took his body, unleashing the Power Patron Shades.
  14. Artmage, D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G. and Oratorio Garden called a truce and rallied around Finmel, Graflare (now DoomZ XIII Over - Graflareio, in armor that carries Drastea's consciousness) and Litera (now Elfnote Regina), with a single goal: save Medius.
  15. Finmel reached Medius deep inside Vidolium and chose to share the burden of his fate as the Gatekeeper. As the Purification Power Patron, she carried him back out as Theorealized Medius.
  16. Together, Finmel and Medius gave birth to a new Power Patron, Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron, and armed with the purest art, "Ars Magna", leapt into the final battle.

What Beyond the Brave changes for Doom-Z

Beyond the Brave prints no new DoomZ card. Its Medius cards reach Doom-Z through Power Patron DoomZ, Jupredo and the Diactorus name, and one of them is always treated as a DoomZ card. This tab covers what each card does for Doom-Z and which Doom-Z effects it counts for. The three new lines are in the combo simulator below the tabs.

  • Consistency

    Ars Magna "Citrinitas" is a DoomZ card that searches Medius, and Doom-Z's own searchers can find it. That puts Medius the Pure one search away from the Doom-Z core.

  • Reach

    Theorealized Medius and Theorealize Past Lull bring out Medius the Pure and DoomZ Break - Diactorus without a Normal Summon, starting from Jupredo in the Pendulum Zone.

  • Payoff

    On a turn without DoomZ Raiders, the Xyz Monsters you make can become Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron, a Link-3 that ignores your opponent's activated effects.

Ars Magna "Citrinitas"

Ars Magna "Citrinitas"

  • Starter · counts as DoomZ
  • Continuous Spell
  • Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026

(This card is always treated as an "Artmage", "DoomZ", and "Elfnote" card.)

While you control a "Power Patron" Link Monster, you can activate the effects of monsters you control with "Ars Magna" in their original names, as Quick Effects. Once, while this card is face-up on the field, during your Main Phase: You can add 1 "Medius" or "Ars Magna" monster from your Deck to your hand. You can only use this effect of "Ars Magna "Citrinitas"" once per turn.

For Doom-Z

  • It is a DoomZ card everywhere, even while its effects are negated (Konami's FAQ). Drastea can target it, Raiders and Diactorus can destroy it, and DoomZ Change can destroy it straight from the Deck.
  • Doom-Z's searchers can find it. Power Patron DoomZ adds any DoomZ card when it is destroyed, Graflareio and a Jupredo that goes face-up to the Extra Deck add a DoomZ Spell/Trap, and Elara Sets one.
  • Its search adds Medius the Pure, whose summon calls Power Patron DoomZ. On its own, Citrinitas becomes Diactorus and a ready Drastea (line 2).
  • While face-up, it is the DoomZ card Theorealize Past Lull needs.

Each copy searches once, and only while it stays face-up. After that, spend it as the card Drastea or Raiders destroys.

Theorealized Medius

Theorealized Medius

  • Extender · calls Diactorus
  • Level 4 · DARK · Fairy · 1800 / 1800
  • Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026

(This card is always treated as a "Power Patron" card.)

If a "Power Patron" card is on the field: You can Special Summon this card from your hand, then you can add 1 "Theorealize" Spell/Trap from your Deck to your hand. If a monster is banished face-up (except during the Damage Step): You can activate 1 of these effects;

● Add 1 "Ars Magna" card from your Deck to your hand.
● Banish this card, and if you do, Special Summon 1 "Diactorus" monster from your Extra Deck.

You can only use each effect of "Theorealized Medius" once per turn.

For Doom-Z

  • It needs a Power Patron card already on the field: Power Patron DoomZ, Jupiter, or Jupredo in the Pendulum Zone.
  • It adds Theorealize Past Lull, which Special Summons Medius the Pure from the Deck while you control a DoomZ card. With Jupredo in the Pendulum Zone that is two cards and no Normal Summon (line 3).
  • Medius the Pure can add it or Special Summon it from the Deck, because it is always treated as a Power Patron card. It only adds a Theorealize card when it summons itself from the hand.
  • After any monster is banished face-up, by either player, it can banish itself to call DoomZ Break - Diactorus, whose "if Special Summoned" effect destroys a DoomZ card and then a monster. It can do this on your opponent's turn.

The Diactorus it calls has no materials, so Diactorus's Equip Spell effect never triggers. Diactorus's destroy effect is once per turn by name, so a second Diactorus that turn does nothing when it arrives. Doom-Z rarely banishes a monster face-up by itself. The Ars Magna hand costs do, and so does a Medius the Pure that revived itself and then leaves the field.

Ars Magna the Corruption and the Purification

Ars Magna the Corruption and the Purification

  • Searcher · Level 8 body
  • Level 8 · WIND · Dragon · 1000 / 2100
  • Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026

You can banish this card from your hand; add 1 "Ars Magna" Spell/Trap from your Deck to your hand, also until the end of the next turn, the first three times each "Power Patron" Link Monster you control would be destroyed by battle, it is not destroyed. If an Xyz or Link Monster(s) is Special Summoned, while this card is banished (except during the Damage Step): You can Special Summon this card. You can target Spells/Traps on the field, up to the number of "Power Patron" Link Monsters you control; banish them. You can only use each effect of "Ars Magna the Corruption and the Purification" once per turn.

For Doom-Z

  • Banish it from your hand to add Citrinitas. It comes back the next time you Special Summon an Xyz Monster, and Drastea, Elara, Amalthe and Power Patron DoomZ all do that.
  • Back on the field it is a Level 8 Effect Monster. If Power Patron DoomZ's effect is still unused, it can make DoomZ XII End - Drastrius from it. That counts as an Xyz Summon, so Drastrius still equips a monster when it arrives.
  • Banishing it from your hand is a face-up banish, the trigger Theorealized Medius waits for.
  • It is a spare body for Mediclius's Link Materials (line 4).

Its last effect and its battle protection only work with Power Patron Link Monsters. On a Raiders turn you can't make one, so it is just a Level 8 body.

Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron

Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron

  • Finisher
  • Link-3 · WATER · Fairy · 3000 ATK
  • Arrows: bottom-left, bottom, bottom-right
  • Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026

2+ monsters, including a Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, or Link Monster

Gains these effects based on the number of monsters it points to.

● 1+: Once per turn, during your Main Phase: You can negate the effects of all face-up monsters your opponent currently controls, also until the end of this turn, their ATK become halved.
● 2+: Unaffected by your opponent's activated effects.
● 3: Once per opponent's turn (Quick Effect): You can banish this card, also all cards your opponent controls.

For Doom-Z

  • It needs an Xyz Monster among 2+ materials, and every Doom-Z Xyz Monster qualifies. DoomZ Break - Diactorus plus two more monsters makes it.
  • All three arrows point down, so whatever you leave below it keeps it switched on. In line 4 that is Drastea with D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G. and a second Medius the Pure.
  • Theorealize Past Lull fills a zone it points to: Medius the Pure from the Deck on the same turn, or a Fusion, Synchro or Xyz Monster from the GY on a later turn.

It is a Link Monster, so you can't summon it on a turn you used the first effect of DoomZ Raiders, or while Power Patron DoomZ is face-up as a monster. When the monsters it points to leave, it loses those effects.

Does it count?

Doom-Z effects ask for a "DoomZ" card, a "DoomZ" monster, a Power Patron card or an Effect Monster with a Level. Pick a Beyond the Brave card to see which of those effects it satisfies.

    Competitive Evolution: Chaos Origin

    The Chaos Origin expansion (July 2026) introduced Theorealize Past Lull, a spell aimed squarely at DoomZ's oldest weakness: its crippling vulnerability to hand traps on the Normal Summon of Medius the Pure. On paper, it shifts the engine from a fragile Normal Summon dependency to a protected Special Summon initiation. In practice, adoption has been slow — Top 64 lists from the July 2026 European WCQ still run the established DoomZ Change shell untouched, with no copies of Past Lull main, extra, or side.

    Chaos Origin Tech: Theorealize Past Lull

    Theorealize Past Lull

    Chaos Origin · Normal Spell · The Protective Hand

    Card Text

    If you control an "Artmage", "DoomZ", or "Elfnote" card: You can Special Summon 1 "Medius the Pure" from your hand or Deck. Your opponent cannot activate cards or effects when that monster is Special Summoned. You can banish this card from your GY, then target 1 Fusion, Synchro, or Xyz Monster in your GY; Special Summon it to your zone a Link Monster points to. You can only use 1 "Theorealize Past Lull" effect per turn, and only once that turn.

    Why It Matters

    • Summon Shield: Your opponent can't activate cards or effects when Medius is Special Summoned this way, so the summon itself can't be answered.
    • Free Normal Summon: The engine no longer relies on the Normal Summon, freeing it for extenders or hand-trap bait.
    • Graveyard Recovery: From the GY, it revives any Fusion, Synchro, or Xyz Monster to a Link Monster's zone — a powerful Turn 3 reload for boss threats.

    Tournament Reality Check

    Despite the clean interaction, Past Lull has yet to show up in top-level results — Brandan Wilson's Top 64 build at the July 2026 European WCQ ran zero copies across the 40-card main, Extra, and side decks, sticking with the established DoomZ Change shell instead. Part of the issue is textual: Past Lull still requires you to already control an Artmage, DoomZ, or Elfnote card before it does anything, so it protects a Medius play rather than creating one — it's a safety net for an opening you still have to find on your own.

    Lore-Mechanic Mirror: The Purification Loop

    Past Lull's revival needs a Link Monster, and the Power Patron engine has one built for it: Purification Power Patron, a Link-2 DARK Fairy, adds a "Theorealize" card such as Past Lull when it is Link Summoned while a Power Patron monster is in your GY. Later, banish Past Lull from the GY to revive a fallen DoomZ Xyz Monster into the zone Purification points to. The story behind these cards, from Medius the Pure taking the blow meant for Artmage Finmel onward, is told in the Medius saga on the Artmage page.

    The Protected Play Sequence

    Opening DoomZ Raiders into Theorealize Past Lull puts Medius the Pure on the field without your Normal Summon, and your opponent can't respond to that summon. Going second, it destroys two of their monsters and leaves Drastea ready to become Drastrius. On paper it's the strongest line the Chaos Origin pool offers — it just hasn't shown up in a Top 64 decklist yet.

    New Hybrid Architectures

    Elfnote DoomZ

    Open with the Elfnote Synchro engine to establish an early Synchro body (e.g., Elfnote Seraphim Strelitzia or Crystal Wing). Controlling any Elfnote card immediately satisfies Past Lull's activation condition — transition directly into DoomZ Xyz plays without ever spending your Normal Summon.

    Jupredo Pendulum Bridge

    Jupredo the Shademachine in the Pendulum Zone is always treated as a DoomZ card, instantly enabling Past Lull. Once it is on the field as a monster, it can banish the top 3 cards of your Deck face-down and destroy itself to Special Summon Jupiter the Power Patron of Destruction (Rank 10, 3500 ATK) from the Extra Deck — eliminating the need for brick-heavy Level 10 main deck monsters entirely.

    Link-First Sequencing

    The GY recovery effect of Past Lull requires a Link Monster's active arrow pointing to an open zone. Competitive lists resolve S:P Little Knight or I:P Masquerena before triggering the DoomZ Xyz locks, preserving the ability to revive boss monsters on subsequent turns.

    Turn 3 Boss Reload

    After the initial Xyz locks expire, banish Past Lull from the GY to revive Jupiter the Power Patron of Destruction or DoomZ XII End - Drastrius to a Link zone — a game-ending threat that costs zero additional resources beyond a card already spent earlier in the duel.

    Executive Summary: Burst Protocol Era

    The introduction of DoomZ Change in the Burst Protocol expansion initially transformed the Doom-Z strategy. Moving away from its historical status as a "glass cannon," the deck evolved into a consistent Tier 1.5 contender utilizing a "Virtual Hand" mechanic. By destroying cards directly from the Deck, the strategy bypassed previous bottlenecks and restrictive Xyz locks, enabling powerful hybrid builds with Artmage and Onomat engines.

    The Paradigm Shift: DoomZ Change

    DoomZ Change

    The card that changed everything

    The Virtual Hand

    Unlike traditional searchers, DoomZ Change destroys a card in your Deck. This immediately triggers the floating effects of monsters like Amalthe and Elara without them ever needing to be drawn. This effectively treats your Main Deck as an extension of your hand.

    Unshackled Potential

    Critically, DoomZ Change imposes no Special Summoning restrictions. This removes the infamous "Xyz Lock" of the pre-BPRO era, allowing players to link climb into S:P Little Knight or perform Fusion Summons in Artmage hybrid builds.
