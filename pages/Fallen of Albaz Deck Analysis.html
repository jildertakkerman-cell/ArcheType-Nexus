Lore Archive

"A mysterious boy who plummeted from the sky through a portal, possessing no memories but harboring the terrifying power to transform into dragons. His journey forms the epic center of the Abyss storyline."

After crashing into the militant nation of Dogmatika, Albaz was hunted as a heretic. Saved by the compassionate Saint Ecclesia, the two became fugitives, embarking on a sprawling journey across the Abyss. From surviving the scorching Golgonda desert with the Springans and Tri-Brigade, to training alongside the noble Swordsouls and the mystical Icejades, Albaz's path is fraught with tragedy and growth. Together, he and Ecclesia must fight back against the apocalyptic machinations of the Despians, the tyrannical Bystials, and his dark reflection, Aluber the Jester of Despia, to save their world from total despair.

Executive Summary: The Burst Protocol

The competitive landscape has been redefined by the "Burst Protocol" (BPRO) expansion. Historically, "Branded" decks operated as linear Fusion strategies reliant on the resolution of Branded Fusion.

The introduction of Fallen of the White Dragon and Ecclesia and the Dark Dragon signals a shift to a "Synchro-Fusion Hybrid" playstyle. This new era prioritizes resilience against "hand traps" (like Ash Blossom) and resource recursion over fragile explosiveness. The key strategic objective is now to decouple success from the resolution of a single Spell Card.

The Protagonists: A Tale of Two Dragons

Fallen of Albaz

The Original

The classic "Super Poly" on legs. Essential for Mirrorjade and board breaking, but vulnerable to normal summon disruption.

Chaos Origins

Level 8 LIGHT Spellcaster Synchro (generic: 1 Level 4 Tuner + 1+ non-Tuners). Fydraulis Harmonia

Blazing Dominion

Level 7 DARK Dragon Tuner (2500 ATK / 2000 DEF). When the opponent activates a monster effect, reveal this card + up to 5 Synchros from your Extra Deck as a Quick Effect: Special Summon itself (2+), send a revealed Synchro to GY — e.g. Golden Cloud Beast – Malong — (4+), destroy an opponent's monster (6). Locks to Synchro-only Extra Deck effects until end of next turn, a restriction this deck manages easily.

Burst Protocol Strategies

The "White Dragon" Standard Line

(Insulates against Ash Blossom)

  1. Activate Fallen of the White Dragon in hand.
  2. Cost: Send Albion the Branded Dragon from Extra Deck to GY. (Opponent cannot Ash this cost).
  3. Special Summon White Dragon. Effect triggers: Summon Incredible Ecclesia from Deck.
  4. Synchro Summon Ecclesia and the Dark Dragon (Level 8).
  5. End Phase: Albion (in GY) sets Branded in Red. Ecclesia Synchro (on field) Quick-Tags out to summon Guiding Quem.

The "Kitt Bridge" Refund

  1. Activate Branded Fusion.
  2. Send Fallen of Albaz and Tri-Brigade Springans Kitt to GY.
  3. Summon Albion the Branded Dragon.
  4. Chain Link 1: Albion (Fuse). Chain Link 2: Kitt (Revive).
  5. Kitt revives the Fallen of Albaz used as material. Albion banishes materials for Mirrorjade.
  6. Result: Mirrorjade + Albaz on field. (Albaz can be used for cost or Link plays if not locked).

The "Three Champions" Extended Line

Chaos Origins — One-Card Granguignol

  1. Activate Fallen of the White Dragon in hand. Cost: send Mirrorjade from Extra Deck to GY.
  2. White Dragon Special Summons itself; triggers to Special Summon Incredible Ecclesia from Deck.
  3. Tune White Dragon (Level 4) + Ecclesia (Level 4 Tuner) to Synchro Summon The Three Brave Swordsouls .
  4. Three Champions searches Blazing Cartesia, the Virtuous from Deck.
  5. Special Summon Cartesia from hand (White Dragon in GY = treated as Albaz). Activate Cartesia's Quick Effect: Fusion Summon Granguignol the Dusk Dragon using Cartesia + Mirrorjade in GY.
  6. End Phase: Cartesia & Ecclesia return from GY to hand. Result: Three Champions + Granguignol on field, two cards recovered to hand.

Analysis of New Support

The Fallen & The Virtuous

The Fallen & The Virtuous

New Support

A versatile Quick-Play Spell that serves as searchable removal. It sends an "Albaz" Fusion from the Extra Deck to the GY (triggering its effect) to destroy a face-up card. It can also revive a monster if Ecclesia is on field/GY, making it an incredible utility card for both offense and defense.

The Dragon that Devours the Dogma

The Dragon that Devours the Dogma

New Support

Level 8 DARK Dragon Fusion (3000 ATK / 2500 DEF). Materials: Fallen of Albaz + 1 LIGHT or DARK monster + 1 Effect Monster — fuse straight through the opponent's board. While Ecclesia is on field/GY it gains 500 ATK and becomes unaffected by other cards' effects, creating a 3500 ATK "towers" most decks cannot remove. On Special Summon, shuffles up to 2 cards from any GY or banishment into the Deck — invaluable for recycling face-down banished combo pieces. Additionally, during the End Phase, if it was sent to the GY that turn, it searches 1 Dogmatika or Tri-Brigade card from the Deck, fueling the next turn's setup.

The Three Brave Swordsouls

The Three Brave Swordsouls

Chaos Origins — July 2026

The defining bridge of the Hybrid Branded-Swordsoul strategy. Despite its "Swordsoul" name, its true power is as a search bridge across both engines. Key search targets include Blazing Cartesia (immediate Fusion plays), Incredible Ecclesia (re-entry into the Swordsoul engine), and The Iris Swordsoul (a free 2500 ATK body via self-negate). Its negation effect doubles as a going-second tool when Ecclesia or Albaz is in the GY — turning a combo piece into board interaction.

Fydraulis Harmonia

Fydraulis Harmonia

Blazing Dominion — May 2026

The deck's new hand trap and extender. Fydraulis enables a "turn-zero" play against virtually any board that activates a monster effect — by revealing itself and Synchros from the Extra Deck, it Special Summons itself for free, sends Golden Cloud Beast – Malong or Enigmaster Packbit to the GY for cascading disruption, and can destroy a monster at six reveals. The Synchro-only lock it imposes is almost irrelevant here: this hybrid deck runs almost exclusively Level 8 Synchros as its Extra Deck payoffs, so the restriction costs nothing. Searchable via Bystial Magnamhut and many Dragon searchers.

Executive Summary: The Fusion Era

The "Fallen of Albaz" series, or "Branded," has long been a Fusion-centric powerhouse. Its core strategy revolved around Branded Fusion to establish board presence via Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon.

This classic playstyle focuses on pure Fusion summoning, utilizing Despia monsters to maximize consistency and Guardian Chimera for explosive interruption. It is defined by a singular choke point: the resolution of Branded Fusion.

The Protagonist: Fallen of Albaz

Fallen of Albaz

At the heart of the entire saga is Fallen of Albaz. His effect allows him to discard 1 card to Fusion Summon using himself and monsters on either field, similar to Super Polymerization.

In this classic build, his on-field effect is a secondary tool. The true strength lies in using him as material from the Deck via Branded Fusion.

Strengths

  • Consistency: Extremely high access to Branded Fusion via Aluber, Opening, Kitt.
  • Grind Game: Resources cycle back endlessly.

Weaknesses

  • The Choke Point: If Branded Fusion is Ash'd, the turn often ends.
