Recipe Summary

The YUMMY archetype is a delicious recipe that mixes flavors perfectly! Once just a quick "sugar rush" that crashed early, new ingredients have turned it into a hearty, never-ending feast. The deck's main goal is to serve a buffet that never runs out, using its small Level 1 snacks and Synchro chefs to keep your table full of treats all game long!
