The Sweet Science of Synchronization: Burst Protocol Edition

Recipe Summary: From Snack to Feast

Midrange Control Engine

With the arrival of Burst Protocol, the YUMMY archetype has matured from a fragile "sugar rush" into a sophisticated Midrange Control strategy. By mastering the unique Link-to-Synchro physics, the deck maintains a "buffet that never runs out," utilizing its small Level 1 snacks and Synchro chefs to recycle resources and disrupt the opponent with non-destructive removal.

The Secret Sauce: Mechanics

Link-to-Synchro Physics

The "Way" Synchros treat your Link-1 monsters as Level 1 Tuners. This bypasses the need for dedicated Tuners in the Main Deck, freeing up space for "hand traps" and board breakers while allowing the Extra Deck to provide the Tuner on demand.

Tag-Out Dynamics

Every "Way" Synchro can return to the Extra Deck as a Quick Effect to summon 2 Yummy monsters from the GY. This dodges removal, multiplies resources, and re-triggers your "Star" effects during the opponent's turn.

The Assist Loop (Midrange)

  • Use Assist to bounce an opponent's key resource or floodgate.
  • Banish it from GY to send Marshmao to GY.
  • Revive Marshmao later to Set Assist back from the GY, creating an infinite loop of disruption.

The Double Pancake
