Yummy

A Deep Dive into the Sweet and Synergistic Archetype

Executive Summary

The YUMMY archetype is a highly-synergistic, combo-centric strategy centered on LIGHT Beast monsters. Initially a "glass cannon" swarm deck, new support has transformed it into a highly resilient, grind-game focused engine. The deck's primary mode of operation now revolves around a continuous, recursive resource loop, leveraging its Level 1 monsters, Link-1 enabler, and Level 2 Synchros to generate significant card advantage.
