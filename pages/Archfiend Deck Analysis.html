Evolution of a Legacy (Burst Protocol)

The release of the Burst Protocol (BPRO) set represents a seminal moment. Historically, the Archfiend designation functioned less as a cohesive strategic engine and more as a loose categorization.

This wave restructured the deck into a Ritual-Pendulum Hybrid. By leveraging the "Royal Court" monsters and Face-Up Extra Deck recursion, the strategy transitioned into a sophisticated loop.

The Blazing Dominion Paradigm

The "Blazing Dominion" (BLZD) expansion of early 2026 introduced a radical shift. By integrating highly flexible Quick-Play spell access with the established hybrid Ritual-Pendulum mechanics, the strategy has been fundamentally revitalized into a cohesive, Extra Monster Zone-focused control hybrid.

This overhaul abandons reliance on passive triggers, instead forcing a proactive playstyle that attacks both the opponent's field and Graveyard simultaneously, largely circumventing the inherent "minus-two" mathematical transaction historically gating Ritual decks.

The Fiendsmith Integration

A flawless parallel engine revolving around LIGHT Fiends, directly matching Doom Regina Archfiend's typing.

  • Rank 6 Xyz: Matador (Lv 6) + Fiendsmith tokens easily summon D/D/D Wave High King Caesar.
  • Link Fodder: Generates board presence without consuming Normal Summons.
  • Consistency: Fiendsmith's Tract increases the probability of seeing a LIGHT Fiend (Doom Regina) for Pendulum setup.

Algorithmic State Management

Turn 1: The EMZ Lock

  1. Establish Doom Regina in the Pendulum Zone; destroy her to recover the Ritual Spell. (She moves to the face-up Extra Deck).
  2. Set Archfiend Usurpation. Pass your turn.
  3. During the opponent's Standby or Main Phase: Activate Usurpation (Effect 2).
  4. Ritual Summon Doom Regina from the Extra Deck into the Extra Monster Zone.
  5. Result: Unaffected 2900 Defense wall + Opponent's Spells and Traps are destroyed & their Graveyard Spells/Traps are banished instantly.

Turn 2: Ritual Coup OTK

  1. Bait opponent monster negates using Fiendsmith extenders.
  2. Activate Usurpation from Hand. Chain to opponent interactions to guarantee resolution.
  3. Summon Doom Regina using hand materials (avoids field interaction).
  4. Wipe backrow entirely -> Push for game with aggressively statted bodies like Chaos Archfiend.

Choke Points
