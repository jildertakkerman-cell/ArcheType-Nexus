Lore Archive

"The Power Patrons are divine benefactors who channel celestial authority into devastating battlefield effects, manipulating the fates of entire nations from the shadows."

For eons, the disparate nations—the magical academicians of Artmage, the technological zealots of DoomZ, and the mystical denizens of the Elvennotes realm—were unaware that their deities were actively orchestrating their conflicts.

The young protagonist, Medius, found himself caught in this crossfire when the DoomZ attacked the Acropolis academy during an Artmage succession ritual. After making a desperate sacrifice to protect his companion Finmel, Medius was reborn, drawing the attention of the Patrons themselves. Now, the introduction of the "Shadow Trinity" pendulums and the dark convergence into the Null Realm of Vidria has forced the warring nations to set aside their differences. Together, they must face the true source of their suffering: the divine Pantheon of the Power Patrons.

Gameplay Evolution Summary

2026 Update (BLZD & CORI): The introduction of the "Shadow Trinity" pendulums and the Null Realm of Vidria has unified the disparate Medius Lore ecosystems (Artmage, DoomZ, Elvennotes). The paradigm has shifted from hand-advantage mechanics to face-down banishment as a primary cost, unlocking mathematically oppressive end-boards.

Beyond the Brave: Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron

  • TCG · Beyond the Brave · 8 Oct 2026
  • OCG · 18 Jul 2026

Beyond the Brave gives Power Patron a Link boss that scales with its board. Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron is a Link-3 whose three arrows all point down, so from the Extra Monster Zone it looks at three of your Main Monster Zones. Each monster it points to unlocks another effect. The same set's Ars Magna monsters fill those zones for free: they return from banishment whenever a Link Monster is Special Summoned, and Mediclius's own Link Summon counts. The Trinity lock and the Pendulum lines in the other tabs are unchanged. This tab covers Mediclius and how it fits beside them.

It counts its arrows

One pointed monster turns on a mass negate, two make it unaffected by your opponent's activated effects, and three unlock a Quick Effect that banishes every card they control.

Triple yours, halve theirs

Its 1+ effect halves your opponent's monsters. The Finite and the Infinite triples its original ATK to 9000. A monster at 2000 ATK drops to 1000, so a single attack deals 8000.

It can arrive on their turn

Purification Power Patron can Link Summon it during your opponent's Main Phase. That Link Summon returns your banished Ars Magna monsters, so Mediclius shows up with its arrows already filled.

Card text

2+ monsters, including a Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, or Link Monster

Gains these effects based on the number of monsters it points to.

  • 1+Once per turn, during your Main Phase: You can negate the effects of all face-up monsters your opponent currently controls, also until the end of this turn, their ATK become halved.
  • 2+Unaffected by your opponent's activated effects.
  • 3Once per opponent's turn (Quick Effect): You can banish this card, also all cards your opponent controls.

A boss that's only as strong as the zones under it. Mediclius has no protection of its own until it points to two monsters, and those monsters have none at all. What makes it work in Power Patron is the Ars Magna engine: the three Ars Magna monsters Special Summon themselves from banishment when a Link Monster is Special Summoned, and you choose which zones they land in. Purification Power Patron is the standard material. As a Link-2 it counts as two, so Purification plus any one monster makes Mediclius.

Mediclius is Medius awakened, ready to strike back at the Power Patrons. Its 1+ effect is word for word the Quick Effect of Artmage Finmel, the friend whose rescue started the story.       Rules That Decide Your Turn

      Extra Monster Zone only

      All three arrows point down. In a Main Monster Zone they point at your Spell & Trap Zone, where monsters never go, so it has no effects there. Link Summon it to an Extra Monster Zone, not to a zone another Link Monster points to.

      The count is checked live

      Each tier depends on how many monsters it points to right now. Remove one of three and the board banish is gone. Remove two and it's no longer unaffected. The pointed monsters get no protection from Mediclius.

      Bonuses after the summon

      The Finite and the Infinite's tripled ATK and the Division and the Union's three attacks apply to the Power Patron Link Monsters you control. In our lines, you banish them from your hand only once Mediclius is on the field.

      The board banish takes it too

      The 3-arrow Quick Effect banishes Mediclius along with your opponent's cards. After that you no longer control a Power Patron Link Monster, so fire the Ars Magna removal effects first. Ars Magna "Philosophorum" only returns a Power Patron Link Monster from the GY, so it can't bring a banished Mediclius back.

      What "unaffected" doesn't cover

      It only ignores activated effects. Tributing it for a Kaiju or Lava Golem isn't an effect, so that still works. Their continuous effects still apply, and cards that negate the activation of its effect, like Solemn Strike, still negate it.

      Medius the Pure isn't a Power Patron card

      Theorealized Medius can only Special Summon itself from your hand if a Power Patron card is on the field, and Medius the Pure doesn't count. Use Medius's own effect to Special Summon Theorealized Medius from the Deck instead: it is always treated as a Power Patron card.

      Also worth knowing: the zones Mediclius points to count as linked zones, so a face-up Pendulum Monster from your Extra Deck, such as Vidolium the Unstable Power Patron of Unity, can be Special Summoned into them.

      Where It Fits

      With the Ars Magna engine

      The three Ars Magna monsters pay their costs by banishing themselves from your hand, then return when a Link Monster is Special Summoned. Mediclius is the Power Patron Link Monster their removal and battle effects ask for, and its own Link Summon is what brings them back. The Ars Magna guide covers the engine card by card.

      With Purification Power Patron

      Purification is a Link-2, so it and one more monster are all Mediclius needs. It also searches a Theorealize card when it's Link Summoned with a Power Patron monster in your GY, and its Quick Effect lets you make Mediclius on your opponent's turn. Run them together.

      With the Trinity deities

      Nerva, Jupiter and Junora are Fusion, Xyz and Synchro Monsters, so they can be summoned from the Extra Deck to any Main Monster Zone. When you build the lock from the Vidria Lock tab (in the TCG the Field Spell is Plundered Power Patron Plane - Vidolia), place them in the three zones under Mediclius: the lock pieces become its three pointed monsters.

      Outside Power Patron

      Its materials only ask for one Fusion, Synchro, Xyz or Link Monster, so any deck that makes a Link-2 can make it, and every tier works there too. What those decks miss is the Ars Magna payoff: without the returning monsters, filling three zones under it is a lot harder.

      Counterplay & Failure Modes

      How opponents beat it

      • Remove what's under it. Destroying or banishing one pointed monster turns off the board banish; two turns off the immunity. Those monsters have no protection.
      • Kaiju and Lava Golem. Tributing Mediclius is part of their summon, not an activated effect, so it gets past the 2+ immunity.
      • Hand traps on the way in. Ash Blossom stops Medius's Deck summon and each Ars Magna search. Droll & Lock Bird stops every search after the first. Every line reaches five summons before Mediclius points to 2 monsters, so Nibiru, the Primal Being can still clear your field.
      • Negating Purification early. In Line 3, Infinite Impermanence on Purification during their Standby Phase stops the Quick Link Summon, because that effect only works in the Main Phase.

      Mistakes to avoid

      • Summoning it to a Main Monster Zone. It points at nothing there and has no effects.
      • Board banish first, removal second. Mediclius leaves with their cards, and the Ars Magna removal effects need a Power Patron Link Monster on your field.
      • Halving too late. The 1+ effect only works in your Main Phase and only reaches face-up monsters. Use it before the Battle Phase, and use the Finite and the Infinite's banish on face-down monsters.
      • Filling the wrong zones. From the left Extra Monster Zone it reads zones 1 to 3. Returning Ars Magna monsters and Past Lull's Medius should land there.

      Verdict

      Mediclius is the payoff the Beyond the Brave Medius cards were built around. From Medius the Pure and one Ars Magna monster, it's a 9000 ATK breaker going second, or going first, a three-arrow board that is unaffected by your opponent's activated effects and can banish their field on their turn.

      Its limit is the zones under it. Every tier depends on monsters it doesn't protect, so decks with plenty of removal, or a Kaiju, get around it. Play it as the finisher of the Ars Magna package, not as a replacement for the Trinity lock.

      Critical Choke Points

      • The Threat of Droll & Lock Bird Because the entire framework demands multiple sequential searches (via Terminus, Vidria, and Pendulums), a well-timed Droll unceremoniously terminates the turn, leaving Medius stranded.
      • Susceptibility to Nibiru Establishing the Trinity Lock takes 6-8 Special Summons. Unless you aggressively route to Artmage Diactorus (omni-negate) early, the board is highly vulnerable.
      • Preemptive GY Floodgates Despite Vidolium punishing the GY, the deck *needs* the GY for setup (Theorealize, Terminus). Dimension Shifter or Macro Cosmos effectively turns off the core engine.

      Metagame Strengths

      • Absolute Immunity to Spot Removal Vidolium is unaffected by non-Power Patron monster effects. Terminus in the GY bypasses battle protection. This invalidates staples like S:P Little Knight or Mirrorjade.
      • The Non-Activating GY Lock Top tier decks (Fiendsmith, Snake-Eye, Maliss) rely on GY activations. Vidria's lock is continuous, meaning it cannot be outed by standard monster negates like Impermanence or Veiler. Opponents must draw unsearchable backrow removal.
      • Bypassing Targeted Negation By using Shade the Obscure from the hand to trigger the Shadow Pendulums directly in the Extra Deck, you bypass on-field monster negation entirely.

      Strategic Dominance (Pre-BLZD)

      • The Bridge Theory: Unlike decks locked to one mechanic, Power Patron fluidly adapts. If Artmage (Fusion) is stopped, it pivots seamlessly to DoomZ (Xyz).
      • Recursion: Medius shuffling itself back means you never run out of grind game.

      Meta Considerations

      • Medius Dependency: While powerful, the deck relies heavily on resolving Medius. A well-timed Droll & Lock Bird or banishing Medius (Bystials) can halt the engine.
      • Complexity: The non-linear combo lines require high pilot skill.
