Lore Archive

"The Tri-Brigade are a multi-species alliance of Beast, Beast-Warrior, and Winged Beast that Link Summon by banishing members from the GY. Ferrijit searches, Rugal recycles, and Shuraig banishes any card on summon. Their coalition proved so effective it dominated tournaments worldwide alongside Lyrilusc and Zoodiac."

Executive Summary: The "Double Extender" Era

The release of "Tri-Brigade Springans Kitt" in the Burst Protocol expansion has addressed the archetype's historic linearity. Previously defined by "Protect the Revolt," the strategy has evolved into a hyper-consistent hybrid machine.

This new Level 4 Keystone acts as a "Double Extender"—it summons itself for free to insulate plays against hand traps like Infinite Impermanence, and it floats into a body when sent to the Graveyard for cost. This crucial interaction with "Branded Fusion" and "Tri-Brigade Fraktall" allows the deck to pivot fluidly between Link, Fusion, and Xyz mechanics, removing the binary "Normal Summon or bust" failure state of the past.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Non-Linear Play: Can extend via Springans Kitt even if Normal Summon is negated.
  • Nibiru Resilience: Springans Kitt triggers in GY even if tributed, ensuring follow-up bodies.
  • Cost Efficiency: Turning discard costs into Special Summons changes the deck's economy.

Weaknesses

  • Defense Lock: Kitt revives in Defense, meaning you cannot revive Link monsters with her effect.
  • Graveyard Reliant: Still loses to Dimension Shifter/Abyss Dweller.

Recommended Builds

  • Branded Hybrid: The strongest variant. Maximizes the "Bridge" synergy.
  • Lyrilusc: Uses Kitt for Level 1/Rank 1 bridges (less common now).
  • 60-Card Pile: Kitt is an excellent "Grass" mill target.
