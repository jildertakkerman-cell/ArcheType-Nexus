Lore Archive

"The Majespecters are Pendulum wind spirits that cannot be targeted or destroyed by card effects—period. This innate, unconditional protection makes them among the most frustrating monsters to remove, and their Pendulum effects search a complete toolkit of Spells and Traps that protect and disrupt with breezy efficiency."

Archetype Fundamentals

The Majespecter archetype is a unique and resilient force among Pendulum strategies. Comprised of WIND Spellcaster monsters, they distinguish themselves not through powerful Pendulum Zone effects, but through on-summon monster abilities and a formidable defensive core. Their core identity is built on inherent resilience, as nearly every monster cannot be targeted or destroyed by opponent's card effects. This forces opponents into unconventional removal methods and allows Majespecters to maintain a steady board presence to fuel their control-oriented game plan.

The Majestic Arsenal

The Searchers

  • Bunbuku (Raccoon): Premier starter. Searches any "Majespecter" monster.
  • Kyubi (Fox): Trap searcher. Accesses Tempest for negation.
  • Yata (Crow): Spell searcher. Grabs extenders like Wind.
  • Nekomata (Cat): Delayed, but versatile search for any archetype card.

The Disruptors & Extenders

  • Kirin (Unicorn): Legendary boss. Non-destruction bounce effect.
  • Yamarashi (Porcupine): Key extender. Special Summons itself from hand.
  • Nue (Orthrus): Link-2 lynchpin. Sets up scales and loads the Extra Deck.
  • Draco - Ryu: Rank 4 catalyst. Summons from Deck when a monster is tributed.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Inherent protection from targeting and destruction effects.
  • Highly recursive engine generates significant card advantage.
  • Strong against decks reliant on standard targeting/destruction removal.

Weaknesses

  • Vulnerable to non-targeting removal like Evenly Matched & Kaijus.
  • Low monster ATK makes it difficult to close out games quickly.
  • Clear choke points (e.g., Nue's summon) are weak to hand traps.
  • Disabled by anti-tribute or type-changing floodgates.

Counter-Strategy

  • Use Ash Blossom on Nue's effect.
  • Destroy Pendulum Scales with backrow removal like Cosmic Cyclone.
  • Utilize Side Deck cards like Mask of Restrict, Droll & Lock Bird, or Evenly Matched.

New Card Spotlight: Wind Unicorn Parallel, the Dracoslayer

Majespecter finally gets an answer to the one question it could never ask. Every removal effect the archetype has ever printed points at monsters, so a resolved floodgate or field spell simply sat there and won. Wind Unicorn Parallel, the Dracoslayer bounces any card the opponent controls, and it does it while doubling as a live Pendulum scale. It is Kirin rebuilt for the modern game, with the protection deliberately made conditional to keep it honest.

Pendulum effect — two modes

With a card in your other Pendulum Zone, either Special Summon itself as a Level 6 body, or destroy itself to Special Summon a Level 5 or lower Pendulum Monster Card out of your other scale. One of the two, once per turn.

Quick Effect bounce

Target 1 Pendulum Monster Card you control and 1 card your opponent controls, and return both to the hand. Once per turn.

Conditional protection

Untargetable and indestructible by opponent card effects only while it is a Pendulum Summoned card in your Monster Zone. Cheat it out any other way and it is a plain 2000 ATK body.

Majespecter Unicorn — Kirin

Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin
Level 6 WIND Spellcaster Scale 2 2000 / 2400
Why it is here

Parallel shares Kirin's Level, Attribute, Type and Scale exactly. The two are built to be read side by side, and they are meant to be played side by side.

Its permanent shield

Kirin cannot be targeted or destroyed by opponent card effects at all, regardless of how it reached the field. That clause is the reason it spent years on the Forbidden list.

Its narrow window

Kirin bounces a Pendulum Monster in your Monster Zone and a monster your opponent controls. Nothing in your scales, and nothing in their backrow.

The Text, Verbatim

Pendulum Effect: If you have a card in your other Pendulum Zone: You can activate 1 of these effects; ● Special Summon this card. ● Destroy this card, and if you do, Special Summon 1 Level 5 or lower Pendulum Monster Card from your Pendulum Zone. You can only use this effect of "Wind Unicorn Parallel, the Dracoslayer" once per turn.

Monster Effect: While this Pendulum Summoned card is in your Monster Zone, your opponent cannot target it with card effects, also it cannot be destroyed by your opponent's card effects. (Quick Effect): You can target 1 Pendulum Monster Card you control and 1 card your opponent controls; return them to the hand. You can only use this effect of "Wind Unicorn Parallel, the Dracoslayer" once per turn.

Read "Pendulum Monster Card" literally. Kirin says "Pendulum Monster in your Monster Zone". Parallel says "Pendulum Monster Card you control", which includes the cards sitting in your Pendulum Zones. That one word is the single largest functional gap between the two unicorns, and almost every line below depends on it.

What Actually Changes

Backrow finally answerable

Storm, Cyclone, Tempest and Tornado all read "monster". Parallel is the archetype's first searchable out to a resolved floodgate, a field spell or a set card.

The scale ceiling is gone

Scales of 2 and 5 only Pendulum Summon Levels 3 and 4. Parallel Special Summons itself out of the scale instead, so a Level 6 no longer needs an outside high scale.

Once-per-turn scales reset

Bouncing your own scale returns it to hand, so a scale effect you already used this turn can be re-placed and used again.

It ignores the Nue lock

Nue restricts you to Majespecter and Dracoslayer Extra Deck summons. Parallel is a Dracoslayer, so the lock never touches it.

The shield is conditional

Summoned by Draco - Ryu, by its own scale effect, or by any Dracoslayer, it lands with no protection and eats Infinite Impermanence or S:P Little Knight.

Bunbuku cannot find it

It has no "Majespecter" in its name, so Bunbuku, Nekomata and Majespecter Wind all miss it. Majesty Pegasus is the bridge you build instead.

The Updated One-Card Line

The classic flowchart in the Core Analysis tab still holds. This is that line extended, with the tribute now doing double duty as the trigger that fetches Parallel out of the Deck. Bunbuku or Yata both open it.

1

Normal Summon Bunbuku, search Yamarashi.

2

Special Summon Yamarashi from hand, then Link Summon Orthrus - Nue.

3

Nue returns Bunbuku and Yamarashi to hand and loads Kyubi and Yata face-up into the Extra Deck. You are now locked to Majespecter and Dracoslayer.

4

Scale Yamarashi at 2 and Bunbuku at 5, then Pendulum Summon Kyubi and Yata.

5

Kyubi searches Tempest, Yata searches Majespecter Wind.

6

Overlay both Level 4s into Draco - Ryu.

7

Activate Majespecter Wind or the field spell Majesty's Pegasus, tributing Nue to Special Summon Ogama from the Deck.

8

The tribute triggers Draco - Ryu. Detach 1 material and Special Summon Wind Unicorn Parallel straight from the Deck as a Level 6 WIND Spellcaster.

9

Ogama sets Tornado from the Deck. Set Tempest from hand and pass.

Parallel arrives naked here. Step 8 is a Special Summon, not a Pendulum Summon, so it has no protection on the pass. That is a deliberate trade: you keep the Quick Effect and accept that a targeting negate can answer the body.

The Four-Interruption Endboard

Draco - Ryu still holds one material, and its effect works up to twice per turn. That is the hinge the whole opponent's turn swings on.

Tempest tributes Ogama to negate a monster effect or a Special Summon.

Tornado tributes a WIND Spellcaster to banish a monster, which answers anything that floats on destruction.

Parallel bounces one of your own scales together with any card they control. Their floodgate leaves, your starter comes back to hand.

Tributing Ogama for Tempest re-triggers Draco - Ryu. Detach the last material to summon Kirin from the Deck for a second, fully protected bounce.

Turn three is where the trade pays off. Because Parallel spent one of your scales rather than a monster on the field, that scale is a card in hand again. Your remaining scale is intact and Nue, Kyubi and Yata are all sitting face-up in the Extra Deck, so a single Pendulum Summon rebuilds the board and fires every search a second time.

Four Things Players Get Wrong

  • Your half of the bounce does not have to be a monster. "Pendulum Monster Card you control" reaches into your Pendulum Zones. You can keep every body on the field and pay with an already-spent scale instead.
  • Cheating it out throws the shield away. Draco - Ryu, Ignister Prominence, Majester Paladin, Dinoster Power and its own Pendulum effect all summon it without protection. Only a real Pendulum Summon switches the clause on.
  • Bouncing a scale resets that scale's once-per-turn. Send Majesty Pegasus back to hand after using its search and you can place it again for another one the same turn.
  • In the Pendulum Zone it is a Spell Card. While it sits in the scale, monster-facing effects do not see it, and Spell removal does.

Wind Unicorn Parallel, the Dracoslayer

Play two or three
  • Multiples matter because the copy in hand is a live scale and the copy in the Deck is a Draco - Ryu target. They do different jobs.
  • When you have the choice, Pendulum Summon it. Widened scales are worth the deckbuilding cost purely to switch the protection on.
  • Summoning it on the opponent's turn through Draco - Ryu forces them to answer the Quick Effect before they can punish the missing shield.
  • Its second Pendulum mode rescues a starter stranded in your other scale, which quietly saves bricked openers.

The Hybrid Shell Around It

A real cost
  • Three Majesty Pegasus plus the Dracoslayer scales is roughly a third of the Main Deck. Pure Majespecter is no longer the best build.
  • The field spell line wants Terraforming to be reliable, which is another slot.
  • Archfiend Eccentrick and the outside high scales come out. That mostly pays for the Pegasus package.

How Opponents Should Play Against It Now

The old plan of holding removal for the untargetable unicorn is wrong against this board. The pressure points moved.

Punish it on arrival

The moment Draco - Ryu or Ignister summons Parallel, it is targetable. Veiler, Impermanence or S:P Little Knight answer it cleanly in that window.

Stop the Link, stop the turn

Nue is still the single bottleneck. A hand trap on its Link Summon effect cuts off the scales and the Extra Deck loading in one shot.

Anti-Pendulum still ends it

Anti-Spell Fragrance stops scales from being placed at all, and Droll & Lock Bird kills the search chain the whole engine runs on.

Low ATK is still the flaw

Most Majespecter bodies sit under 1500 ATK, and Pendulums destroyed in the hand go to the graveyard rather than the Extra Deck.

The Verdict

A strong rogue deck, not a tier one deck

Parallel is the best card Majespecter has been given since Nue and Draco - Ryu, and it is not broken. It fixes the two structural holes the archetype has carried since it was printed: no answer to non-monster cards, and a scale range that could not summon its own best monster. In exchange, the designers made the protection conditional and put the card under a different archetype name, which means every practical way you summon it costs you the shield. The current format helps too, because generic negate piles have thinned out and native, archetype-specific interaction is worth more than it was.

What it does not fix is the deck going second. Setting scales and resolving Nue through an established board is still close to impossible, and one resolved Anti-Spell Fragrance still ends the game on the spot. Parallel raises the ceiling and the skill floor at the same time. It rewards a pilot who knows exactly which of their own cards to bounce, and it leaves the archetype where it has always sat: interactive, resilient, and a very uncomfortable matchup rather than a favourite.

Where It Still Falls Over

Going second is unchanged. Parallel needs a scale or a tribute to appear, and neither survives a board that already has negates on it.
Anti-Spell Fragrance is still lethal. No scales means no Pendulum Summon, no Nue recovery and no Parallel.
The shield is off in practice. Almost every realistic summon method skips the Pendulum Summon, so plan around a targetable body.
One bounce per turn. Both the scale effect and the Quick Effect are once per turn each, so extra copies do not stack disruption.
The Pegasus tax is real. Making Parallel searchable costs roughly a third of the Main Deck in Dracoslayer support.
