Enneacraft

System: Reactive Obscurity // Status: Face-Down

PENDULUM FLIP BURN

Executive Summary

The Enneacraft archetype introduces a paradigm shift known as "Reactive Obscurity." By fusing the disparate mechanics of Pendulum Summoning and Flip Effects, the deck functions as a psychological minefield. The deck subverts the "Face-Up" nature of modern Yu-Gi-Oh! by forcing a game state where threats are hidden in Face-Down Defense Position, effectively "blanking" common interactions like Infinite Impermanence.

The core win condition revolves around the Sinquisition Counters. As opponents trigger hidden threats, they generate counters which are converted into massive burn damage (900 per counter) during the End Phase via the field spell, accessing the Enneacraft Archives for lethal damage without ever declaring an attack.

The Face-Down Lock

"You cannot Special Summon... except in Face-Down Defense Position."

This restriction prevents Link, Synchro, or Xyz plays for the turn but insulates the player from "Turn 0" interruptions. Monsters never hit the field face-up, meaning target-based negates have no legal targets.

The Sinquisition Economy

1 Counter = 900 Damage

Counters are generated when monsters flip face-up. The field spell, Enneapolis, removes these counters to inflict 900 damage each. A typical turn cycle can generate 3000+ burn damage simply by surviving.

Tactical Sequences

The "Release" Line (1.5 Card Combo)

The safest setup that plays around Droll & Lock Bird.

  1. 01. Activate Enneacraft - Release. Place orgIA directly into Pendulum Zone.
  2. 02. Activate orgIA Scale Effect: Pay 900 LP. Reveal 3 Enneapolis. Add 1 to hand.
  3. 03. Activate Field Spell Enneapolis. Use effect to bounce orgIA from Scale to Hand.
  4. 04. Reveal orgIA in hand to Special Summon another Enneacraft from hand (Face-Down).
  5. END BOARD: Field Spell active + 1 Face-Down Monster + orgIA in hand (Anti-Hand Trap live).

The "Spirit + Zaborg" Aggro Line

High risk, game-ending reward by ripping the opponent's Extra Deck.

  1. 01. Normal Summon Spirit of the Fall Wind. Search any Enneacraft.
  2. 02. Reveal Enneacraft in hand to Special Summon another body Face-Down.
  3. 03. Tribute Summon Zaborg the Mega Monarch. Target self to destroy.
  4. 04. Send 8 cards from BOTH Extra Decks to GY.
    • You send: 3x Elder Entity N'tss (Pop 3), 1x Garura (Draw 1).
    • Opponent sends: Their entire engine (Promethean Princess, Albion, etc.).

Strengths

  • Turn 0 Immunity: Cannot be hit by Impermanence/Veiler while setting up.
  • Extra Deck Lock: Atori.MAR flips boards face-down, preventing Link/Synchro/Xyz summons.
  • Burn Damage: Does not need to attack to win; ignores high ATK boards.

Weaknesses

  • Droll & Lock Bird: Hard counter to the searching loop.
  • Non-Destruction Removal: Snake-Eye (placing in S/T zone) bypasses Archa.TAIL protection.
  • Slow Tempo: Requires "Set and Pass," vulnerable to time rules if burn doesn't kill fast enough.
