PROJECT: VELOX VOLATILE EXCAVATION

Executive Summary: Controlled Chaos

The GMX (Genetic Modification X) archetype represents a paradigm shift for Dinosaur strategies. Moving away from the linear "Go Second, Break Board" philosophy of Ultimate Conductor Tyranno, GMX introduces a sophisticated Midrange Control engine driven by deck excavation and LP resource exchange.

The core mechanic, the Excavation-Fusion Protocol, treats your Life Points as a transactional currency. By paying LP to excavate materials directly from the deck, GMX achieves explosive, non-linear plays. It sacrifices stability for high ceilings, utilizing "Researchers" to initiate experiments and "Specimens" to dominate the board.

CLASSIFICATION: NORMAL SPELL // VOLATILE

The engine's combustion chamber. Unlike Branded Fusion, this card does not send specific targets. Instead, it Excavates until it finds valid materials.

  • COST: 400 LP per excavated card.
  • RISK: Vulnerable to Ash Blossom (sends from Deck).
  • REWARD: Non-linear resource generation; no "Garnets" required.

Operational Maneuvers

Protocol: 1.5 Card Starter

  1. 01. Normal Summon GMX Researcher Selande. Effect: Summon Returned Dino Daneen from Deck.
  2. 02. Daneen Effect: Search GMX Chairman Kimridge.
  3. 03. Hand Effect: Reveal Kimridge, search Applied Experiment #55, shuffle Kimridge back (Stacking Deck).
  4. 04. Activate Applied Experiment #55. Excavate until GMX + Dino found. Fusion Summon GMX - VELOX.

Competitive Analysis

System Strengths

  • Midrange Flexibility: Unlike traditional "OTK or Bust" Dinos, GMX can play a resource game via Alos recursion.
  • Deck Thinning: Excavation + Search chains rapidly thin the deck, increasing the odds of drawing Side Deck cards like Hand Traps.
  • Dino Synergy: Selande summoning Babycerasaurus from Deck is a massive consistency boost for hybrid builds.

Critical Failures

  • Ash Blossom Vulnerability: Applied Experiment #55 sends from Deck. A well-timed Ash can end the turn if no extenders are held.
  • LP Management: Bad RNG on excavation can leave you within lethal range of Burn decks or Time Rules.
  • Droll & Lock Bird: The Daneen -> Kimridge -> Spell search chain folds to Droll immediately.
