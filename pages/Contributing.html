How suggested edits get reviewed

Every deck page has a "suggest edit" control on its combo walkthroughs, strengths/weaknesses lists, and card notes. Nothing you submit goes live automatically — here's exactly what happens to it, and what doesn't.

Suggestions are reviewed for spam and vandalism only — not for whether the reviewer personally agrees with them. If you play this deck and I don't, you know it better than the page does.

What gets rejected

  • Spam, vandalism, or content unrelated to the section it's attached to.
  • Obviously fake, joke, or bad-faith submissions.
  • Something that's verifiably, factually wrong — not "phrased differently than I would have written it."

What doesn't get rejected

Disagreeing with your tech choices, your combo priorities, or your writing style isn't grounds for rejection. This system exists because the AI-drafted content that used to live on these pages had real errors a duelist would catch and I wouldn't — gatekeeping suggestions on personal taste would just recreate that problem with an extra step.

When suggestions conflict

Occasionally two people will submit genuinely contradicting corrections for the same section. When that happens I won't just pick one — I'll flag it and ask rather than decide alone.

Transparency

Every section has a [history] control showing every past version — who wrote it, when, and what it replaced, including the original AI draft. Nothing gets quietly rewritten.
