Armed Dragon

The "Burst Protocol" Evolution

Executive Summary (2026 Update)

The introduction of Fist Armed Dragon (aka Threat Armed Dragon) in the Burst Protocol set has fundamentally transformed the archetype. No longer just a linear "level-up" strategy, Armed Dragon has evolved into a dynamic midrange toolbox.

The deck now leverages "Fist" as a horizontal expansion engine, effectively turning Graveyard destruction into advantage. This allows for complex plays including the infamous "Amorphage Lock", robust Rank 7 plays, and one-card starters via Artillery Catapult Turtle. The focus has shifted from "hold the line" defense to aggressive field control and recursion loops.

New Mechanics

The "Fist" Recursion Loop

Fist Armed Dragon can destroy a Dragon you control to revive itself from the GY. This converts low-value tokens or spent searchers into a Level 5 body with 2400 ATK, creating an "infinite" resource loop as long as you have fodder.

Cost Inversion

The "cost" of discarding cards for Fist Armed Dragon's field effect is actually a benefit. Discarding Armed Dragon Thunder LV3 draws a card, effectively making the powerful removal and summon effect of "Fist" card-neutral or even positive.

The "Stun & Negate" Setup

The modern endboard combines generic interaction with floodgate pressure.

  • Amorphage Sloth: Prevents opponent from using the Extra Deck (Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Link).
  • Apollousa / S:P Little Knight: Provides monster negation or banishing interaction.
  • Armed Dragon Thunder LV10: (If accessible) Quick Effect destruction.

Competitive Analysis (2026)

Strengths

  • Economy Engine: Fist Armed Dragon turns discard costs into draw power and board presence.
  • Floodgate Access: Easy access to Amorphage Sloth to shut down meta decks.
  • Infinite Recursion: Fist revives itself every turn, maintaining pressure.

Weaknesses

  • Droll & Lock Bird: The deck searches constantly; Droll ends the turn immediately.
  • Targeting Protection: "Fist" must target to destroy. Untargetable monsters (e.g., Sauravis) stop the removal.
  • Bystials: Banishing "Fist" or "LV" monsters from GY breaks the recursion loop.

Key Synergies

  • Dragon Rulers: Tempest remains the best searcher; Blaster/Redox offer extension.
  • Rokket Engine: Quick Launch summons Tracer (Lvl 4), which Fist can pop to summon Level 6s.
  • Burst Protocol: New set support solidifies the deck as a rogue contender.

The New Arsenal

Fist Armed Dragon

Level 5 / WIND / Dragon

The heart of the new strategy. (1) Destroys a Dragon you control to revive itself from GY. (2) Discards a card to destroy a monster ( <2400 ATK) and Special Summon a Dragon 2 Levels higher from Deck.

Artillery Catapult Turtle

The 1-card starter. Tributes itself to Special Summon "Fist Armed Dragon" directly from the Deck, bypassing the need to draw it.

Amorphage Sloth

The floodgate. Summoned from Deck by destroying a Level 4 Dragon with "Fist". Prevents both players from Special Summoning from the Extra Deck.

Armed Dragon Thunder LV3

Still the primary starter. Discarding it for "Fist" triggers a draw, maintaining hand advantage.

Pile Armed Dragon

Essential extender. Can dump "Fist" to GY to start the recursion loop.

Tempest, Dragon Ruler

Mandatory searcher. Accesses any WIND Dragon and facilitates Rank 7 plays.
