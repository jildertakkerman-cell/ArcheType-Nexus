Ally of Justice

An Analysis of the Mismatched Miasma of the Machines

Lore Archive

"Forged in the crucible of an ancient war against the forces of Light, the Allies of Justice are relentless dark-attribute sentinels. Their technology is specifically calibrated to annihilate LIGHT-attribute beings, making them the ultimate counter-weapon against celestial threats. Cold, mechanical, and unyielding, they represent the iron will of darkness not as evil, but as balance—a necessary shadow cast to keep the blinding light of tyranny at bay."

  1. For ages the continent's tribes had fought one another for land and power. Then the Worms crashed down on meteorites: invaders from outer space that mutate to fit any world, multiply, and consume it.
  2. Each tribe fought back in its own way. The Ice Barrier freed Brionac, one of its sealed ice dragons, while the X-Sabers, Mist Valley and Flamvell sent their strongest. Every attack only pushed the Worms to evolve.
  3. As whole tribes died out, the four strongest, the Flamvell, the Ice Barrier, the X-Sabers and Mist Valley, joined forces as the Ally of Justice to build anti-Worm weapons. Catastor, made by all four together, won ground on every front.
  4. The Allies also found the Genex, silent machines that seemed to improve the land around them. Seeing the Worms as polluters, the Genex set about wiping them out, and the Allies began studying what they were.
  5. The surviving Worms kept evolving, until clever Worm Nobles took command of the rest. Worn down, the X-Sabers' leader Gottoms chose a new generation, the XX-Sabers, fitted with the Allies' machinery.
  6. The Worms reached even the forest of the peaceful Naturia. They hated fighting, but not one of them stepped back from the invaders fouling their home.
  7. From the Worms' meteorites the Allies built the Ally Mind, began capturing Worms, and made captured Genex run their weapons. Mist Valley disliked using the voiceless Genex, and the Fabled secretly fed that doubt until they left the alliance.
  8. Woken by the chaos on the surface, or perhaps by the Allies' experiments with other dimensions, the Fabled rose from deep below: evil gods true only to their own desires, who joined fights only when it amused them.
  9. The Fabled fell on the nest of the Jurrac, where Worms rarely came. The proud Jurrac refused to be prey and burned the hunters with the flames that clothed their bodies. Meanwhile the Allies fitted some Genex with fusion reactors, making the R-Genex.
  10. With Worms marching day and night and the Fabled striking where they pleased, the Ice Barrier freed its second dragon, Gungnir. Its breath turned invaders and gods alike into ice.
  11. Alone since leaving the Allies, Mist Valley was close to defeat when its strongest weapon, the Mist Wurm, fell. Then the legendary Dragunity rode in from Dragon Ravine: knights who become one with their dragons in body and soul.
  12. As Worm attacks grew strangely rare, the Allies built their ultimate weapon. Then space tore open and Worm Zero emerged, devouring its own kind. The floating fortress Decisive Armor met it, both struck at once, and the Worms were wiped out.
  13. In the brief peace, the Fabled moved to conquer the surface, led by the beasts called The Fabled. Mist Valley agreed to help the Allies again, and together they built the Genex Allies from repaired Genex and R-Genex reactors, one model for each tribe.
  14. Their flames halved, the last Jurrac became one blazing meteor and fell on their own nest, burning the Fabled there to ash. From the ashes rose the Neo Flamvell, and their wishes woke the Ancient Flamvell Deity, which burned away the Fabled left in the nest.
  15. The other Fabled only fought harder, and the exhausted land was laid waste. Divided, the Ice Barrier broke the seal of its third dragon. Trishula destroyed every Fabled on the planet, but it would not obey, and all three dragons rampaged together.
  16. Not even the Tiger King Dewloren could stop them. The Sacred Spirit, which had watched over the Ice Barrier since its founding, gave up its own being to seal all three dragons at once.

Side story: Program 'Return 0;'

The Valuable Book EX 4 (2024): weapons built against an enemy from outside, thrown away when the battle ended, and the program hidden inside them.

When Worms from outer space attacked, the scattered tribes united and built weapons around the Genex, lifeforms that had existed since antiquity and could produce natural energy. The tribes spoke to them through "Controller" and half forced them into service as weapons (Ally of Justice Decisive Armor, Worm Zero).

The decisive weapons of the Allies of Justice were wrecked in the showdown with the Worms. "Spare", built into one of them, had stopped working and could barely move, and Controller waited for its own end. Just before it came, Controller was infected by the wicked thoughts of the Evilswarm, but a special program reinstalled by the remaining Genex, 'Return 0;', set it free. Without a word, Controller repaired itself with parts from Spare, holding in its hand the flower that reminded it of Spare (Genex Controller, Genex Spare, Repair Genex Controller).

The revival of Sophia also reactivated 'Return 0;'. The Genex seemed to be working hard at improving the environment, but their true purpose was to destroy the world Sophia had made and create one for Tierra. Fooling even the god of star-creation, they followed their programming and secretly built "Return Zero" (R-Genex Undine, R-Genex Turing).

The new final weapon, with Repair built into it, entered the battle to return both the god and all it had created to nothingness (Arms of Genex Return Zero).

Executive Summary

The Ally of Justice archetype, designed to counter the "Worm" archetype, is a collection of DARK Machine monsters with a hyper-focused design philosophy: to defeat LIGHT monsters. This created a fundamental competitive flaw, making the archetype unplayable against most of the card pool. Despite this, it yielded two iconic "tech" cards: Ally of Justice Catastor and Ally of Justice Cycle Reader, which transcended the archetype's original design.

Genesis and Design Philosophy

A Hero Built for a Single Villain

The archetype's conceptual foundation is linked to the "Duel Terminal" lore, where Ally of Justice are a military force of DARK Machine monsters created to combat the invading "Worm" archetype, which consisted of LIGHT monsters. This lore-driven design resulted in a "silver bullet" problem, making the deck ineffective against most other attributes and playstyles.

The Inconsistent Arsenal

Main Deck Monsters

Ally of Justice Searcher

Discards a card to flip an opponent's face-down monster, a now outdated and irrelevant effect.

Ally of Justice Unlimiter

Tributes itself to double another Ally of Justice monster's ATK, but is a strictly worse version of a generic spell card.

Ally of Justice Cycle Reader

A powerful "hand trap" that banishes LIGHT monsters from the opponent's Graveyard. This card succeeded by breaking from the archetype's core design philosophy.

Extra Deck Monsters

Ally of Justice Catastor

A classic staple Level 5 Synchro monster. Its effect to destroy any non-DARK monster it battles made it a universally useful tech card.

Ally of Justice Decisive Armor

A powerful boss monster with high summoning requirements, but its effects are conditional on the opponent controlling a LIGHT monster, rendering it situational.

Competitive Assessment

Strengths

  • Side Deck Utility: Individual cards like Catastor and Cycle Reader were powerful "tech" cards in their prime.
  • Thematic Cohesion: The deck's design is a great example of a lore-driven archetype, despite its flaws.

Weaknesses

  • Conditional Effects: The majority of the archetype's effects are only usable against LIGHT monsters.
  • Lack of Consistency: No dedicated search or swarming Spell/Trap cards, making the deck slow and unreliable.
  • Obsolete Design: Its battle-centric and slow playstyle is completely out of place in the modern game.
