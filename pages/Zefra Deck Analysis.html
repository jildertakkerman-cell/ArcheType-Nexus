ZEFRA

The Divine Pendulum Collective

Lore Archive

"The Zefra are not an archetype in the traditional sense — they are a convergence point, the moment when nine different prophecies from nine different traditions all resolve to the same answer. As Pendulum monsters, they bridge the gap between the deck and the Extra Deck, and as members of multiple other archetypes, they serve as the connective tissue between strategies. The Oracle of Zefra is the pivot around which this entire cosmology rotates: when all nine gather, the future becomes visible and inevitable."

  1. All life on the planet came from the Naturia Sacred Tree and returned to it at death. Long ago its goddess, Sophia, sealed Tierra, the Source of Destruction, inside it and broke the seal's Core into ten Pyroxenes. Now Tierra was stirring.
  2. Sensing the disturbance, Constellar Sombre used eight Pyroxenes to create the eight Yang Zing, dragons made from the land's life energy.
  3. In the Mist Valley marshes, the revived Gem-Knight Master Diamond had become Cairngorgon, stealing souls. He absorbed his old friend Evilswarm Kerykeion and became Shaddoll Core, which spawned the Shaddolls: the dead of the last war, trapped by Tierra.
  4. The Shaddolls stole the shadows of two Yang Zing. Then Chiwen fell from the sky and joined every Yang Zing into Baxia, Brightness of the Yang Zing.
  5. Warriors of light descended as the Tellarknights, heirs of the Constellar. When Unukalhai was taken and became Shaddoll Squamata, Kerykeion slipped his staff's shadow out to them, and Deneb, Altair and Vega became Stellarknight Delteros.
  6. With the Tellarknights' stolen power, the Shaddolls fused the fallen Yang Zing into dark dragons. From the eight Yang Zing Pyroxenes, Lapis's and its own Antiluminescent Stone, Shaddoll Core gathered the ten Cores, became El Shaddoll Grysta and, at Tierra's will, attacked the Sacred Tree.
  7. The Tellarknights, Ritual Beasts and Nekroz held a barrier around the Tree, until the Shaddolls took Ritual Beast Tamer Wen, born with great spiritual power, and made her El Shaddoll Wendigo.
  8. While the Tellarknights brought down Grysta, Wendigo broke the barrier, and El Shaddoll Winda, once the Gusto priestess Winda, touched the Tree. Her divine aura unsealed the Qliphort system sleeping beneath it.
  9. The Tree tore itself from the ground and drew the ten Cores out of Grysta's remains. They woke the Qliphorts, which hunted the Shaddolls as errors in the system. With Kerykeion holding it from within, the allies chained El Shaddoll Construct with the Apoqliphort Towers.
  10. But when Qliphort Scout absorbed Winda, Tierra took control and released the Infernoids, who flooded the world with purgatory flames, while the Qliphorts set out to purge all life on the surface.
  11. Dance Princess of the Nekroz revived Gem-Knight Master Diamond. To atone for what he had done as a Shaddoll, he fused with four Tellarknights into Stellarknight Constellar Diamond.
  12. Facing Infernoid Onuncu, Constellar Diamond was about to fall when Sombre returned with the ninth Yang Zing and fused with him and the Tellarknights into Tellarknight Ptolemaeus, who brought Onuncu down.
  13. Infernoid Devyaty stole the power of the three Ice Barrier dragons, freed El Shaddoll Shekhinaga from its icy seal and absorbed it, becoming El Shaddoll Anoyatyllis.
  14. Dance Princess of the Nekroz channelled the goddess herself as Nekroz of Sophia, and Exa channelled eight ancient Dragunities through the revived Yang Zing into a lance that could pierce gods.
  15. The Sacred Tree chose ten lives as the Zefra. With their help, Ptolemaeus and Sophia launched the lance with Satellarknight Alsahm's Sagittarius power and shattered Anoyatyllis.
  16. In its remains, Infernoid Decatron woke Tierra's true body. Tierra turned the revived Gem-Knight Lapis into Darktellarknight Batlamyus, absorbed her and the Qliphorts, and returned in full as Tierra, Source of Destruction.
  17. Ptolemaeus released her nine Yang Zing into the Tree, waking an eleventh Zefra, and Zefraath faced Tierra for a thousand days and nights. At dawn on the last day, both were gone.
  18. The dead trapped in the Infernoids' tubes came home. Gem-Knight Crystal and Lapis walked into the Tree's last light, seeking a world free of the gods.

Side story: Zefra Metaltron

The Valuable Book EX 5 (2025): the other holy war, fought while Zefraath faced Tierra.

While Zefraath and Tierra, Source of Destruction fought for the fate of the world, Infernoid Flood, the last seer of the blue flames, drove the allies into a corner, even Ritual Beast Ulti-Nochiudrago. Spiritual Beast Tamer Lara awakened the power of the Goddess of Rebirth in herself and unleashed a power to rival it.

As if in answer, the massive weapon hidden in the Sacred Tree, Zefra Metaltron, activated itself and in the blink of an eye appeared on the battlefield, covered in blue flames. Lara, having released the power that held her life together, had none left to run it, so she entrusted it to the other being who held part of the goddess's power: Nekroz of Sophia.

Nekroz of Sophia turned the Mirror into the Nekroz Divinemirror, equipped Metaltron through it and began the final fight against Flood: Nekroz of Metaltron.

Executive Summary

The Zefra archetype is a unique and complex Pendulum-based strategy, representing a narrative culmination of the Duel Terminal story arc. It unites champions from five distinct archetypes'?Nekroz, Ritual Beast, Tellarknight, Yang Zing, and Shaddoll'?into a singular, cohesive engine. Its playstyle is defined by exceptional consistency, a high combo ceiling, and the ability to build resilient end boards layered with searchable negations. The deck's power lies in its interconnected network of searchers that allow it to reliably execute its game plan and adapt to various threats.

The Zefra Collective

Core Monsters & Playmakers

Zefraath

The single most important main deck monster. Its Pendulum effect sets up the entire combo by sending a Zefra from deck to the Extra Deck and copying its scale.

Zefraniu, Secret of the Yang Zing

The primary value engine. On summon or destruction, it searches any "Zefra" or "Yang Zing" Spell/Trap, creating a loop of advantage.

Zefraxi, Treasure of the Yang Zing

A crucial combo piece that turns another Zefra monster into a Tuner, enabling powerful Synchro plays.

Divine Spells & Traps

Zefra Providence

The omni-searcher. Adds any "Zefra" card from deck to hand and offers protection from its Graveyard effect.

Oracle of Zefra

A Field Spell that searches any Zefra monster and has a powerful secondary effect to stack any monster on top of your deck.

Zefra Divine Strike / Nine Pillars

Searchable Counter Traps that provide powerful omni-negation, forming the core of the deck's end board.

The Path to Enlightenment

The deck's consistency allows even a simple two-card hand to establish a solid interruption. From just a starter and another Zefra monster, this collective of divine warriors can assemble a negate and a body on field.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Exceptional Consistency: Multiple one and two-card starters lead to powerful combos.
  • Layered Boards: Ends on a mix of monster-based interruptions and powerful backrow.
  • Resilience: Can play through some disruption and rebuild boards using the Pendulum mechanic and GY effects.
  • Counter Trap Access: Uniquely resistant to board breakers like Dark Ruler No More due to its searchable Counter Traps.

Weaknesses

  • Key Choke Points: A well-timed hand trap on Zefraath or Zefraniu can be difficult to recover from.
  • Pendulum Disruption: Vulnerable to cards that remove Pendulum Scales, like Cosmic Cyclone, or floodgates like Anti-Spell Fragrance.
  • Graveyard Hate: Relies on the GY for some recovery and combos, making it weak to cards like Dimension Shifter.

Synergies & Engines

  • Yang Zing: The most critical internal synergy, providing access to Tuners and powerful Synchro monsters.
  • Tellarknight: The Tellarknight Zefras provide excellent, searchable spot removal.
  • Endymion: A historically powerful pairing where Zefra spells generate Spell Counters to fuel the Endymion engine's powerful negates.
