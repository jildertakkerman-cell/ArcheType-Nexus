What are .yrpx Files?

.yrpx files are replay files generated by Yu-Gi-Oh! simulators. These files contain a complete record of gameplay, including every card played, every phase, and the exact sequence of moves.

Replay files are commonly used to:

  • Record and share combo demonstrations
  • Document deck combos for tutorials and deck profiles
  • Practice and refine your combo lines in solo mode
  • Create step-by-step combo guides for the community

Compatible Simulators

The .yrpx format is used exclusively by EDOPro (Commonly found at: C:\ProjectIgnis\replay)

EDOPro (.yrpx)

Note: Other simulators use different replay formats:

YGOPro (.yrp) YGO Omega (different format)

This converter currently only supports EDOPro's .yrpx format.

How This Converter Works

This tool is designed for combo demonstration replays. It extracts the card sequence from your replay and generates a clean PDF document showing your combo step-by-step.

Best Practice: For optimal results, record your combo in solo/puzzle mode or against a passive AI. Full competitive duels may not convert cleanly into a structured combo PDF.

The PDF includes:

  • The sequence of cards played during your combo
  • A structured breakdown of the combo flow
  • Easy-to-read formatting for study or sharing

Note: Your replay file is processed securely. The data is sent to our conversion service and is not stored permanently.

Having issues? Make sure your file has the .yrpx extension and was exported from a compatible simulator.
