What are .yrpx Files?

.yrpx files are replay files generated by the EDOPro simulator. They contain a complete record of gameplay, tracking every move, phase, and card interaction.

Unlike standard .yrp files, the .yrpx format is specific to EDOPro and often contains richer data for modern interactions. This analyzer reads that data to provide:

  • Resource advantage graphs
  • Chain depth and negation statistics
  • Opening hand "Brick" analysis
  • Tempo and action speed metrics
What gets shared?

Only the card sequences from your replay are used to enhance our Combo Visualizer. Your data is fully anonymized. Unchecking this box ensures your replay is processed but not saved.

Opening Hand:
How to read: The verdict shows if the hand was statistically favorable (God Hand) or unfavorable (Brick). A "Brick" with a loss suggests bad luck. A "God Hand" with a loss suggests misplay.
Draw Chance: Measures your hand trap "luck". 80% means 80% of possible hands would have fewer hand traps than yours. A high percentage indicates a very defensive draw.
Turn Factor: Scoring is adjusted by turn order. 1st player wants engine access (0-1 hand traps is ideal). 2nd player needs interruption (2+ hand traps is ideal).
Garnets: 1-off archetype cards you want to stay in the deck. Drawing them incurs a -15 point penalty. Powerful 1-offs restricted by the Banlist are NOT penalized.
