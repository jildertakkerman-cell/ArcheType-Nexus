Lore Archive

"The Fur Hire mercenaries are a ragtag company of beast-warriors who Special Summon each other from the hand in an ever-expanding chain of recruitment. Each new hire triggers a unique effect—searching, destroying, drawing—until the team's leader, Rafale, stands atop a fully staffed board. They fight not for glory, but for the paycheck."

Executive Summary: The Burst Protocol

The "Fur Hire" archetype (Skyfang Brigade) has evolved. With the release of the Burst Protocol (BPRO) support, the strategy has transformed from a fragile "glass cannon" into a resilient mid-range control contender. New recruits like Rex, Ride Fur Hire and Keel, Shipwright Fur Hire address historical weaknesses by decoupling the strategy from its reliance on the Normal Summon and stabilizing the resource economy. This versatile group of mercenaries is always available "For Hire", now equipped to out-grind even the toughest meta contenders.

Burst Protocol: Impact Assessment

Rex, Ride Fur Hire

Fixing the "Glass Cannon"

Historically, "Fur Hire" lost if the Normal Summon was negated. The new support fundamentally solves this:

  • Decoupling from Normal Summon: Whirlwind Fur Hire and Rex, Ride Fur Hire allow you to establish board presence before you even Normal Summon, bypassing traditional chokepoints like "Infinite Impermanence".
  • Economy of Negation: Previously, using Rafale's negate emptied your hand. Now, you can banish Whirlwind from the GY to pay the cost, keeping your hand full for follow-up plays.

The Chain Block

The new Rex, Ride Fur Hire summons itself after another monster. This allows you to order your chain links so that your critical search effects (like Beat) are Chain Link 1, and Rex is Chain Link 2, protecting the search from Ash Blossom.

The "Pure" Imperative

Keel and Whirlwind lock you into "Fur Hire" summons. This "xenophobic" restriction means abandoning hybrid engines like Spright or Runick in favor of a dedicated "Pure" build that maximizes internal synergy and consistency.

Infinite Resource Loop

The trap Furtive Techniques recycles itself every turn you summon a high-level Fur Hire. Combined with Keel recycling monsters, the deck now possesses an "inevitability factor" in the grind game.

Competitive Analysis (Post-Burst Protocol)

Strengths

  • Resilience: Can now play through 1-2 hand traps comfortably due to multiple starter avenues.
  • Grind Game: Infinite recursion via Furtive Techniques and Fandora ensures you never run out of steam.
  • Hand Trap Density: The compact engine allows for 12-15 non-engine slots (Hand Traps) to disrupt meta decks.

Weaknesses

  • The "Lock": Inability to access generic bosses like Accesscode Talker makes dealing with "Towers" (unaffected monsters) difficult.
  • Maxx "C": The deck special summons extensively, giving opponents significant draws under Maxx "C".

Deck Construction

The "Pure" build is now superior to Hybrid variants.

  • Runick/Spright: Now largely incompatible due to Keel and Whirlwind's restrictions.
  • Fossil Dig: Essential for consistency, effectively giving you 6 copies of the new Rex.
