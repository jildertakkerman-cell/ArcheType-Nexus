UNCHAINED

The Symphony of Self-Destruction

Lore Archive

"The Unchained exist in a paradox of destruction: they feed upon their own dismantling, growing stronger with every chain shattered, every bond broken. Imprisoned abyssal spirits given physical form, they do not resist their opponents' attacks — they welcome them, turning every act of aggression into an invitation for escalation. To break an Unchained monster is to release something far more dangerous than what you sought to destroy."

The Unchained take their names from Yama, the Hindu god of death, and his watchdogs, and their artworks follow two twins from a prison to their escape. Follow a marked name to walk the trail.

Executive Summary

The Unchained archetype thrives on a paradoxical strategy: the deliberate destruction of its own cards to generate overwhelming advantage. This theme of weaponized self-destruction is woven into every monster, spell, and trap, creating a resilient, recursive engine. The deck's core gameplay loop involves "floating" effects, where destroyed "Unchained" cards summon replacements from the hand, Deck, or Graveyard. This turns what would be setbacks for any other strategy into the primary catalyst for their most powerful combos, allowing them to maintain a relentless board presence and out-resource opponents.

The Instruments of Ruin

Core Monsters & Starters

The premier one-card starter. Summons itself by destroying a card you control and sets an "Unchained" Spell/Trap when sent to the GY.

Unchained Twins - Aruha & Rakea

Key starters that destroy a card you control to Special Summon themselves, triggering your other "Unchained" card effects.

Unchained Twins - Sarama

The main recursion tool, allowing you to reset an "Unchained" card from your GY to destroy another card and continue combos.

Spells & Extra Deck Threats

Abomination's Prison

The archetype's search spell, adding any "Unchained" card to your hand for immense consistency.

Abominable Chamber of the Unchained

The primary trap card, used to revive "Unchained" monsters for extension or disruption.

Unchained Soul Lord of Yama

The heart of the Extra Deck. Searches any "Unchained" monster and provides recursion from the GY.

Blueprint for Destruction

The deck's power comes from its flexible combo routes. The goal is not just to build a board, but to establish a resource loop. This allows the player to continuously convert their own cards into threats, leaving the opponent struggling to deal with the never-ending legion of fiends.

The Unchained End Board

Standard Control Board

Unchained Soul of Rage
  • Link Away: Use Rage's quick effect to use an opponent's monster as Link material for a DARK monster.
  • Recursion: A set "Unchained" trap like Abominable Chamber provides revival.
  • GY Follow-up: Unchained Soul Lord of Yama in the GY offers extension if your board is broken.

The Abomination Board

Unchained Abomination
  • Triple Destruction: Destroys cards when another card is destroyed by effect, when a monster is destroyed by battle, and during the End Phase.
  • Relentless Pressure: Can systematically dismantle an entire board over a single turn cycle.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Resilience: Plays through disruption by turning destruction into advantage.
  • Powerful Removal: "Contact linking" with opponent's monsters bypasses most forms of protection.
  • High Consistency: Multiple one-card starters and powerful searchers like Yama and Prison.

Weaknesses

  • Graveyard Hate: Cards like Abyss Dweller or Dimension Shifter can shut down recursion.
  • Non-Destruction Removal: Vulnerable to Kaijus, Evenly Matched, and other effects that don't destroy.
  • Floodgates: Cards like There Can Be Only One are devastating due to the all-Fiend monster lineup.

Synergies & Engines

  • D/D Engine: Provides extra Fiend extenders and access to powerful Rank 6 and 8 Xyz monsters.
  • Yubel Engine: Natural synergy, as both archetypes benefit from destroying their own cards.
  • Generic Fiend Support: Tour Guide and Fiendish Rhino Warrior provide easy access to Unchained Soul Lord of Yama.

Unchained Oni of Shyma

One-card starter

Unchained Oni of Shyma

Effect Monster · WATER · Fiend · Level 4 · ATK 1500 / DEF 2000

Card text

If this card is Normal Summoned: You can Special Summon 1 Level 4 or lower "Unchained" monster from your Deck, except "Unchained Oni of Shyma", then destroy 1 card you control, also you cannot Special Summon for the rest of this turn, except Fiend monsters. If this card on the field is destroyed by card effect, except "Unchained Oni of Shyma", or by battle: You can Special Summon 1 "Unchained" monster from your hand or Deck, except "Unchained Oni of Shyma". You can only use each effect of "Unchained Oni of Shyma" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

The new one-card starter. Normal Summon it and it Special Summons a Level 4 or lower "Unchained" monster from your Deck: one of the Twins or . Then it destroys 1 card you control.

Destroy the monster it just summoned. Aruha, Rakea and Sarama float when another card's effect destroys them, so one Normal Summon becomes two summons from the Deck before you have used anything else.

If another card's effect destroys Oni of Shyma later, it floats too, into any "Unchained" monster from your hand or Deck. That includes the Level 6 , which is how Line 1 reaches .

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • The first effect is a Normal Summon trigger. A Special Summon doesn't start it.
  • The summon and the destroy are not simultaneous, and the Fiend-only lock applies once the effect resolves, even if the summon or the destroy failed, unless the effect was negated (Konami card notes).
  • If it destroys itself with its own first effect, its second effect doesn't trigger: that one excludes "Unchained Oni of Shyma".
  • The second effect works in the Damage Step, and also when it is destroyed and banished face-up instead of going to the GY.
Unchained Oni of Shara

Hand extender, GY recursion

Unchained Oni of Shara

Effect Monster · FIRE · Fiend · Level 4 · ATK 1500 / DEF 2000

Card text

During the Main Phase (Quick Effect): You can discard this card; Special Summon 1 Fiend monster from your hand, then destroy 1 card you control. If a card(s) on the field is destroyed by card effect (except the effect of "Unchained Oni of Shara") or by battle, while this card is in your GY: You can add this card to your hand or Special Summon it, but place it on the bottom of the Deck when it leaves the field. You can only use each effect of "Unchained Oni of Shara" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

A hand extender. In either player's Main Phase, discard it to Special Summon any Fiend from your hand, then destroy 1 card you control. That destroy can start a float, so Shara plus , or any other Fiend in hand is a two-card start.

In the GY it waits. Whenever a card on either field is destroyed by battle or by a card effect other than its own, you can add it to your hand or Special Summon it. A copy summoned this way goes to the bottom of the Deck when it leaves the field, which is why Line 1 can summon it from the Deck a second time.

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • The first effect is a Quick Effect from the hand that also works in your opponent's Main Phase (Konami card notes).
  • The summon comes first, then the destroy. The destroy isn't optional once the summon succeeds.
  • The second effect works in the Damage Step, and Shara has to be in the GY already when the card is destroyed.
  • The bottom-of-the-Deck clause ends once Shara is no longer face-up in the Monster Zone.
Unchained Zealot - Kamra

Multi-card destroyer, board wipe

Unchained Zealot - Kamra

Effect Monster · DARK · Fiend · Level 8 · ATK 2000 / DEF 2500

Card text

If this card is in your hand: You can target up to 3 cards you control; destroy them, Special Summon this card, then you can apply any of these effects in sequence (once each), based on the original types of those destroyed cards.
● Monster: Draw 1 card.
● Spell: Change 1 monster's battle position.
● Trap: Destroy 1 other card on the field.
If this card on the field is destroyed by card effect: You can destroy all monsters on the field. You can only use each effect of "Unchained Zealot - Kamra" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

Destroys up to 3 of your cards to Special Summon itself from the hand. Every "Unchained" floater among them triggers once Kamra's effect has finished.

Then it pays out by the original card types it destroyed. A Monster draws 1 card, a Spell changes 1 monster's battle position, and a Trap destroys 1 other card on the field. That last one can hit your opponent's card, or another of your floaters for one more summon.

If a card effect destroys Kamra on the field, you can destroy every monster on the field. Going second, your own or can be that card effect (Line 2).

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • The first effect is an Ignition Effect in the hand, so it only works in your own Main Phase.
  • The destroy and the summon count as simultaneous, and Kamra is only summoned if at least 1 card was destroyed. The bonuses come after that, one at a time, in printed order (Konami card notes).
  • Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring can be chained to the first effect whatever you target (Konami Q&A 23050).
  • The wipe is optional ("You can"), and battle doesn't start it: Kamra has to be destroyed by a card effect.
Unchained Firelord of Yama

End-board boss

Unchained Firelord of Yama

Link Monster · DARK · Fiend · LINK-4 · ATK 3000 · Top, Bottom-Left, Bottom, Bottom-Right

Card text

2+ monsters, including an "Unchained Soul" monster
During your End Phase: You can target up to 2 Fiend monsters in your GY that were destroyed this turn; Special Summon them, but for each one, as long as it remains face-up in your Monster Zone, you cannot Special Summon, except Fiend monsters. If this card on the field would be destroyed by battle or card effect, you can destroy 1 other face-up card on either field instead. You can only use each effect of "Unchained Firelord of Yama" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

The new boss. In your End Phase it Special Summons up to 2 Fiends from your GY that were destroyed this turn. After a long Unchained turn that is two more bodies for free, and the best picks keep a Quick Effect: or .

Once per turn, if it would be destroyed by battle or a card effect, you can destroy 1 other face-up card on either field instead. That can be one of your opponent's cards, or one of your face-up floaters so it summons something.

Its materials need an "Unchained Soul" monster: Soul Lord of Yama, Soul of Sharvara, Shyama, Disaster, Rage, Anguish, Abominable Rage or Abominable Unchained Soul. The Twins, both Onis, Kamra and Unchained Abomination don't count.

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • Only your own End Phase. A target must have been destroyed and sent to your GY this turn and stayed there; a monster you revived and then used as Link Material doesn't count (Konami Q&A 24344).
  • While a monster it revived stays face-up, you can only Special Summon Fiends from the Extra Deck (Konami card notes).
  • The replacement is once per turn. If Firelord is destroyed together with other cards, none of those other cards can be the replacement.
  • Replacing makes "destroy it, and if you do, Special Summon" effects fail, your own Sharvara, Aruha and Soul of Shyama included, if they aim at Firelord (Konami Q&A 24345). If Sarama, Aruha, Rakea or either Oni destroys Firelord with its effect and Firelord destroys that monster, or a copy of it on the field, instead, the destroyed monster still floats (Konami Q&A 24334).
Unchained Soul of Abominable Rage

Negate on the field, destroy from the GY

Unchained Soul of Abominable Rage

Link Monster · DARK · Fiend · LINK-3 · ATK 2100 · Left, Right, Bottom

Card text

2+ monsters, including a Fiend monster
If your opponent Special Summons a monster(s) face-up (except during the Damage Step): You can target 1 of them; destroy 1 Fiend monster you control, and if you do, negate the targeted monster's effects until the end of this turn. When a monster effect is activated on the field and you control a Link-4 or higher "Unchained" Link Monster (Quick Effect): You can banish this card from your GY; destroy the monster that activated its effect. You can only use each effect of "Unchained Soul of Abominable Rage" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

On the field: when your opponent Special Summons a face-up monster, target it, destroy 1 Fiend you control and negate the target's effects until the end of the turn. The Fiend you destroy can be a floater.

In the GY: while you control a Link-4 or higher "Unchained" Link Monster ( or ), banish it when a monster effect is activated on the field to destroy that monster.

It is an "Unchained Soul" monster, so it is legal Firelord material. Link Summon it, use it for Firelord, and its GY effect is live for your opponent's turn. Line 1 does exactly this.

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • The first effect is a trigger, not usable in the Damage Step. You need a face-up Fiend to destroy; the destroy and the negation count as simultaneous, and nothing is negated if the destroy fails (Konami card notes).
  • The second effect chains directly to the monster effect's activation, on either player's side, and not in the Damage Step. It destroys the monster; it doesn't negate the effect.
Reincarnation of the Unchained

Searcher, recycler, self-destruct

Reincarnation of the Unchained

Continuous Spell

Card text

When this card is activated: You can add 1 "Abomination's Prison", or 1 "Unchained" Spell, except "Reincarnation of the Unchained", from your Deck to your hand. Once per turn: You can target up to 3 "Unchained" cards in your GY, except "Reincarnation of the Unchained"; shuffle them into the Deck, then you can destroy other cards you control up to the number shuffled. You can only activate 1 "Reincarnation of the Unchained" per turn.

What it does for the deck

When you activate it, add 1 "Unchained" Spell or from your Deck. Prison adds any "Unchained" card, so Reincarnation is a one-card start: Reincarnation, Prison, , then Line 1.

Once per turn it shuffles up to 3 "Unchained" cards from your GY into the Deck, Link Monsters back to the Extra Deck, then can destroy that many of your other cards. One activation can pop two Set floaters.

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • You can only activate 1 "Reincarnation of the Unchained" per turn. The search is part of the card's activation and you decide it on resolution (Konami card notes).
  • The second effect is a Main Phase effect that starts a chain. The destroy is optional, comes after the shuffle, and can't include Reincarnation itself.
Unchained Hunger

Opponent-turn removal by Link Summon

Unchained Hunger

Normal Trap

Card text

Target 2 face-up monsters anywhere on the field, including an "Unchained Soul" Link Monster you control; immediately after this effect resolves, Link Summon 1 Fiend Link Monster using only those 2 monsters. If this Set card is destroyed by card effect: You can Special Summon 1 "Unchained" monster from your Deck. You can only use each effect of "Unchained Hunger" once per turn.

What it does for the deck

Target 2 face-up monsters, one of them an "Unchained Soul" Link Monster you control (Soul Lord of Yama, Soul of Rage, Soul of Anguish or Abominable Rage). The other can be your opponent's. Then Link Summon a Fiend Link Monster using only those 2. Their monster leaves as Link Material, so destruction protection doesn't save it.

A Link Monster counts as its Link Rating. Soul of Rage plus one of their monsters makes a Link-3 ( or ). Abominable Rage plus one of their monsters makes a Link-4 ( or ).

If it is destroyed while Set, it Special Summons an "Unchained" monster from your Deck, like the older Unchained Traps.

Rulings that matter

Based on the card text and Konami's notes and Q&A on this card, the official rulings in Konami's Japanese card database. The TCG follows them unless it rules otherwise.

  • Both targets must be legal materials for a Fiend Link Monster in your Extra Deck. A monster unaffected by Traps can't be used, and if either target has left the field or turned face-down, nothing happens (Konami card notes).
  • It is a Trap your opponent can respond to, not a Quick Effect that "can't be responded to". Not in the Damage Step.
  • Like any Trap it can't be activated the turn it is Set. Sharvara Sets it from the Deck, Sarama Sets it from the GY, and Abomination's Prison can add it.

Which cards are "Unchained Soul" monsters?

Firelord of Yama's materials and Unchained Hunger's target both ask for an "Unchained Soul" monster. Those are , , , , , , and . Firelord of Yama itself, Unchained Abomination, the Twins, both Onis and Kamra are "Unchained" cards but not "Unchained Soul" ones.

For Hunger the Soul has to be a Link Monster you control: Soul Lord of Yama, Soul of Rage, Soul of Anguish or Soul of Abominable Rage.

Mind the Fiend locks. Oni of Shyma locks you for the turn, Aruha and Rakea do too when they use their own effect, and Sharvara does while it stays face-up. Non-Fiend Extra Deck monsters such as S:P Little Knight only fit before the first of those resolves. In the TCG, Called by the Grave, Crossout Designator, Maxx "C", D/D/D Wave High King Caesar and Kashtira Arise-Heart are Forbidden, so lists copied from the OCG need changing.

What Still Beats It

  • Banishing. Macro Cosmos doesn't stop the floats themselves: a floater that is destroyed and banished face-up still triggers. It stops everything that works from the GY: Sharvara's Set, Oni of Shara, Soul Lord of Yama, Soul of Shyama, Abominable Rage and Firelord's revive.
  • Face-down banishing. Evenly Matched removes the field without destroying anything, so nothing floats.
  • Tributes. Nibiru, the Primal Being Tributes your monsters, so none of them float. Sharvara still Sets a card because it was sent to the GY, and destroying the Primal Being Token with your own effect later counts as a card you control being destroyed.
  • Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring. It answers Oni of Shyma's summon trigger, Soul Lord of Yama's search and Kamra. Lead with whichever you can afford to lose.
  • Droll & Lock Bird. Soul Lord of Yama, Abomination's Prison and Reincarnation all add from the Deck, so after Droll only the first add happens. The summons from the Deck still work.
  • GY banishers. Bystial Druiswurm and Magnamhut banish the DARK Fiends that work from the GY: Soul Lord of Yama and Abominable Rage.
  • What it handles well. Infinite Impermanence or Effect Veiler on one of your monsters: Sharvara or Rakea destroys that monster in response, the negation has no target, and the monster floats.
