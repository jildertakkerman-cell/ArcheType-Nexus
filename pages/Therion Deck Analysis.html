Lore Archive

"The Therion are ancient mechanical warriors who equip monsters from the GY as components, gaining their effects. King Regulus negates a card once per turn by returning an equipped monster to the GY, creating a recyclable negation engine. They scavenge the graveyard for power and find it in abundance."

Executive Summary

The "Therion" archetype operates on a unique design philosophy centered around the Graveyard. Therion monsters function as high-level "pilots" that Special Summon themselves from the hand by equipping a monster from the Graveyard, effectively donning them as powerful "power armor."

Update: The introduction of the "Gung-Ho! Springans!" support wave has fundamentally altered the deck's identity. By utilizing Gigantic "Champion" Sargas and Tally-ho! Springans, the deck has transitioned from a linear control strategy into a high-velocity "Rank 4/Rank 8 Combo-Toolbox" that accesses Therion "King" Regulus with deterministic consistency.

Gung-Ho! The Springans Bridge

The alliance between the Springans and Therions (seen in the "Albaz" lore) provides the deck with its most powerful engine. This "Gung-Ho!" support wave solves the deck's historical reliance on hard-drawing its boss monsters.

Competitive Analysis

Strengths

  • Deterministic Search: Sargas guarantees Regulus access.
  • Dual-Layered End Board: Mixes Omni-negates with Monster-negates and Destruction.
  • Going Second: "Tally-ho!" can break boards before committing summons.

Weaknesses

  • Droll & Lock Bird: Hard-stops the multi-search Springans engine.
  • Graveyard Reliant: Still vulnerable to Dimension Shifter and Macro Cosmos.
  • Nibiru: The swarm capability often triggers Nibiru before Regulus is live.

Top Engine Choice

Springans Engine (Tier 1 Synergy)

The superior choice over P.U.N.K. or Adventure. Discards bricks for cost while searching Regulus directly.

  • Plant Link: Synergizes with "Lily" Borea.
  • Gear Gigant X: Searcher for any Level 4 Machine.
