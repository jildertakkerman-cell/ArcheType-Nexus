Lore Archive

"The Topologic monsters are digital void entities that trigger catastrophic effects when monsters are summoned to zones they point to. Topologic Bomber Dragon nukes the Main Monster Zones, Trisbaena banishes all Spells and Traps, and Zeroboros banishes the entire field. They are black holes made of code."

The Topological Paradigm

The Topologic Series constitutes a suite of DARK Cyberse Link Monsters that redefine spatial dynamics. These are not merely monsters; they are environmental hazards that manipulate the field's geometry to punish opponent summons or facilitate mass field resets.

"Topology: the mathematical study of geometric properties and spatial relations. In-game, this translates to effects that detonated when monsters enter specific zones identified by Link Arrows."

03. COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

Strategic Strengths

  • Mandatory Trigger Priority: SEGOC rules mean Topologic effects are difficult to "Chain Block" with optional effects.
  • Cyberse Consistency: Access to **Mathmech Circular**, **Cynet Mining**, and **Lady Debug** makes them easily accessible.
  • Zero-Reset Win-Con: Zeroboros scaling past 8000 ATK provides an OTK option that ignores established boards.

Vulnerabilities

  • Double-Edged Sword: Savvy opponents can intentionally summon to a linked zone to trigger a wipe at the *wrong* time for the controller.
  • Positional Dependence: 100% of their effectiveness relies on board geometry; a "Book of Moon" or "Kaiju" immediately shuts down the threat.
  • Banned Elements: Loss of Gumblar Dragon removed the archetype's primary win-condition for hand-control builds.
